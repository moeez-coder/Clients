C&G Consulting Services — cgcsinc.net

A New Jersey staffing firm that finds and places scientific and technical people for companies that need to hire — on contract or permanently.

WHO THEY SELL TO
The people who sign off on hiring: VPs and Directors of HR, Talent Acquisition, Clinical Operations, Regulatory Affairs, IT and Engineering, plus C-suite at smaller firms. Biotech, pharma, clinical research and tech companies, **roughly 50 to 5,000 staff**, US-wide but strongest from Washington DC up to Boston.

WHAT THEY SELL
- Contract and permanent placement of life sciences people: regulatory affairs, pharmacovigilance, biostatistics, clinical data, medical affairs, QC, lab techs, post-docs
- Contract and permanent placement of tech people: software engineers, developers, data scientists, IT security, project managers
- **Market intel alongside the resumes** — what a role actually pays, current contractor rates, and whether the job spec is realistic

WHY A PROSPECT TAKES THE CALL
They have open niche roles sitting unfilled, or an agency that sends piles of irrelevant CVs from an offshore team nobody can get hold of. C&G's recruiters are all US-based, they have a 30-year candidate database, and because the firm is privately owned with no investors demanding a margin floor, they can price each role on its merits. Strongest line: they **grew one biotech client from 10 people to 500** after it entered the US market, and most clients stay over 15 years.
