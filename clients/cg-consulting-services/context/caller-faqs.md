Q: What is this about?
A: I'm calling on behalf of Jon at C&G Consulting Services — they're a New Jersey staffing firm that fills life sciences and technology roles, things like regulatory affairs, biostats, data science, IT security. I'm just seeing whether a short intro with Jon is worth your time.

Q: Who are you, are you C&G?
A: I work with Jon's team on the outreach side — I book the conversations, he runs them. He's the one who's been doing this 30 years, so the detail is his department.

Q: How did you get my details?
A: You're listed publicly as one of the people who owns hiring there, so you came up on our list. Nothing more sinister than that — and if you'd rather we didn't contact you again, I'll take you off right now.

Q: We already have recruiters / we're on a PSL.
A: Most people Jon speaks to do. He's usually the one they call when an agency can't crack a niche role, or when they want a second opinion on what it should actually pay. **Worth being in the rolodex before you need it.**

Q: What does it cost?
A: That depends on the role, whether it's contract or permanent, and how many you're hiring. Jon prices each one on its merits — C&G is privately owned, no investors forcing a minimum margin — but he'd need the specifics before quoting anything.

Q: How is this different from every other agency ringing me?
A: Two things. Every recruiter is **US-based, not offshore**, so the person your candidates speak to actually knows the market. And they don't just dump resumes — they'll give you salary and contractor rate data so you know if your spec is realistic.

Q: Who else do you work with?
A: They're the **#1 vendor to the largest pharmaceutical company in the world**, and they took a biotech from 10 people to 500 after it entered the US market. Jon can walk you through named references on the call — he offers them freely.

Q: We're not hiring right now.
A: Fair enough, and that's usually the best time to have the conversation. It's 30 minutes to get your requirements on file so when something does open up Jon isn't starting cold.

Q: How fast would we see candidates?
A: Jon will give you a realistic timeline for your specific role on the call. The 30-year database means a lot of these niche life sciences people are already known to them rather than sourced from scratch.

Q: I'm not the one who makes this decision.
A: No problem — who owns hiring or vendor relationships on your side? Happy to speak to them, and I'll leave you out of it.

Q: Just email me something.
A: I can do that, though honestly the useful part is Jon asking about the roles you're struggling with — a PDF won't do that. Shall I put **30 minutes** in and you can cancel if it's not relevant?
