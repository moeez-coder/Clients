## What they do
C&G Consulting Services, Inc. is a full-service staffing and consulting agency that, since 2007, has offered contract staffing, direct hiring, temp-to-perm recruitment, and project team services. The firm describes itself as being led by leaders with over 25 years' worth of expertise in sales and recruitment. Its core method is matching clients with candidates: matching their clients' needs with candidates with specific skills has been the cornerstone of the agency's success, which the staffing team achieves by thoroughly evaluating skills, building strong relationships and developing a deep understanding of the people they work with to facilitate the best matches for both sides of the equation. Today the service focus is sharpened: they focus on staff augmentation and managed services, and can also help with Account Management Leadership, IT Staffing, Payroll Solutions and Professional Staffing. To source talent, they utilise prominent job boards such as Monster, Career Builder, Indeed, Dice and LinkedIn, and they operate a candidate/client referral program, giving rewards to every referrer whose referred candidate is hired, and paying generously for referrals of potential client opportunities as well. What they position as distinctive is relationship depth and adaptability — being constant through business, economic and workforce changes: adapting, growing and delivering professional talent — backed by their own network of seasoned consulting experts.

## Who they target
C&G targets employers across all sectors rather than a single vertical: they have vast experience working across all industries, and the agency describes empowering various clients and organisations across the nation. Within that, the service mix points to specific demand areas, namely IT Staffing, Payroll Solutions, Professional Staffing and Account Management Leadership, alongside staff augmentation and managed services. The buyers are company decision-makers responsible for recruitment — the messaging is aimed at those who want more than someone capable of finding candidates who can help a company grow; someone they can count on to take their hiring process and needs as seriously as they do. The firm is based in Parsippany, New Jersey, operating from 1055 Parsippany Blvd. Suite 304, Parsippany, NJ 07054. A second audience is job-seeking professionals: they are continually looking for talented professionals with various skill sets, directing candidates to apply via the job boards where their active roles are posted.
