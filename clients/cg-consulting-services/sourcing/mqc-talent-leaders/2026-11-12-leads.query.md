# MQC: talent leaders at hiring companies no CG campaign has touched (2026-11-12)

**Purpose:** the audience for C&G's **MQC campaign**, whose only goal is to get a reply (any reply) and to learn which messaging draws one. Moe, 2026-11-12: "for this one no need to go through the document, I give you full control, get me some result." **Scope: Campaign-specific: `CG | MQC | TA Leaders | v1`.** This is a **new segment** (a new persona, TA/HR leaders, at new companies), not an expansion of an existing one.

**Produced by:** `sourcing/pull_mqc.py` (filters in `sourcing/mqc_spec.py`) with the Blitz API. Every request body is in `2026-11-12-requests.jsonl`. The first line there is a request Blitz rejected with 422 (57 job titles, the limit is 50; 0 records). The spec now collapses redundant titles to exactly 50.

## Why this audience
- The live CG campaigns show where replies are not coming from. Security and Data Bench (`677893`/`686451`, CTO/CISO/VP Eng, blank request, pitch-led message 1): **2,418 requests, 91 accepted (3.8%), 52 conversations, 5 replies**, all "not hiring", "not a fit" or job-seekers (HeyReach `get_overall_stats` and `get_conversations_v2`, 2026-11-12). The open-role MPC campaigns started today (29 requests sent, no data yet).
- **Persona:** talent-acquisition leaders first, HR leaders as the fallback. They are the people who actually engage agencies, they live on LinkedIn, and no live CG campaign has them as its primary persona.
- **Companies:** 51–1,000 employees (smaller companies reply more and often have no in-house sourcing bench), C&G's verticals (life sciences + the tech/finance/healthcare/education/retail labels of `open_role_spec.py`, without the catch-all consulting labels), US-HQ, `is_agency: false`, and **a posting in a C&G role family in the last 30 days**. That posting is `{Open_Role}` in message variant V3.
- **Company-level exclusion:** any company already in a CG campaign or open-role snapshot (5,280 companies: Bench wave 1 + wave 2 rows 1–2100, and every open-role companies/leads file). A reply here then can't be credited to another campaign's touch, and no company gets a second CG campaign.
- **1 contact per company (TAM/SAM read):** the untouched pool is large relative to sender capacity (~175 requests/day shared by 7 campaigns), and the test needs many independent companies, not depth. 1 per company maximises the number of independent accounts the variants are measured on.

## What ran
1. `POST /v2/company/tam-by-jobs`, **Northeast pass** (HQ state in DC→Boston): paged 781 companies, 91 not already touched. That pool is mostly used by earlier campaigns.
2. **Rest-of-US pass** (same filters, HQ state excludes the Northeast): paged 2,387 companies (the pool's end), 722 new.
3. `POST /v2/jobs/company` (max 1, same title filter, 30 days) per new company: 813 jobs.
4. `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword`, 1 per company: tier 1 TA leader titles, tier 2 HR leader titles (`mqc_spec.TA_TITLES` / `HR_TITLES`), with IC titles excluded (`PEOPLE_EXCLUDE`). 503 companies returned someone.

**Blitz records used: 4,484** (tam-by-jobs 3,168, jobs/company 813, waterfall 503). The CG running total is **~24.0k** (19.5k before), under Moe's 40k stop line.

## Result
- `2026-11-12-companies.csv`: 813 companies (`leads_in_snapshot` 0 for the 310 with no TA/HR leader found, kept so the drop-off is visible).
- `2026-11-12-jobs.csv`: 813 postings, one per company.
- `2026-11-12-leads.csv`: **503 leads, 499 `live_eligible`** (197 tier-1 TA, 302 tier-2 HR). 59 at Northeast-HQ companies. Excluded: 3 with no usable first name and 1 title caught by the exclude list ("Account Manager - Donor Recruitment").
- **Dedupe:** 0 overlap with every other CG leads file on LinkedIn URL and first+last+domain (`tools/leads.py overlap`). HeyReach also excludes leads in other campaigns.
- Copy-ready columns: `first_name_clean` (`{Fname}`), `company_name_clean` (`{Company}`, legal suffixes dropped) and `open_role_clean` (`{Open_Role}`; location, req-id and bracket tails dropped, `-` for 12 titles that don't read as a role name, which then get the step's fallback text).
- No email enrichment. No DNC applied: the client's `Non_Connect` file is still not loaded anywhere (Moe's go-live-without-it decision of 2026-11-11 applies here too).
