## TAM/SAM read and contacts per company

**Small TAM/SAM.** Only 126 US non-agency LS companies (51–5,000 employees) have a matching Clinical/Data posting in the last 30 days. Per CLAUDE.md, a small TAM means more people per company, so the cap is **3 contacts per company** (Moe-approved 2026-11-05). The waterfall fills tier 1 first and uses the talent fallback only when fewer than 3 hiring managers exist.

## Method

1. **Jobs:** `POST /v2/jobs/search`, cursor-paged at 50/page, segment cap 5000 jobs. Title matching is Blitz keyword matching: all words of a phrase must appear, in any order.
2. **Companies:** `POST /v2/company/tam-by-jobs` with the same filters. It gives the distinct hiring companies with HQ state. Each company's **most recent** matching posting becomes its `open_role` (`{Open_Role}`) and `jd_summary` (`{JD}`, Blitz `ai_summary`).
3. **People:** `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword`, one call per hiring company. Tier 1 is the hiring manager for the role family and tier 2 is the talent fallback (Moe's rule). Both tiers are US-located. `max_results` = contacts per company. LinkedIn URLs only, **no email enrichment**.
4. **Dedupe** (flagged, nothing dropped), by normalised LinkedIn URL:
   - `in_heyreach_list_1056840`: the wave-1 file plus wave-2 rows 1–2100. That is exactly list `1056840` (2,593 leads, verified 2026-11-05) used by HeyReach `677893`/`686451`.
   - `dupe_of`: every other CG `*-leads.csv`, including the Security and Data Bench snapshots and the open-role segments built earlier, in this order: clinical-data → regulatory-quality → medical-affairs → tech. A person stays `live_eligible` only in the first segment they appear in.
   - Nothing was checked against the client's `Non_Connect` DNC file, because it is not loaded anywhere yet.
5. **Clean-up:** credential suffixes after a comma are stripped from `last_name` ("Quinn, CCDM" → "Quinn"). Empty values are written as `-`. Leads are sorted Northeast first, then by most recent posting.
6. **Known noise:** keyword titles occasionally catch an adjacent function, e.g. "Director, Product Management - Data Products" matched "Director Data Management". Review in GTM Sheets before any push.
