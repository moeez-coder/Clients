## Method (same as 2026-11-12)

1. **Companies:** `POST /v2/company/tam-by-jobs`, segment filters, **60-day posting window** (2026-09-26 to 2026-11-25). It covers every posting since the 11-12 pull, plus any posting Blitz indexed late. Every company already in an earlier `*-companies.csv` of this segment is skipped. The whole TAM is paged. The TAM is small, so the cost of re-paging known companies is only 211 records.
2. **Open role:** `POST /v2/jobs/company` (`max_results: 1`, same title filters, 60-day window) per new company.
3. **People:** `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword` per new company, same cascade, **3 per company**. TAM/SAM read unchanged: a small pool (only 29 new hiring companies in 13 days), so 3 contacts per company under the inverse rule.
4. **Dedupe** (flagged, never dropped):
   - Every CG `*-leads.csv`: all rows of `security-and-data-bench` and `mqc-talent-leaders`, and the live rows of every earlier open-role snapshot. Same-day order: Clinical & Data is built before Medical Affairs.
   - **New this time:** a live export of the HeyReach lists behind all 7 CG campaigns, pulled 2026-11-25 with `get_leads_from_list` (limit 1000, paged): `1056840` (677893/686451) 2,593; `1141179` (723837) 425; `1141180` (723838) 1,435; `1141181` (723839) 512; `1141182` (723841) 1,611; `1149590` (727548) 499. That is 7,075 unique URLs. A match appears in `dupe_of` as `heyreach <list> (<campaign>)`. Every HeyReach match was also caught by a repo file.
