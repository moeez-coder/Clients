## Method (same as 2026-11-12)

1. **Companies:** `POST /v2/company/tam-by-jobs`, segment filters, **60-day posting window** (2026-09-26 to 2026-11-25). It covers every posting since the 11-12 pull, plus any posting Blitz indexed late. Every company already in an earlier `*-companies.csv` of this segment is skipped. The whole TAM is paged. The TAM is small, so the cost of re-paging known companies is only 226 records.
2. **Open role:** `POST /v2/jobs/company` (`max_results: 1`, same title filters, 60-day window) per new company.
3. **People:** `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword` per new company, same cascade, **3 per company**. TAM/SAM read unchanged: a small pool (only 31 new hiring companies in 13 days), so 3 contacts per company under the inverse rule.
