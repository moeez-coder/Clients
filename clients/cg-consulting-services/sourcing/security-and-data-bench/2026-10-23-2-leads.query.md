## What ran

1. **Batches:** `2026-10-23-leads.csv` was grouped by company into **99 batches** of at most 60 companies or 120 leads. Each row carried name, domain, industry, size band, LinkedIn headcount, type, founding year, HQ, the first 350 characters of Blitz `about`, and the company's leads (URL, name, title, location).
2. **Agents:** one **Claude Haiku 5.5** agent per batch, run as a Claude Code workflow at effort `low`.
   - Each judged every company and every lead individually against `2026-10-23-qualification-spec.md`.
   - No web access: they used the row data plus the model's own knowledge.
   - Pilot: batch 000. Full run: workflow `wf_7e447545-f19`. Re-run of 4 incomplete batches: `wf_55adf1a4-031`; it had not returned when this snapshot was cut.
   - About **15.5M Haiku tokens** in total, roughly $2–4.
3. **Pilot fix before the full run:** credit unions and mutual insurers are not "nonprofits". This is recorded in the spec.
4. **Merge:** `merge_qualification.py <out> 2026-10-23 2026-10-23-2 --allow-pending`.
   - A lead is QUALIFIED only if both it and its company are.
   - The 3-per-company cap is re-ranked among qualified leads only (`qualified_rank`, `within_company_cap`).
   - **5 leads came back without a verdict.** They are marked `PENDING` and were not pushed.
