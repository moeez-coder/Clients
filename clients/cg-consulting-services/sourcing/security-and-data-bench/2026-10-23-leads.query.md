## Tool and query

**Blitz API**, flat plan; about 41k records used of ~6.9M. Both sweeps are cursor-paged at `max_results` 25. Both totals are under the 50k-per-query cap, so no sharding was needed.

### Companies: `POST /v2/search/companies`

```json
{"company": {
  "industry": {"include": "<33 labels, spec.BLITZ_INDUSTRIES>"},
  "hq": {"country_code": ["US"]},
  "employee_range": ["201-500", "501-1000", "1001-5000"],
  "revenue": {"min": 20000000}}}
```

`total_results` was 28,850, and all 28,850 rows came back. That is 28,579 unique companies after normalising the company LinkedIn URL.

### People: `POST /v2/search/people`

The same company filter, plus:

```json
{"people": {
  "job_title": {"include": "<19 keywords, spec.TITLE_KEYWORDS>", "exclude": ["Assistant", "Intern", "Executive Assistant", "Advisor", "Former", "Recruiter", "Sales"]},
  "location": {"country_code": ["US"]}}}
```

`total_results` was 12,482, and all 12,482 came back. The first attempt died at 5,350 rows on an `IncompleteRead`; the people sweep was re-run from scratch after the fix in commit "make Blitz sweep resumable".

**Industries** were taken verbatim from `docs/blitz-api/company-search-industries.txt` (PR #7):

## Cleaning (`build_csv.py`)

1. LinkedIn URLs were normalised to `https://www.linkedin.com/in/<slug>` and deduplicated; there were 0 duplicates.
2. **Persona title check** on the matched current title:
   - The title must be one of CIO, CTO, CISO, VP/SVP/EVP Engineering, VP of Data, Head of Data or Head of IT.
   - Combined titles count, e.g. "Chief Product & Technology Officer" or "VP Platform Engineering".
   - Rows dropped:

   | Reason | Rows |
   |---|---|
   | Field, sales, solutions or customer engineering, or partner-facing CTO | 395 |
   | Deputy, assistant or associate | 208 |
   | Not a persona role (e.g. Director of IT) | 191 |
   | Fractional, advisory or consultant | 55 |
   | In residence, former or board | 9 |

3. **Company join:** the lead's matched-job company must be in the company sweep. 30 were dropped.
4. **Per-company ranking:** role priority CIO → CTO → CISO → VP Engineering → VP of Data → Head of Data → Head of IT, then the most recent start date. `company_rank` and `within_company_cap` (cap 3) are written on every row. **No lead is deleted for the cap.** 8,393 are within the cap and 3,201 are over it and flagged `no`.

- Blitz `revenue.min` was applied, but company rows don't return revenue, so it can't be checked locally.
- No email enrichment was done; output is LinkedIn URLs only.
