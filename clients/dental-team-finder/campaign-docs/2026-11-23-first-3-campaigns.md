# Dental Team Finder: First 3 Campaigns

## Overview

Goal: qualified discovery calls with Keith McLachlan (https://calendly.com/keithmclachlan) from commercial leaders at dental companies who are building or fixing their sales, marketing and leadership teams.

Who we target: dental manufacturers, dental AI and software companies, dental supply and distribution companies, dental service providers, and DSO groups with a corporate commercial team. United States and Canada. 51 to 1,000 employees and $5M+ revenue, plus the named dream accounts (Overjet, Pearl, VideaHealth, Align Technology) at any size.

Titles we target: CEO, Chief Revenue Officer, Chief Commercialization Officer, VP of Sales, VP of Marketing, Sr. Director of Sales, Director of Sales, Regional Director of Sales, Sr. Regional Manager, Regional Manager, Regional Sales Manager, Head of Sales, Head of Marketing.

Never contacted: Territory Managers, Territory Sales Managers, Sales Representatives, Sales Associates, Independent Contractors, individual practice owners and solo practitioners, any B2C company, any company outside dental, and anyone on the Dental Team Finder do-not-contact list.

Sending account: all 3 campaigns send from Keith McLachlan's own LinkedIn account, in Keith's voice.

Tone: consultative, warm and professional. Dental-specific in every message. We never mention fees or pricing, never use "staffing", and never name other recruiting firms.

Approval: Keith reviews and approves every message in this doc before anything is sent.

## Campaign 1: Is That Role Still Open? (Most Placeable Candidate)

### Offer

A specific, screened candidate Keith is already working with, matched to a role the company is advertising. We ask whether the role is still open and offer to send the candidate's anonymised profile for free, with no obligation.

### Persona

The hiring manager for the matching role: VP of Sales, Director of Sales or Regional Director of Sales for sales roles; VP of Marketing for marketing roles; CEO, CRO or Chief Commercialization Officer for VP and C-level roles.

### ICP

Dental companies from the overview with a live job post that matches the candidate's role, seniority and location. Any posting date: the message asks whether the role is still open, so it works for new and older posts. Up to 50 jobs per candidate. Never the candidate's current or past employers.

### Lead list

Job post scrape of LinkedIn and Indeed for roles that match the candidate, then the hiring manager found for each post in Sales Navigator. One campaign per candidate. Keith provides the candidate's resume or anonymised profile, and the candidate has agreed to be put forward.

### Candidate profile

Every message uses the anonymised profile, never the resume itself: a one-liner (seniority, role, dental sector, location), years of experience, and 2 wins with numbers. Past employers are described by type and size, never by name. No name, photo, contact details or LinkedIn URL.

### Messaging

Connection note

Hi {first_name}, I came across the {job_title} role at {company}. Is it still open? I think I may have a candidate who's a strong fit.

Message 1 (on connect)

Thanks for connecting, {first_name}.

Is the {job_title} role at {company} still open? I'm working with {candidate_one_liner} who {candidate_proof_1} and {candidate_proof_2}, and I think they may be one of the best fits for it.

Happy to send over their profile, free and with no obligation. Would that be useful?

Message 2 (2 days later)

Quick one, {first_name}. If the {job_title} role is still open, I think this candidate is worth a look. Shall I send the profile over?

Message 3 (3 days later)

No problem if you've already filled it, {first_name}. If not, just reply here and I'll pass the profile over.

### Accounts

Keith McLachlan's own LinkedIn account.

## Campaign 2: Keith's Network, Relationship First

### Offer

A catch-up chat over a virtual coffee. Nothing is sold. The aim is to reconnect, hear what the person is working on, and stay front of mind for when they next hire.

### Persona

Keith's existing 1st-degree LinkedIn connections who hold a target title (CEO, CRO, Chief Commercialization Officer, VP of Sales, VP of Marketing, Director of Sales, Regional Director of Sales, Regional Manager) at a dental company.

### ICP

Any dental company that fits the overview, any size. No signal needed: the relationship is the reason to reach out.

### Lead list

Keith's 1st-degree connections, exported from his LinkedIn account in HeyReach and filtered to target titles at dental companies. Excluded titles, current clients and anyone already in Campaign 1 removed.

### Messaging

Message 1

Hi {first_name}, it's been a while since we last caught up. I hope things are going well at {company}.

I've been spending a lot of time with commercial leaders across the dental space lately, and I'd love to hear what you're working on and how the year is shaping up for your team.

Would you be open to a virtual coffee in the next couple of weeks?

Message 2 (4 days later)

No rush at all, {first_name}. I know the end of the year gets busy.

If a quick 20 minutes in early December works better, I'm happy to fit around you.

Message 3 (7 days later)

One last note from me, {first_name}. If now isn't the right time, no problem at all. My door is always open whenever you want to compare notes on what's happening in dental.

### Accounts

Keith McLachlan's own LinkedIn account.

## Campaign 3: Dental Commercial Team Health Check

### Offer

A free 30-minute consultation with Keith (normally $350) on the commercial team: which sales and marketing hires to make next, where turnover or uncovered territories are costing them, and what strong dental talent looks like in the market right now.

### Persona

CEO, Chief Revenue Officer, Chief Commercialization Officer, VP of Sales, Sr. Director of Sales, Director of Sales, Regional Director of Sales and VP of Marketing.

### ICP

Dental equipment, device, implant, imaging, aligner, materials and supply manufacturers and distributors, dental AI and software companies, and DSO groups with a corporate commercial team. United States and Canada, 51 to 1,000 employees, $5M+ revenue. Overjet, Pearl, VideaHealth and Align Technology are named accounts. Companies that raised funding in the last 12 months go first. No live job post needed.

### Lead list

Sales Navigator search for the target titles at dental companies in the size range, plus the four named accounts. Cleaned against the excluded titles, the do-not-contact list, Keith's 1st-degree connections and anyone already in Campaign 1.

### Messaging

Connection note

Hi {first_name}, I work with commercial leaders across dental on building sales and marketing teams. Would be glad to connect.

Message 1 (on connect)

Thanks for connecting, {first_name}.

Growing revenue starts with having the right people in place. Most dental sales leaders I speak with aren't short on ambition. They're short on time, and tired of bad hires that don't stick and recruiters who send dozens of unqualified resumes.

I work in dental exclusively, and my team and I keep a pool of warm candidates already looking for great companies. I'd be glad to offer you a free 30-minute team health check (normally $350) to look at where hiring, turnover or open territories are slowing {company} down.

Would that be worth a conversation?

Message 2 (4 days later)

Following up, {first_name}. I'd rather build long-term partnerships than one-off placements, and some of our clients have been with us for over 10 years. If there's a role on your mind for 2027, now is a good time to get ahead of it.

Here's my calendar if useful: https://calendly.com/keithmclachlan

Message 3 (5 days later)

Last one from me, {first_name}. If the team is fully set for now, that's great to hear. Happy to stay in touch for when things change.

### Accounts

Keith McLachlan's own LinkedIn account.
