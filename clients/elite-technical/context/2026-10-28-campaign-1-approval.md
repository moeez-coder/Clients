    Cleared Talent for New Federal Contracts  •  October 28, 2026
    Approval status
    Approved for implementation subject to the targeting, verification, and messaging requirements below. This approval applies to Campaign 1 only. Do not launch the remaining nine campaigns without separate approval.
    Required target criteria
    Annual company revenue: $100 million to $1 billion inclusive. This is a hard filter; do not include companies outside this range.
    Geography: Washington, DC Metro first, East Coast second, Midwest third. Exclude West Coast headquartered companies.
    Industries: U.S. federal contractors, government services, aerospace and defense, and related IT/engineering businesses. Exclude staffing and recruiting competitors.
    Contacts: Director/VP of Engineering, Director/VP of IT, Director of Technical Operations; relevant Talent Acquisition and contingent workforce decision makers when responsible for supplier selection or program hiring.
    Trigger: independently verified federal contract award within the prior 60 days. Confirm recipient, award, date, and relevance. Do not assume an award automatically creates hiring.
    Final LinkedIn outreach sequence
    1 | Connection request — Day 1
    Hi {first_name}, congratulations on the recent {contract_or_agency} award! Exciting news for your team. I’d welcome the opportunity to connect.
    2 | First message — After acceptance
    Thanks for connecting, {first_name}!
    Congratulations again on the {contract_or_agency} award. I imagine there’s quite a bit of planning underway as the program gets moving.
    We’ve been supporting federal contractors with specialized IT and engineering talent for 34 years, including positions requiring security clearances.
    Curious, are you anticipating any challenging technical hires as this program ramps up?
    3 | Second message — About 7 days later if no reply
    Hi {first_name}, one thing we’ve seen over the years is that new federal programs can create some pretty challenging hiring demands, especially when specialized technical skills and active clearances are both required.
    Elite Technical holds its own DoD Top Secret Facility Clearance and has a dedicated Facility Security Officer.
    Are cleared technical positions part of your hiring plans for this program?
    4 | Third message — About 7 days later if no reply
    Hi {first_name}, one last thought.
    If you’re facing any difficult IT or engineering openings tied to the new contract, we’d be happy to take a look and share what we’re seeing in the talent market.
    No obligation, just an opportunity to see whether we might be a helpful recruiting resource.
    Would that be of interest?
    5 | Positive reply — Manual response
    Great to hear, {first_name}!
    I’d enjoy learning a little more about the program and where you may need additional recruiting support.
    Would you have 15 minutes sometime next week for a quick introduction? Happy to work around your schedule.

    Every outreach must reference a real, verified award. If the award or contact’s involvement is uncertain, do not use award-specific copy; hold for review.
    Do not invent program ramp-ups, job openings, clearance requirements, candidate availability, or hiring plans.
    Use the prospect’s actual award or agency name and review the message for natural phrasing before sending.
    Do not send identical sequences to multiple contacts at one company at the same time. Coordinate account coverage and respect the Do-Not-Contact list.
    Pause all automated messages upon any substantive reply; route interested prospects to Elite for a personal response.
    Do not insert a calendar link until a prospect has expressed interest or agreed to a call.
    Do not promise candidate counts or clearance availability without recruiter validation. Our facility clearance does not mean every candidate has an active clearance.
    Track weekly: eligible accounts, verified triggers, invites, acceptances, replies, positive replies, qualified meetings, supplier onboarding discussions, signed agreements, and pipeline.
