## Caller FAQs

Q: What is this about?
A: I'm calling on behalf of Elite Technical — they're an IT and engineering staffing firm, 34 years in, based out of Florida with a big footprint in the DC Metro area. You'd connected with us on LinkedIn, so I wanted to put a voice to it rather than keep messaging.

Q: Who are you, exactly? You're not Elite Technical?
A: Correct — I handle the first conversation on their behalf. **My only job is to see if it's worth a 30-minute call with their team**, and if it isn't, I'll happily say so and leave you alone.

Q: How did you get my details?
A: Through LinkedIn — you're in a role that looks after technical hiring or supplier relationships, which is who Elite Technical works with. Nothing more clever than that.

Q: What does it cost?
A: Staffing rates depend on the role, the skillset and whether it's contract or permanent, so I'd be guessing. **That's exactly what the call is for** — and the first piece, the talent assessment, costs nothing.

Q: We already have recruiters / an agency on the PSL.
A: Most people we speak to do. They're usually brought in on the roles the existing list keeps handing back — cleared work, niche engineering, anything that's been open ninety days. Worth hearing where they'd fit as a second string.

Q: We hire internally, we don't use agencies.
A: Understood. The angle that usually interests internal teams is the screening side — video interviews and identity verification, so you're not burning your own hiring managers on candidates who aren't who they say they are. That's a short conversation.

Q: What do I actually get out of the call?
A: Pick one role you're struggling to fill. They'll come back with **who's actually available, what the market rate is and how fast it could be filled** — free, no obligation. Even if you never use them, you've got real market data.

Q: Who else do you work with?
A: They've worked with 400+ clients over 34 years across IT, engineering and defense, and they hold a Department of Defense Top Secret facility clearance. For names relevant to your space, ask them directly on the call — they'll be specific.

Q: How long before we see anything?
A: On the assessment, days not months. On an actual role, that's down to the brief — the team will give you a straight answer on the call rather than me guessing.

Q: I'm not the one who decides this.
A: No problem — who owns technical hiring or supplier selection on your side? Happy to include you both, or just take the name and go from there.

Q: Just send me an email.
A: I can do that. **Can I put 30 minutes in alongside it** so it doesn't die in your inbox? If it's not useful on the day, cancel it and I won't chase.
