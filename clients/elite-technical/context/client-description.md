## What they do

Elite Technical is a technical recruiting and staffing firm, founded in 1992 and headquartered in Ronkonkoma, New York, that places vetted IT and engineering professionals into high-stakes industries. Since 1992, Elite Technical has provided technical recruiting and staffing solutions across Aerospace & Defense, Healthcare IT, Government, and Commercial Enterprise, with senior recruiters combining industry expertise, AI-powered recruiting, and candidate verification to deliver highly qualified talent.

Their core services span several engagement structures. For permanent roles, Direct Placement brings deep role understanding, right-to-represent confirmation, and a commitment to long-term fit; Elite can also participate in MSP and VMS-managed programs; and its Technical/Contract Staffing provides vetted IT and engineering talent with technical screening, submittal integrity, and account continuity. Flexible Engagement Options include contract, contract-to-hire, staff augmentation, subcontracting, and SOW arrangements that fit the client's procurement model, project timeline, and internal buying process. They also offer program management staffing and IT consulting. As an IT staffing and consulting company, Elite both places vetted technology professionals and provides consultants who define, plan and deliver technical initiatives, covering enterprise strategy, ERP on SAP/Oracle/Microsoft, IoT and cloud, cybersecurity, GDPR compliance, software services, and industry-specific healthcare, government and financial IT consulting.

What makes them distinct is their emphasis on submittal integrity and verification. Through SubmittalCheck, their proprietary candidate authorization system, every submittal represents a candidate they have properly screened and confirmed the right to represent—reducing wasted time and hiring headaches. They pair this with a technology-driven sourcing approach: cutting-edge AI and world-class technologies enable their recruiters to identify active and passive candidates ideally suited for client positions, and this smarter sourcing, paired with personal engagement, connects them with top talent faster. Their industry-specific IQD Recruiting Process ensures they identify, qualify and deliver people with the specialised technical experience each client needs. They also deploy recruiting triads that include veteran recruiting engineers who have spent years hands-on in the technical trenches, supporting senior and supporting recruiters with insight into talent requirements. As a firm, they are a nationally recognised, woman-owned technical staffing partner that started more than 33 years ago as a bootstrapped IT recruiting firm on the East Coast.

## Who they target

Their primary buyers are employers in specialised, high-consequence technical sectors where hiring mistakes are costly. The industries they serve are Healthcare IT, Aerospace & Defense, Government / Government Adjacent, and Commercial Enterprise. Their broader industry list also spans Aerospace & Defense, Automotive, Commercial Enterprise, Financial IT, Government / Government Adjacent, Healthcare IT, Legal Enterprise, Medical Device, Media and Broadcasting, Power and Energy, and Telecommunications.

In company size and profile, they target large and enterprise organisations. Over the years, Elite Technical has grown into a trusted staffing partner for Fortune 500 companies, enterprise organisations, government contractors, healthcare systems, and technology-driven businesses nationwide. Named clients and placements referenced on the site include APWU Health Plan, BlueCross BlueShield, Healthfirst, Boeing, CSRA, L3 Communications, Harris Corporation, and UNISYS. In the federal space, they have served the departments of Defense, Homeland Security, Treasury, and Health & Human Services, among others, and in aerospace/defense they have worked with the Department of Defense, the Department of Homeland Security, the wider intelligence community, and NASA, as well as other federal customers.

Within financial services, they serve brokerage firms, commercial banks, credit unions, foreign exchange, investment banks, insurance companies, and savings & loan associations. They also work across the contracting chain — primes, subcontractors and government-adjacent organisations under contract, contract-to-hire, direct placement and SOW arrangements, and as a certified WOSB they can support supplier diversity goals.

On the buyer-role side, their messaging targets hiring managers and technical leaders responsible for getting specialised hires right, plus procurement and program stakeholders. Their IT consulting positioning explicitly addresses discerning managers and leading companies. Their second audience is technical professionals and job seekers — they help candidates find work suited to their skills, preferences, and long-term goals, with direct access to top employers in healthcare IT, aerospace, and enterprise technology.

As a supplier, they fit specific diversity and size criteria that matter to enterprise and government buyers: they are a privately held Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) with SBA size standards of under 150 employees and under $37M.
