**Why:** on 2026-11-02 Moe said: "Just keep it USA, remove the other region filters for now." The DC Metro, East Coast and Midwest state filter was dropped. HQ only needs to be in the US, which means West Coast companies are now included. That reverses the "never the West Coast" rule from the ICP form for this campaign, on Moe's instruction.

## Method

1. `scripts/tbj_us.py` runs Blitz `POST /v2/company/tam-by-jobs` with pass B's job filters: 27 tech title keywords, Hybrid or Remote, posted in the last 90 days.
   - Company filters: `is_agency: false`, 501–10,000 employees, `hq.country_code` US, and **no state filter**.
   - **5,188 companies.**
2. The same filters as the first pull: $100M–$1B revenue, excluded industries, staffing and the DNC list. **3,109 companies passed.**
   - 1,376 of those were new, meaning not already in `2026-11-02-companies.csv`.
   - The new ones are mostly California (536), Texas (249), unknown state (148), Colorado (78) and Washington (78).
3. `scripts/c9_people3.py` uses the same Blitz waterfall cascade. It found contacts at 824 of the 1,376 companies, 1,724 people in all.
4. `scripts/c9_build.py` does the title clean-up. Selection is 2 per company, P1 first. 3 people already in another snapshot were skipped.

## Output

- `2026-11-02-2-companies.csv`: the 1,376 new companies.
- `2026-11-02-2-leads.csv`: 1,531 prospects. **1,175 are selected, at 797 companies.**
- **Campaign 9 total across both snapshots: 2,633 leads at 1,811 companies.**
