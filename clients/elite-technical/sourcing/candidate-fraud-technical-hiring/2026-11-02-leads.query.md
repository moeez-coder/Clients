## Method (scripts in `scripts/`)

1. **Trigger → companies:** Blitz `POST /v2/company/tam-by-jobs` returns the distinct companies with matching live job postings.
   - **Pass A** (`tbj.py`, `tbj_all.py`):
     - job title contains one of 15 core tech titles (Software Engineer/Developer, DevOps, Data Engineer, Cloud, Cybersecurity/Security Engineer, Systems/Network Engineer, SRE, Full Stack, Java/.NET, Solutions Architect, Sysadmin)
     - `work_arrangement` is Hybrid, Remote OK or Remote Solely
     - `date_posted.last_days` is 30
     - company `is_agency: false`, `employee_count` 501–10,000, HQ country US, HQ state in 27 DC Metro, East Coast or Midwest states (exact match)
     - **993 companies.**
   - **Pass B** (`tbj_wide.py`): the same filters, plus 12 broader tech keywords (Engineer, Developer, Data Scientist, Architect, ML, Programmer, QA Automation, IT Manager, Infrastructure, DBA, Platform, Embedded), with the window widened to 90 days.
     - **2,798 companies**, of which 993 overlap with pass A.
     - Pass B was needed because pass A yielded about 740 clean leads, short of the 1,000 target.
2. **Filters:**
   - Blitz `revenue_range` must be `$100M-$1B`. This is Joseph's hard filter for all ten campaigns.
   - Excluded industries: education, government administration, nonprofit, public policy, public safety, and individual/family services.
   - Names matching staffing or recruiting were excluded.
   - The DNC list was applied with the minimum-length name rule. This dropped 12, including Thales, Noblis, By Light, PenFed, Capital Blue Cross, Healthfirst, Red River and Textron Specialized Vehicles.
   - Two DNC hits are probably false positives but were left excluded to be safe: "The Aerospace Corporation" (it matched "British Aerospace") and "Optimum" (it matched "Catamaran Optimum Rx").
   - **1,733 companies passed**: 655 from pass A and 1,078 new from pass B. The `window` column records which pass found each one.
3. **People:** `c9_people.py` / `c9_people2.py` call Blitz `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword` once per company, with `max_results` 5. The cascade is:
   - Tier 1: Director, VP or Head of Talent Acquisition or Recruiting.
   - Tier 2: Talent Acquisition, Recruiting or Technical Recruiting Manager.
   - Excluded titles: assistant, intern, coordinator, sourcer, specialist, partner, consultant, contract.
   - This returned 2,043 people at 1,062 companies.
4. **Build:** `c9_build.py` re-checks each title. It must contain "talent acquisition" or "recruit" at Director/VP/Head level (P1) or Manager level (P2). Campus, payroll, benefits and operations roles are dropped, as are duplicate people and missing names.
   - **Per-company depth: 2 contacts, P1 first.** The TAM is large (about 1,000 companies with contacts), so the repo standard calls for fewer per company. Two gives a TA leader plus a recruiting manager.
   - 25 people already in another Elite campaign were left unselected.
