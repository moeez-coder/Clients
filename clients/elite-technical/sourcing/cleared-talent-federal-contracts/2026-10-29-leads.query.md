# 2026-10-29 sourcing: Campaign 1, "Cleared Talent for New Federal Contracts"

**Segment status:** new segment (the first sourcing for Elite Technical).

**Spec:** the client-approved Campaign 1 doc, [`context/2026-10-28-campaign-1-approval.md`](../../context/2026-10-28-campaign-1-approval.md):
- revenue $100M–$1B, a hard filter
- DC Metro first, then East Coast, then Midwest; no West Coast
- federal contractors, government services, A&D and IT/engineering; no staffing competitors
- contacts: Director/VP Eng/IT/Tech Ops, then TA or contingent-workforce decision makers
- trigger: a verified federal contract award in the prior 60 days

**Pipeline:** USAspending (trigger) → Blitz (company match and revenue) → Blitz + Clay (revenue cross-check and contacts). LinkedIn URLs only; no email enrichment.

## 1. Trigger: USAspending.gov (free public API)

`POST https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/search/spending_by_award/`, script `scripts/usas_pull.py`. Filters:
- `award_type_codes`: A, B, C, D (definitive contracts, purchase orders, delivery orders and BPA calls)
- `time_period`: 2026-08-30 to 2026-10-29 with `date_type: new_awards_only`
- `recipient_locations`, one query per state:
  - DC Metro: DC, MD, VA
  - East Coast: ME NH VT MA RI CT NY NJ PA DE NC SC GA FL
  - Midwest: OH MI IN IL WI MN IA MO ND SD NE KS
- `naics_codes.require`: 5415 (computer systems design), 5413 (engineering), 5417 (R&D), 5416 (consulting), 5182 (data processing/hosting), 3364 (aerospace), 3345 (instruments), 5419
- `award_amounts`: at least $1M

Results:
- 1,706 awards. Descriptions matching option/extension/bridge/renewal/continuation/annual-call/licence/equipment/hardware were dropped as "not a new program", leaving 1,497 awards from 787 distinct recipients. Each recipient's largest qualifying award was kept.
- Each kept award's `date_signed` was then re-checked through `GET /api/v2/awards/{generated_internal_id}/`. Every lead's award was signed between 2026-08-31 and 2026-09-30, inside the 60-day window as of 2026-10-29.

**Known gap: DoD.** FPDS publishes DoD contract actions with a 90-day delay, so DoD awards from the last 60 days are almost entirely missing. This list is civilian-agency heavy (DHS, HHS, VA, State, NASA, EPA…). A second source is needed for defense awards, such as defense.gov daily contract announcements.

## 2. Company match and revenue: Blitz API

- `POST /v2/search/companies` was called once per recipient with `company.name.include=[normalized recipient name]` and `hq.country_code=["US"]`, `max_results` 5.
- A match was accepted on an exact normalized name (524) or an exact token-set match (12). 251 recipients had no confident match and were dropped.
- Kept only `revenue_range == "$100M-$1B"`, which left 51.
- Manual relevance review removed 19:
  - universities, think tanks and accreditation bodies
  - product, licence and equipment purchase awards
  - international-development firms
  - duplicates of an entity already kept

  That left 33. The reasons are in `2026-10-29-companies.csv` under `decision`.

## 3. Revenue cross-check and entity checks: Clay

Clay `search-contacts` (4 calls, 10 companies each) also returns each company's `annual_revenue` band. Both sources were applied:
- **Excluded** for falling outside $100M–$1B in Clay, being an entity mismatch, or being a subsidiary of a $1B+ parent:
  - Institute for Defense Analyses (1B-10B)
  - ILC Dover (1B-10B, part of Ingersoll Rand)
  - Goldbelt C6 (25M-75M)
  - Systems Plus (the LinkedIn page is a different Dallas/Pune company)
  - WSP USA Solutions and Leidos Biomedical Research (subsidiaries of $1B+ parents)
  - Manhattan Associates (public company with revenue above $1B)
- **`confirmed`**: both Blitz ($100M-$1B) and Clay (200M-500M or 500M-1B) are inside the range.
- **`overlap-verify`**: Clay says 75M-200M, which straddles the $100M floor. This covers Telos, AMA, Aptive, GAP Solutions, REI Systems, Sev1Tech and MIL Corp. They are **held out of wave 1** until their revenue is confirmed.
- Lynker and NORC produced no matching contacts from either source.

## 4. Contacts: Blitz + Clay, LinkedIn URLs only

- **Blitz** `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword`, one call per company, `max_results` 8, person location US. The cascade (the exact JSON is in `scripts/blitz_people.py`) had two tiers:
  - Tier 1: VP/Director of Engineering, VP/Director of IT, Director of Technical Operations, Director of Software Engineering, Director of Infrastructure, CTO, CIO
  - Tier 2: Director/Head/VP of Talent Acquisition, Director/VP of Recruiting, Contingent Workforce/Labor, Workforce Solutions
- **Clay** `search-contacts`, scoped by company LinkedIn URL, US-located people, with:
  `job_title is_similar_to (Director of Engineering, VP of Engineering, Director of IT, VP of IT, Director of Technical Operations, CIO, CTO, Director of Talent Acquisition, Head of Talent Acquisition, Contingent Workforce Manager) limit 6 by clay_company_id`.
  Rows whose current company didn't match the target were dropped.
- Merged and deduped on the normalized `linkedin_url` (rule 3b). `source` is `blitz`, `clay` or `blitz+clay`.
- **`title_priority`:**
  - 1 = Director/VP-level IT, engineering or technical operations (the client's listed titles)
  - 2 = TA or contingent-workforce leader
  - 3 = C-suite only (CIO/CTO/CISO). These are **not** on the ICP form's title list ("avoid any other than what we listed"), so they are held out of wave 1.
  - Assistants, recruiters, coordinators, sub-director managers and people at another company were removed.

**Result:** 98 leads across 24 companies (64 P1, 9 P2, 25 P3; 85 at revenue-confirmed companies), in `2026-10-29-leads.csv`.

## 5. TAM/SAM read and contacts per company

- **The TAM is small.** 24 companies have usable contacts and only 17 are revenue-confirmed, because the 60-day award trigger, the $100M–$1B band and the civilian-only data window all narrow it.
- Per the inverse rule, a small TAM means **more contacts per company**: up to 4 per company over the life of the campaign.
- The client's own rule overrides doing that all at once: "Do not send identical sequences to multiple contacts at one company at the same time." So contacts are **staggered one per company per wave** (`company_wave_rank`):
  - **Wave 1** = rank 1 at each revenue-confirmed company with a P1/P2 contact, which is **17 leads** (`2026-10-29-heyreach-wave1.csv`).
  - Rank 2+ follow once the previous contact at that company has finished the sequence, about 3 weeks later (day 0 plus 7 plus 7 after acceptance).

## 6. Personalization variable

`contract_or_agency` is the awarding agency's common short name (NIH, VA, TSA, FEMA, EPA, …), taken from USAspending `Awarding Sub Agency`. Where that is a generic procurement office, the parent department is used instead:
- DHS Office of Procurement Operations → "DHS"
- HHS OASFR → "HHS"
- DOL OASAM → "Department of Labor"
- Interior Departmental Offices → "Department of the Interior"

The full award ID, date and USAspending URL are on every lead row, for review.
