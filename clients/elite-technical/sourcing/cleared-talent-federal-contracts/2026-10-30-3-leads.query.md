# 2026-10-30-3 leads pull — Campaign 1b (12-month award window)

**Config:** ICP config *Cleared Talent for New Federal Contracts* `d286c219-eb64-4aa8-bccf-3a800c3e4d08`, lead list `f52caf59-e687-4ec4-94de-5d4d2862bc55`.
**Segment:** expanding an existing segment (Campaign 1). The award window is widened from 60 days to 12 months, on Moe's instruction ("Widen the award window it's fine").
**Why this method:** matching award recipients to companies one by one gave too few companies with $100M–$1B revenue. Moe chose option 1 ("go ahead with 1"): run the Blitz firmographic sweep first, then keep only companies that also won a qualifying award.

## Steps (scripts in `scripts/`)

1. **Awards:** `usas_pull_12mo.py` calls USAspending `POST /api/v2/search/spending_by_award/`.
   - Filters: `new_awards_only` from 2025-10-30 to 2026-10-29; recipient state in the DC Metro, East Coast and Midwest states; award amount ≥ $500k.
   - NAICS must be one of: 5415, 5413, 5417, 5416, 5182, 3364, 3345, 5419, 5112, 5132, 3344, 3342, 3341, 5174.
   - This step yielded about 3,825 recipients.
   - `dod_pull_aug.py` added DoD awards from the war.gov daily announcements (via Exa). Coverage for August is thin: one page.
2. **Companies:** `blitz_company_sweep.py` calls Blitz `POST /v2/search/companies` once per state, 29 states in total.
   - Filters: 19 LinkedIn industries (defense/space, IT services, engineering, software, security, research, aerospace, consulting, telecom and others), `revenue` $100M–$1B, and US HQ in that state.
   - This step returned 6,531 companies; 4,050 of them are tagged `$100M-$1B`.
3. **Match:** `award_match.py` matches name tokens and HQ state between the $100M–$1B sweep companies and the award recipients. It keeps the largest award per company and found 153 matches.
   - Each match was then checked by hand for name collisions and staffing-firm exclusion.
   - DNC names were excluded with the same minimum-length substring rule as the earlier pulls.
4. **People:** `blitz_people_batch1.py` calls Blitz `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword` with `max_results` 8.
   - P1 titles: VP/Director of Engineering, IT, Technical Ops, Software Engineering and Infrastructure. CTO and CIO are fetched but held.
   - P2 titles: TA and contingent-workforce leads.
5. **Build:** `build_batch.py` writes the leads CSV.
   - It sets `selected_for_launch` = 2–3 per company, P1 first. Per Moe ("2-3 per company is fine for now"), TAM is the binding constraint, so this goes deeper per account.
   - Each row carries its verified award (`award_id`, `award_url`, amount, agency) and `contract_or_agency`, the agency short name used in the connection note.
