# 2026-10-30 sourcing: Campaign 1, widened pull with 2–3 contacts per company

**Segment status:** expanding existing segment `cleared-talent-federal-contracts`. The method of [2026-10-29](2026-10-29-leads.query.md) applies; this file records only what changed.

**Why:** Moe asked for at least 1,000 leads to start. He then approved **2–3 contacts per company at once** for now (2026-10-30). This **deviates from Joseph's Campaign 1 doc**, which says "do not send identical sequences to multiple contacts at one company at the same time". Joseph has not been told yet.

## What changed vs 2026-10-29

1. **Civilian awards, wider net:** `scripts/usas_pull_wide.py`.
   - All NAICS codes (no industry filter), a floor of $250k (was $1M), and new IDVs as well as contracts.
   - Same 60-day window and the same states.
   - Result: 7,302 awards from 3,044 recipients after dropping option/extension/renewal/bridge awards.
2. **DoD awards** (FPDS delays DoD data 90 days):
   - `scripts/dod_pull.py` uses Exa search plus `/contents` to fetch the daily "Contracts for <date>" announcements on war.gov. 36 of ~43 business days were found; 34 had text. Cost: **$0.34 Exa**.
   - `scripts/dod_parse.py` parses single-awardee paragraphs and drops modifications, options and extensions: 274 new awards, 128 companies in the target states.
   - **Known gap:** about 160 multi-awardee paragraphs are not parsed yet.
3. **Blitz company match:** `scripts/blitz_match_wide.py`, plus a token-set fallback.
   - 2,112 of 3,172 recipients matched; **211 are in the `$100M-$1B` band**.
   - The match used ~6.5k Blitz records.
4. **Relevance filter:**
   - Dropped by industry: construction, food, hospitals, universities, nonprofits, law and similar.
   - Dropped staffing competitors, West Coast HQs, other HQs outside the target regions (TX, CO), and DNC matches (Noblis, Trident Maritime).
   - Then a manual review of the remaining 103 dropped product and equipment purchases (guns, furniture, laundry machines, commodities, licences), guard and detention services, and expert-witness work.
   - **That leaves 45 companies.** Two more fall out on award date: DLH and Michael Baker were signed 2026-08-31, outside 60 days as of 2026-10-30. **43 remain.**
5. **Revenue cross-check (Clay):** 2 more `search-contacts` calls. Newly excluded:
   - SRC Inc (1B-10B)
   - CDM Federal (subsidiary of CDM Smith)
   - MAG DS (10M-25M)
6. **Contacts:**
   - Blitz `waterfall-icp-keyword` for 19 new companies (52 people), plus Clay.
   - The 3 contacts found stale on 2026-10-29 are excluded.
   - Selection per company: up to 2 P1 (Director/VP IT/Eng/Tech Ops) plus 1 P2 (TA/contingent workforce), filled to 3 from what remains. P3 (C-suite only) is still excluded under the ICP form's "no other titles" rule.
   - Marked in `selected_for_launch`.

## Result

- `2026-10-30-leads.csv`: 145 leads at 38 companies, keyed by `linkedin_url`.
- **76 selected for launch at 34 companies:** 16 companies × 3, 10 × 2, 8 × 1.
  - Revenue: 54 confirmed by both sources; 17 where Clay says 75M-200M, straddling the floor; 5 Blitz-only (Clay has no value).
  - Titles: 59 P1, 17 P2.
- `2026-10-30-companies.csv`: every one of the 43 companies, with its `decision` and award evidence.
- **TAM read:** the Campaign 1 trigger (a new award in 60 days) combined with the $100M–$1B band is **about 40 companies** at any moment. Per the inverse rule a small TAM means more contacts per company, so 2–3 is the right direction. But **the 1,000-lead target cannot be reached within Campaign 1's definition.**
