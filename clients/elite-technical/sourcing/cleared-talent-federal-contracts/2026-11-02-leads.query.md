**Why:** both live campaigns had 0 pending leads on 2026-11-02, and Moe said "Push those to the campaigns".

## Method

1. **Recipients:** `scripts/blitz_match_12mo.py` (run 2026-10-30) matched each 12-month award recipient to a Blitz company.
   - Recipients came from the `usas_pull_12mo.py` USAspending pull plus the war.gov DoD awards.
   - Option, extension, bridge and renewal awards were dropped first.
   - Matching used Blitz `POST /v2/search/companies` with a name filter: an exact normalised name match or an identical token set.
   - 3,825 recipients went in and 2,562 matched.
2. **Filters** (applied in a session script, reproduced in the `decision` column of `2026-11-02-companies.csv`):
   - Blitz `revenue_range` must be `$100M-$1B`. This dropped 2,333.
   - HQ must be in the DC Metro, East Coast or Midwest states. This dropped 33.
   - The company must not appear in any earlier snapshot. This dropped 109.
   - Staffing firms were dropped (1).
   - The DNC list was applied with the minimum-length name rule. This dropped 7: Thales Defense & Security, By Light, Noblis (×2), Natus Medical, Astronautics Corp of America and Trident Maritime Systems.
   - **77 companies passed.**
3. **ICP review (manual):** 26 companies were excluded as outside Joseph's ICP: universities, health systems, a state agency, nonprofits, furniture makers, construction general contractors and an architecture firm.
   - 4 more were excluded because the award is not a hiring trigger: Veradigm and Open Systems International (annual maintenance), Numerator and Circana (data purchases). This follows Joseph's rule: "Do not assume an award automatically creates hiring."
   - **47 companies remained.**
4. **People:** `scripts/blitz_people_batch2.py` calls Blitz `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword` once per company (51 companies, including the 4 later dropped), with `max_results` 8.
   - The cascade is the same as earlier pulls. P1: VP/Director of Engineering, IT, Technical Ops, Software and Infrastructure. P2: TA and contingent-workforce leaders.
   - This returned 174 people.
5. **Build:** `scripts/build_batch.py` picks 2–3 contacts per company: 2 P1 plus 1 P2, filled to 3 from P1. The small TAM justifies going deeper per company, as Moe approved on 2026-10-30.
   - Four duplicate people (the same person under two profile URLs) were removed.
   - Two swapped names were fixed: Pallavi Pratapa and Anthony Colonnese.
   - The `exclusion_reason` column records every drop.
