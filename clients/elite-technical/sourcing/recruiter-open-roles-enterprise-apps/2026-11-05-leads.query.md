## Campaign design

- **Sequence:** the Talent Edge / Alcor "open-role sequence" (19.4% reply rate). Only the role family changes between the 8 campaigns.
  - In message 2 this campaign says "an enterprise applications open role"; in message 3 it says "Enterprise application roles".
- Steps: connection request, then message 1 two days after connecting, message 2 two days later, message 3 two days later.
- Copy: `../../campaigns/2026-11-05-recruiter-open-role-copy.json`.
- **Every lead is tied to one real live posting** at its company, posted in the last 30 days. Its HeyReach custom fields are:
  - `open_role` (cleaned title)
  - `open_role_with_article` ("a"/"an" plus the role)
  - `company_name` (cleaned)
  - `jd`: a 3–10-word phrase that fills "looks like you need someone with ___". It was written by a model from the posting's Blitz `ai_summary`, and phrases are reused across segments when the posting is the same.
- **Persona:** every hiring manager for the role family that Blitz finds at the company, up to 10 per company, with **no per-company cap** (Moe).
  - Head of Talent / Talent Director / VP People is used **only** when no hiring manager is found, and then only 1.
  - Every lead at a company gets the same posting.
  - Nobody appears in more than one Elite campaign. Segments are claimed in the order security → data → cloud → enterprise apps → IT → electrical → mechanical → software.

## Method (scripts in `scripts/`; `segs8.py` holds every title list and regex)

1. **`pull8.py`** runs Blitz `POST /v2/company/tam-by-jobs`.
   - Job title includes: SAP, Oracle, Workday, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Dynamics 365, NetSuite, ERP, Business Systems Analyst, CRM Developer, Epic Analyst, Peoplesoft, Integration Developer.
   - Job title excludes: Sales, Intern, Recruiter, Director, Manager, Vice President, Head of, Chief, Account Executive, Customer Success.
   - Posted in the last 30 days, job location US.
   - Company: `is_agency: false`, 501–10,000 employees, US HQ (US-wide).
   - Filters: Blitz `revenue_range` `$100M-$1B` (Joseph's hard filter); education, government, nonprofit and similar industries out; staffing out; DNC list with the minimum-length name rule.
   - Then, per company:
     - `POST /v2/jobs/company`: up to 5 postings.
     - `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword` for hiring managers: up to 10, titles Director of Enterprise Applications, Enterprise Applications Manager, Director of Business Systems, Business Systems Manager, Director of ERP, ERP Manager, Director of Business Applications, Business Applications Manager, Head of Enterprise Applications, Director of IT Applications, IT Applications Manager, Director of IT, IT Director, VP of IT.
     - The talent fallback, only if no hiring managers came back.
   - `retry8.py` re-ran the 45 calls that errored. 0 errors remained.
2. **`build8.py`** builds the leads:
   - It picks the most recent posting whose cleaned title matches the segment's discipline words, with sales, internship, co-op and leadership-level postings excluded.
   - It keeps every person whose title passes the segment's hiring-manager check, minus other-discipline titles (for example, a software manager never gets an electrical role).
   - It drops anyone already in an Elite campaign or snapshot.
