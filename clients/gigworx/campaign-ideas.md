# GigWorx Campaign Ideas

On-demand shift staffing in Michiana only. Six signal-led LinkedIn campaigns sent from Mathew's own profile. All six are options; which ones run is decided on the pre-launch call. Each one offers something concrete tied to the prospect's own open shifts: their first shift with GigWorx, comped. That way they can judge the workers before any commitment.

## Shared across all six

**Market:** Saint Joseph County and Elkhart County, Indiana (South Bend, Mishawaka, Granger, Elkhart, Goshen, Nappanee, Middlebury, Bristol and surrounding towns). Location is matched on where the person works, not where the company is headquartered. Most of the largest local hourly employers are headquartered out of state.

**Segment:** Manufacturing plants (including RV and RV-supplier plants), warehouses and distribution centres, and retail operations with 11 or more employees at a Michiana site, that staff hourly, shift-based roles

**Persona, primary:** Plant Manager, Operations Manager, Production Manager, Warehouse Manager, General Manager, Owner, President

**Persona, secondary:** Production Supervisor, Shift Supervisor, Distribution Center Manager, Store Manager (large-format stores only), HR Manager, HR Business Partner, HR Director

**Exclusions:**

- Staffing agencies and on-demand staffing competitors, including the local ones (Peoplelink, Express Employment Professionals, Alliance Staffing, Jobsquad, Agile Staffing, TalentSource, Specialized Staffing)
- Car dealerships, office-only businesses, health care, education and nonprofits
- Self-employed and businesses under 11 employees
- Any site outside Saint Joseph and Elkhart County
- Current GigWorx clients, open deals and previous opt-outs, from HubSpot (the DNC list is empty today). Contacts already in another live sequence. One person is in one campaign only. Suppression is refreshed right before launch.

**Connection request (all six):** Hi {first_name}, I run GigWorx here in Michiana. We cover open shifts for local plants, warehouses and stores with vetted workers. Would be good to connect.

**Language:** English. Plain, short, owner to owner, in Mathew's voice. No corporate staffing language.

## Campaign 1: Hourly Roles That Won't Close

**Custom Qualifiers:** Claygent checks the company's own postings on its careers page, Indeed and LinkedIn for hourly roles at a Michiana site. Qualifying roles: production, assembly, machine operator, welder, fabricator, material handler, forklift, warehouse, picker, packer, shipping and general labour. Exclude salaried roles, licensed trades, CDL drivers and postings by staffing agencies. The site must have 3 or more open hourly roles, with at least 1 open for 30+ days or reposted under the same title.

**Persona:** Plant, Production or Operations Manager at that site first, then Production Supervisor, then HR Manager.

**Signal (Claygent-verified, gates inclusion):** in the last 60 days, the site has 3+ open hourly roles and at least 1 is ageing or reposted. Claygent returns {role_title} for the oldest open role, {posted_month}, {open_roles_count}, {city} and the posting URL.

**Angle:** An hourly role that stays open or keeps getting reposted means the floor runs short every shift while hiring catches up. Overtime and missed output cost more than the shift.

**Lead magnet:** Their first {role_title} shift, comped. A yes also tells us the role, the shift and the site.

**Message 1:** Hi {first_name}, saw {company} has had a {role_title} opening up since {posted_month}, with {open_roles_count} other hourly roles open.

While you hire, GigWorx can cover the shifts. You post a shift in our app, vetted local workers claim it, and you only pay for the hours worked.

Want me to comp your first {role_title} shift so you can see the quality for yourself?

**Message 2:** No worries if that one is filled now, {first_name}.

There's no subscription, no minimums and no contract. If a worker isn't a fit, you block them in the app and we handle the conversation. If one is great, you can bring them onto your team.

**Message 3:** I'll leave it here, {first_name}.

If {role_title} is still open next month, the free shift still stands.

**CTA variants to test in Message 1:**

- Want to try one shift on us?
- How are you covering those shifts today? (question only, no offer)
- Is {role_title} still costing you overtime? (question only, no offer)

## Campaign 2: Peak Season Crews

**Custom Qualifiers:** Warehouses, distribution centres, food and beverage plants and large-format retail at a Michiana site. Claygent checks postings for "seasonal", "temporary", "peak" or "holiday" hourly roles, or for 5 or more new hourly postings in the last 30 days. Exclude postings by staffing agencies.

**Persona:** Warehouse Manager, Distribution Center Manager, Operations Manager, General Manager. Secondary: Store Manager at large-format stores, HR Manager.

**Signal (Claygent-verified, gates inclusion):** in the last 45 days, the site posted seasonal or temporary hourly roles, or 5+ new hourly roles. Claygent returns {role_title}, {city}, {open_roles_count} and the posting URL.

**Angle:** Peak volume needs people for weeks, not permanent hires you have to let go in January. Peak is also when call-offs hurt most.

**Lead magnet:** Their first peak shift, comped.

**Message 1:** Hi {first_name}, noticed {company} is hiring seasonal {role_title} help in {city}.

Peak is when call-offs hurt most. GigWorx fills short-notice shifts with vetted local workers, and you only pay for the hours worked. No contract to unwind in January.

Want your first peak shift on us?

**Message 2:** Fair enough if you're staffed up, {first_name}.

It also works as a backup. Keep the app for the days three people call off, post the shift, and a vetted worker claims it.

**Message 3:** Last note from me, {first_name}.

If peak gets tight, just reply "shift" and I'll set up the free one.

**CTA variants to test in Message 1:**

- Shall I set up a free shift for the next busy week?
- How are you covering call-offs during peak? (question only, no offer)

**Timing:** run now through mid-December, then again ahead of the spring peak.

## Campaign 3: RV Corridor Ramps

**Custom Qualifiers:** RV makers and RV suppliers with a plant in Elkhart or Saint Joseph County: motorhomes and travel trailers, cargo and utility trailers, plastics, composites, fibreglass, aluminium, furniture, cabinetry, chassis and components. Claygent confirms from the website that the company builds for or supplies the RV or trailer industry. It must also have either an hourly posting in the last 90 days, or news of a new line, plant or model-year ramp in the last 6 months. For the large OEMs (Thor, Forest River, Lippert, Jayco, Grand Design), target plant-level managers only.

**Persona:** Plant Manager, Production Manager, Production Supervisor. Owner or President at suppliers under 200 employees.

**Signal (Claygent-verified, gates inclusion):** an RV or trailer supply-chain company with a recent hourly posting or ramp news at a Michiana plant. Claygent returns {role_title} or {ramp_note}, {city} and the source URL.

**Angle:** RV output swings with dealer orders and model-year changeovers. Hiring permanent staff for a ramp means layoffs after it, and agencies want notice. Shifts on demand flex with the build schedule.

**Lead magnet:** A comped shift on their next ramp.

**Message 1:** Hi {first_name}, saw {company} is hiring {role_title} in {city}.

RV builds swing with dealer orders, so a lot of plants around here run short on the line one month and overstaffed the next. GigWorx lets you add vetted workers by the shift and only pay for the hours worked.

Want me to comp a shift on your next ramp?

**Message 2:** No worries if the line is staffed, {first_name}.

There's no contract and no minimums, so it can sit there until the weeks you need it. Workers you like can join your team with no minimum hours first.

**Message 3:** I'll leave it here, {first_name}.

When the next model-year push starts, the free shift is there if you want it.

**CTA variants to test in Message 1:**

- How are you covering ramps today: overtime, agency, or running short? (question only, no offer)
- Want to try one shift on us?

**Timing:** start in January, ahead of the spring dealer-stocking build.

## Campaign 4: Try Before You Hire

**Custom Qualifiers:** Any site in the three markets with 51 or more employees and 5 or more open hourly roles posted in the last 60 days. Exclude anyone already in Campaign 1 at the same company.

**Persona:** HR Manager, HR Business Partner, HR Director only.

**Signal (Claygent-verified, gates inclusion):** 5+ open hourly roles at a Michiana site in the last 60 days. Claygent returns {open_roles_count}, {role_title} and the posting URL.

**Angle:** For HR, every hourly hire means screening, onboarding and hoping the person shows up in week two. Seeing someone work real shifts before making an offer cuts bad hires. GigWorx carries the payroll, insurance and compliance in the meantime.

**Lead magnet:** Their first shift, comped, so they can see a worker on the floor before making an offer.

**Message 1:** Hi {first_name}, saw {company} has {open_roles_count} hourly roles open, including {role_title}.

GigWorx lets you see people work real shifts before you make an offer. Our workers are vetted W-2 employees on our payroll, and there are no minimum hours before you can hire one onto your team.

Want your first shift on us to try it?

**Message 2:** No worries if hiring is under control, {first_name}.

If a worker isn't a fit, you block them in the app and we have that conversation, not you. You only pay for the hours worked, with no subscription or contract.

**Message 3:** I'll leave it here, {first_name}.

If the {role_title} openings keep coming back, happy to set up the free shift.

**CTA variants to test in Message 1:**

- Want to try a worker on one shift first?
- How many of your hourly hires are still there after 90 days? (question only, no offer)

## Campaign 5: Owner on the Floor

**Custom Qualifiers:** Owner-operated manufacturers, warehouses and retailers with 11 to 50 employees in the two counties. Small shops rarely post jobs online, so no hiring signal is required. Claygent confirms from the website that the business runs hourly production, warehouse or retail operations. It also confirms the business isn't a staffing firm, a dealer or office-only.

**Persona:** Owner, President, General Manager only.

**Signal (Claygent-verified, gates inclusion):** owner-operated, 11 to 50 employees, with hourly operations at a Michiana site. Claygent returns {what_they_make}, a short phrase such as "custom cabinetry".

**Angle:** In a small shop, when two people call off, the owner ends up on the line.

**Lead magnet:** None in Message 1. Owners respond to a plain question better than to an offer from a stranger. Their answer tells us whether to offer the free shift in the follow-up.

**Message 1:** Hi {first_name}, quick question from one local business owner to another.

When a couple of people call off at {company}, who ends up covering? You, overtime, or does the work just wait?

**Message 2:** Asking because that's what GigWorx is for, {first_name}. You post a shift in our app, a vetted local worker claims it, and you only pay for the hours worked. No contract and no minimums.

Happy to comp your first shift so you can see the quality.

**Message 3:** I'll leave it here, {first_name}.

If you're short-handed on a busy week, reply "shift" and the first one's on us.

**CTA variants to test in Message 1:**

- Who covers when your crew is short? (question only, no offer)
- Would a free shift on your next busy week help?

Exclude staffing agencies and any role needing a trade licence. This campaign only runs if Mathew confirms construction and events sit under "Other" on the onboarding form. Suggested order: Campaigns 2 and 1 first, from now, because peak season and open roles point most directly at a current need. Campaign 3 from January, ahead of the RV spring ramp. Campaigns 4 and 5 alongside at lower volume, to test whether HR or owners reply better. Campaign 6 only if Mathew confirms construction and events.

- **Lead handling:** interested replies get the booking link. Replies from people looking for work are classified separately and pointed to the GigWorx worker app (link to confirm with Mathew).
