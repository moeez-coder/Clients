## Qualifier
The `Qualify` AI column uses `google/gemini-3.8-flash` with web access and JSON output. It checks the company type, the Michiana site and the exclusions, then the campaign signal:
> 3+ open hourly roles at the Michiana site in the last 60 days, at least 1 open 30+ days or reposted. Returns role_title, posted_month, open_roles_count, job_url.

`fit` is true only when every rule holds and the signal is verified. Rows with no company were skipped (`not run: missing company`).
**Result: 0 of 25 fit.** `status` = Review (fit) or Excluded. The CSV holds every qualifier field except the agent's step log.

## Columns
`linkedin_url` (key) and the standard GetLeads fields, then `qualify_*` (the AI output), `status`, and `gtm_row_id` (the row `_id` in the GTM Sheets table).
