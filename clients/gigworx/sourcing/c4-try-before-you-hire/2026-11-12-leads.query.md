## Qualifier
The `Qualify` AI column uses `google/gemini-3.8-flash` with web access and JSON output. It checks the company type, the Michiana site and the exclusions, then the campaign signal:
> 5+ open hourly roles at a 51+ employee Michiana site in the last 60 days. Returns open_roles_count, role_title, job_url.

`fit` is true only when every rule holds and the signal is verified. Rows with no company were skipped (`not run: missing company`).
**Result: 3 of 25 fit.** `status` = Review (fit) or Excluded. The CSV holds every qualifier field except the agent's step log.
