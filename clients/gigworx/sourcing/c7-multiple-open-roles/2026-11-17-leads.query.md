**Companies:** the 236 ICP-fit companies with 2+ live Indeed roles posted in the last 6 months (`../tam/2026-11-17-c7-indeed-180d-companies.csv`, `icp_fit = yes`, `distinct_roles >= 2`).

## Personas (GigWorx ICP, from the onboarding form and ICP configs)
Seniority goes **down to Manager and Supervisor on purpose**. GigWorx's form names Production Supervisor and Operations Manager, and the person who books shift labour is usually at the site, not at HQ. So `title_check` "fail" (the director+ guard) is expected for most rows.

Ranked within each company (column `persona`):
1. **Site ops lead.** By vertical:
   - manufacturing: Plant / Production / Operations / General / Manufacturing Manager, Plant Superintendent
   - warehousing: Warehouse / Distribution Center / Site / Terminal Manager
   - retail: Store Manager / Director / Leader, District Manager
   - hospitality: General Manager, Restaurant GM, F&B, Kitchen Manager, Executive Chef, Housekeeping, Banquet, Catering, Event Manager
2. **HR:** HR Manager / Business Partner / Director / Generalist, Talent Acquisition Manager.
3. **Owner/exec:** Owner, President, CEO, COO, Founder, VP or Director of Operations.
4. **Supervisor/lead:** Production / Shift / Warehouse Supervisor, Assistant Store Manager, team leads.

**Cap:** 10 per company, filled in persona order (people found by both tools first within a rank). This follows the TAM/SAM inverse rule: 236 companies is a small-to-medium SAM, so go deep per company.

**Location:** the person must be in Indiana (pass 1: in one of the Michiana towns; pass 2: Indiana or Michigan). National chains (Walmart, Kohl's ...) only get people at their Michiana stores and plants, never HQ staff.

## Pull
1. **Company LinkedIn pages:** 130 already known. 70 came via Blitz `POST /v2/enrichment/domain-to-linkedin`. 24 came via Blitz `POST /v2/search/companies` `name.include`; 5 of those were wrong matches (IL&FS India, TRAMEC Italy, an antique mall, Villa International Atlanta, a home-health firm) and were cleared. 12 were unresolved.
2. **Blitz** `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword`, one call per company, `max_results: 10`, a 4-tier cascade per vertical (titles in `people_blitz.py`), `exclude_title` for sales, marketing, finance, IT, engineering, quality and safety.
   - Pass 1: person `location.city.include` set to the 10 Michiana towns.
   - Pass 2: no town filter, for non-national employers where pass 1 found fewer than 3.
   - **State check:** Blitz puts the state in `location.state_code`, and its city filter matches same-named towns elsewhere. 86 pass-1 people were in Bristol CT/TN/VA/PA/RI, Goshen OH, Osceola WI and similar, and were dropped. Kept: `state_code` IN (pass 1); IN or MI (pass 2).
3. **Clay** (MCP `search-contacts`, 16 batches of 10 companies, run by subagents) for the 159 companies with fewer than 3 Blitz contacts:
   `location_state = "Indiana" and location_city in (17 Michiana towns) and experiences.any(is_current = true and job_title is_similar_to (persona titles)) limit 10 by clay_company_id`.
   It returned 166 people. The Clay **REST API** could not be used: the workspace is at 999,988 of its 1,000,000 annual results (resets 2027-01-01).
4. **Merge:** dedupe on LinkedIn URL (`sources` lists every tool that found the person). A persona title regex (`merge_people.py`) dropped 39 off-persona titles (VP Marketing, VP Finance, accounting, engineering managers ...). Cap 10 per company.
5. Written through `python tools/leads.py normalize ... --source "blitz+clay"`: 473 in, 2 collapsed, **471 rows**. Extra columns: `persona`, `sources`, `signal_tier`, `hourly_roles_6mo`, `open_roles_6mo`, person city and state.
