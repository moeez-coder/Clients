## Inserted after paragraph 131 (tracked suggestion 1)


Campaign 6: Recruitment Specific (Open Roles)

ICP
Market: United States, Eastern and Central time zones
Segment: Manufacturers and fire & life safety companies with 201 to 1,000 employees that are hiring for a leadership or specialist management role right now: operations and plant leadership, engineering and quality, supply chain and purchasing, finance and HR leadership, and fire & life safety management roles.
Angle: The company has already told the market what it needs. We offer a candidate for that exact role, and the conversation about how GRN Piedmont works follows from there.
How it differs from Campaign 1: Campaign 1 asks for a conversation about a role that has been open a long time. Campaign 6 offers a candidate for any qualifying role posted in the last 120 days. A company is only ever in one of the two.

Persona
The executive the open role reports to: COO, VP Operations, VP Manufacturing, VP Engineering, VP Quality, VP Supply Chain or Chief Procurement Officer, depending on the role. If none is found: CEO, President or Owner, then CHRO or VP Human Resources. No Managers or Directors.

Signal and data
Signal: a job post for one of the roles above, published in the last 120 days, at a company matching the size, industry and geography.
Source: live job postings from the last 120 days, read from a jobs database that is refreshed daily.
Companies matching the profile: about 22,400 manufacturers with 201 to 1,000 employees and a head office in the Eastern or Central United States.
Companies showing the signal: 4,426 matching job posts in the last 120 days. After removing duplicates and posts that are not a fit (for example retail, hotel, marketing and IT roles, and general contractors in the fire & life safety search), 1,161 companies have a qualifying open role. 25 of those are fire & life safety companies.
Reachable contacts: we found 1,563 executives at 915 of those companies, all at C-level, VP or Owner level.
Why the small fire & life safety pool is fine: it runs inside this campaign as a small list with up to 4 contacts per company. Campaign 3 already covers every fire & life safety company whether or not it is hiring.

Lead list
Source: companies with a live job post in the last 120 days matching the roles above.
Volume: 2 contacts per company for manufacturers, because the pool is large. Up to 4 per company for fire & life safety, because the pool is small.
Per lead we capture: the open role title, and a short phrase from the job post saying what the role needs.
Filtered out: hourly, technician, entry-level, sales and internship roles, posts by agencies, car dealerships, retailers, government bodies, other GRN Network offices and your Do-Not-Contact list.

Messaging
This campaign leads with Offer 1 below: a candidate profile for the open role. Connection request with no note.
Message 1: Hey {FIRST_NAME}, thanks for connecting.
Saw {COMPANY} has a {Job_Title_Open_} role listed, looks like you need someone with {Job_Description}.
Not sure if that's a fresh one or something you've had open for a bit, but either way I have a candidate who fits that pretty closely.
Want me to send their profile over so you can take a look?
Message 2 (2 days later): Hey {FIRST_NAME}, following up on this, didn't want it to get buried in your inbox.
If the {Job_Title_Open_} role is still open, happy to send over a candidate or two who could be a fit.
Worth a quick look, even just to compare against who's already in your pipeline?
Message 3 (2 days later): No worries if the timing's off, {FIRST_NAME}.
Roles like this sometimes sit for a while or get filled quietly, so if it's still open I'll send a profile over, and if not, no hard feelings.
Just let me know either way.
{Job_Title_Open_} is the open role, for example "Plant Manager". {Job_Description} is a short phrase from the job post, for example "experience running a multi-shift stamping plant".
When a prospect says yes: the reply goes to Harvey the same day, and GRN Piedmont chooses and sends the candidate profile.

What it costs you: a recruiter's time to match a candidate you already know to the role and send the profile. It works best in role groups where you have candidates on hand.
