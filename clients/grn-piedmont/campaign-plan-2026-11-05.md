# GRN Piedmont Campaign Ideas

Executive search for mid-sized manufacturers and fire & life safety companies. Five signal-led LinkedIn campaigns. Each one opens with something concrete tied to the prospect's own situation: a seat that has sat open, a new site, an acquisition, or the question of whether they would rehire last year's hires. Each one offers something useful before any call, so the prospect can judge GRN Piedmont's method first.

## Shared across all five

**Market:** United States, Eastern and Central time zones

**Segment:** Mid-sized manufacturers (industrial, discrete and process) and fire & life safety companies (fire sprinkler and suppression contractors, fire alarm and detection integrators, inspection, testing and maintenance providers) with 201 to 1,000 employees

**Persona, primary:** Owner, Founder, President, CEO, COO

**Persona, secondary:** CFO, CHRO, Chief People Officer, VP Operations, VP Manufacturing, VP Human Resources, VP People

**Contacts per company:** Fire & life safety is a narrow market, so up to 3 or 4 contacts per company. Manufacturing is a wide market, so 1 or 2 contacts per company (the top executive, plus the COO or VP Operations when the open role sits in operations).

**Exclusions:**

- Managers and Directors (client instruction), plus coordinators, specialists, recruiters, talent acquisition staff and assistants
- Recruiting firms, HR consultancies and HR technology vendors
- Government and public sector organisations (not requested on the onboarding form)
- Companies under 201 or over 1,000 employees, and anything outside the Eastern and Central US time zones
- GRN Piedmont's Do-Not-Contact list (uploaded on the onboarding form; to be loaded before launch), existing GRN Piedmont clients, and any companies another GRN Network office already works with. Suppression is refreshed right before launch.

**Language and tone:** English. Consultative, warm and plain. We never use the word "staffing", never lead with speed, and never make guarantees. "We" for the firm and its method, "I" for the ask.

**Channel:** LinkedIn. Connection request with no note, then Message 1 on acceptance, Message 2 on day 4, Message 3 on day 9.

## Campaign 1: The Seat That Won't Close

### ICP

**Market:** United States, Eastern and Central time zones

**Segment:** Manufacturers and fire & life safety companies, 201 to 1,000 employees, with a senior or hard-to-fill role that has been open for a long time

**Angle:** A leadership or specialist seat that sits open for 60 days, or gets reposted, usually means the right people are not applying. The strongest candidates are performing well somewhere else and are not reading job boards. Every month the seat is open, the work lands on someone else.

### Persona

Owner, President, CEO first. COO or VP Operations when the open role is in operations, engineering, purchasing or supply chain. CHRO or VP Human Resources as a secondary contact.

### Lead list

**Custom qualifiers:** Claygent checks the company's own postings on its careers page, LinkedIn and Indeed for senior or specialist roles in the functions GRN Piedmont covers: executive management, plant or general management, operations, engineering, quality, purchasing, supply chain, finance leadership and HR leadership. Exclude hourly, technician, entry-level and internship roles, and postings by agencies.

**Signal (Claygent-verified, gates inclusion):** at least one qualifying role posted 60 or more days ago, or reposted under the same title in the last 90 days. Claygent returns {role_title} for the oldest qualifying role, {posted_month}, and the posting URL.

**Lead magnet:** A one-page draft position profile for their open role: what the hire must achieve in the first year, written as measurable goals. It is the first step of GRN Piedmont's method, and it is useful to the prospect whether or not they hire GRN.

### Messaging

**Message 1:** Hello {first_name}, saw {company} has had the {role_title} role open since {posted_month}.

When a seat like that sits open, the right people usually aren't applying. They're doing the job well somewhere else.

We start every search by defining what the hire must deliver in year one. Want me to draft a one-page profile like that for your {role_title} role?

**Message 2:** No worries if that one is moving now, {first_name}.

The reason we start there: over 90% of the candidates we present get an interview, and eight of our searches this year were filled with the first and only candidate we presented.

**Message 3:** I'll leave it here, {first_name}.

If {role_title} is still open next month, happy to send that profile over.

**CTA variants to test in Message 1:**

- Shall I send one over?
- Is {role_title} still a priority, or is it close to filled? (question only, no offer)
- Worth comparing notes on what that hire needs to deliver?

## Campaign 2: New Site, New Leaders

### ICP

**Market:** United States, Eastern and Central time zones

**Segment:** Manufacturers and fire & life safety companies, 201 to 1,000 employees, going through a visible change: a new plant, facility or branch; an acquisition or change of ownership; or an ERP or operations systems rollout

**Angle:** New sites, acquisitions and systems rollouts all create leadership seats that did not exist before, and they need people who have already done that exact job. A mis-hire at that stage costs a year.

### Persona

Owner, President, CEO, COO. Secondary: VP Operations, VP Manufacturing, CFO (for acquisitions and systems rollouts).

### Lead list

**Custom qualifiers:** Claygent checks news, press releases, state economic development announcements and the company's own site for one of: a new plant, facility, branch or expansion; an acquisition, merger or new ownership; or an ERP, MES or operations systems implementation. The event must be in the last 9 months.

**Signal (Claygent-verified, gates inclusion):** one qualifying event with a source URL. Claygent returns {change_type} (new site, acquisition or systems rollout), {change_short} (a plain 3 to 8 word description, for example "the new plant in Greenville"), {change_month}, and the source URL.

**Lead magnet:** The Complimentary Hiring Process Effectiveness Review: a look at how the company defines a good hire, interviews, and decides, with practical recommendations. It is pitched here as preparation for the leadership hires the change will need.

### Messaging

**Message 1:** Hello {first_name}, congrats on {change_short}.

Changes like that usually create one or two leadership seats that need someone who has done it before, and those people are rarely job hunting.

We offer a complimentary review of how a company defines, interviews for and decides on leadership hires. Would that be useful before the new roles open?

**Message 2:** Happy to keep it light, {first_name}.

The way we work: before searching, we write down what the hire must achieve in the first year, then go after people already delivering it elsewhere. Eight of our searches this year were filled by the first candidate we presented.

**Message 3:** I'll leave it here, {first_name}.

If a leadership seat opens up as {change_short} comes together, happy to help.

**CTA variants to test in Message 1:**

- Worth a 20-minute look?
- Which role do you expect to be the hardest to fill? (question only, no offer)

## Campaign 3: Fire & Life Safety Leaders

### ICP

**Market:** United States, Eastern and Central time zones

**Segment:** Fire & life safety companies with 201 to 1,000 employees: fire sprinkler and suppression contractors, fire alarm and detection integrators, and inspection, testing and maintenance providers. Every qualifying company is covered, since this market is small.

**Angle:** Fire & life safety leaders are hard to find because the pool is small and nearly everyone good is already employed, often by a competitor. GRN Piedmont has worked this industry for years, and has the results to show it.

### Persona

Owner, President, CEO, COO, VP Operations, CHRO or VP Human Resources, CFO. Up to 3 or 4 contacts per company.

### Lead list

**Custom qualifiers:** Claygent confirms from the company's website and LinkedIn that its core business is fire protection, fire alarm, suppression, or inspection, testing and maintenance (not a general contractor or security-only company), and that it operates its own field teams. Branches of national platforms are included only if the branch has its own leadership team on LinkedIn.

**Signal (Claygent-verified, optional, sets the message variant):** an open senior or specialist role (as in Campaign 1), or an acquisition or new branch in the last 12 months (as in Campaign 2). Leads with no signal get the base version of Message 1.

**Lead magnet:** A one-page draft position profile for their open role (if there is one), or the Complimentary Hiring Process Effectiveness Review (if there isn't).

### Messaging

**Message 1 (base):** Hello {first_name}, we've spent years recruiting leaders for fire & life safety companies.

On one recent engagement, we searched 380 people, presented 10, and 8 were hired.

Who would you call today if a key leader at {company} gave notice tomorrow?

**Message 1 (open role):** Hello {first_name}, saw {company} is looking for a {role_title}.

Good fire & life safety leaders are almost all employed, so the right one probably isn't applying. On one recent engagement, we searched 380 people, presented 10, and 8 were hired.

Want me to draft a one-page first-year profile for the {role_title} role?

**Message 2:** No worries either way, {first_name}.

What makes the difference is the order we work in: we define what the hire must achieve in year one, then go after the people already delivering it. 85% of our work comes from repeat clients.

**Message 3:** I'll leave it here, {first_name}.

If a leadership seat opens up, or you want a second opinion on how you hire for one, happy to help.

**CTA variants to test in Message 1:**

- Worth comparing notes on how you hire leaders today?
- Is there a seat you already know will be hard to fill? (question only, no offer)

## Campaign 4: The Rehire Question

### ICP

**Market:** United States, Eastern and Central time zones

**Segment:** Manufacturers and fire & life safety companies with 201 to 1,000 employees and a senior HR leader. No signal is required: this campaign is for companies without an open role.

**Angle:** Ask the question GRN Piedmont is known for. Most HR leaders know which of last year's hires they would not rehire, and why. That gap is a hiring process problem, not a candidate problem.

### Persona

CHRO, Chief People Officer, VP Human Resources, VP People. Also the most senior HR person at companies with no VP-level HR leader, if the client approves (see Before launch).

### Lead list

**Custom qualifiers:** The company has a senior HR leader on LinkedIn and no open in-house recruiter or talent acquisition role (if it is hiring a recruiter, it goes to Campaign 1 instead). Exclude anyone who has been in role under 3 months.

**Signal:** None. Claygent returns {industry_short} ("manufacturing" or "fire & life safety").

**Lead magnet:** None in Message 1, which asks the signature question. The Complimentary Hiring Process Effectiveness Review is offered in Message 2, once they have answered or engaged.

### Messaging

**Message 1:** Hello {first_name}, a question we ask every {industry_short} leader we work with:

Of the people {company} hired over the past year, how many would you enthusiastically rehire?

**Message 2:** No need to share the number, {first_name}.

When the answer is "fewer than we'd like", it is usually the process, not the candidates: how success is defined, how people are interviewed, how the decision gets made.

We offer a complimentary review of exactly that, with practical recommendations. Worth a look?

**Message 3:** I'll leave it here, {first_name}.

If a leadership hire comes up and you want a second opinion on the process, happy to help.

**CTA variants to test in Message 2:**

- Worth a 20-minute look?
- Would it help to see how we structure the first-year profile? (offer the profile instead of the review)

## Campaign 5: Owner Succession

### ICP

**Market:** United States, Eastern and Central time zones

**Segment:** Founder-led or family-owned manufacturers with 201 to 1,000 employees, where the Owner, Founder or President has been in the top seat for 15 or more years

**Angle:** Many long-tenured owners know who would run operations if their key leader left tomorrow, and many do not. Succession planning is the leadership hire nobody posts a job for. GRN Piedmont's approach of defining what a successor must deliver fits this conversation.

### Persona

Owner, Founder, President, CEO only.

### Lead list

**Custom qualifiers:** The company's LinkedIn page, website or state records show it is privately held and founder-led or family-owned (exclude PE-backed and public companies). The current Owner, Founder, President or CEO has been in that seat for 15 or more years according to LinkedIn.

**Signal (Claygent-verified, gates inclusion):** ownership type and the leader's tenure. Claygent returns {years_in_role} and {ownership} ("family-owned" or "founder-led").

**Lead magnet:** The Complimentary Hiring Process Effectiveness Review, framed around succession planning for the top two or three roles.

### Messaging

**Message 1:** Hello {first_name}, {years_in_role} years leading {company} is a real run.

A question I ask {ownership} manufacturers: if a key leader gave notice tomorrow, would you know who you'd call?

**Message 2:** Happy to keep it light, {first_name}.

A lot of our work is succession planning: defining what the next operations or plant leader must deliver, then quietly finding people already doing it elsewhere. Those people are rarely looking.

We offer a complimentary review of how a company plans and hires for its key seats. Worth a look?

**Message 3:** I'll leave it here, {first_name}.

If succession comes onto the agenda, happy to compare notes.

**CTA variants to test in Message 2:**

- Worth a 20-minute conversation?
- Which seat would be the hardest to replace today? (question only, no offer)

## Before launch

- **Do-Not-Contact list must be loaded.** The file was uploaded on the onboarding form but is not yet in the system. Nothing launches until it is loaded and existing GRN Piedmont clients are added.
- **Position profile turnaround needs confirming by Harvey.** Campaigns 1 and 3 offer a one-page first-year position profile. GRN Piedmont needs to commit to sending one within a few business days whenever a prospect says yes. If that isn't possible, those campaigns switch to their question-only CTA or the Hiring Process Effectiveness Review.
- **Senior HR titles.** The client asked us to avoid Directors. At companies under about 500 employees, the most senior HR person is often an HR Director. Campaign 4 only reaches them if Harvey approves that exception.
- **GRN Network conflicts.** We need to know whether other GRN offices' clients or territories should be excluded.
- **Claims:** every message stays within the stats from the onboarding form: over 90% presentation-to-interview ratio, eight single-candidate hires in 2026, 85% repeat business, the fire & life safety engagement (380 searched, 10 presented, 8 hired), GSA contract holder. No client names, pricing, fee structure, speed promises or guarantees in the messages. The "eight vs nine" single-candidate figure needs confirming; we use eight until then.
- **Launch order:** Campaigns 1 and 3 first, as their signals point most directly at a current need and Campaign 3 carries the strongest proof. Campaigns 2, 4 and 5 run alongside to test which angle replies best.
- **Senders:** five client LinkedIn seats and five virtual SDR seats are being set up. Campaign 4 and 5 copy uses "I" and suits Harvey's own profile best; we'd like to confirm which campaigns run from whose account.
- **Lead handling:** interested replies go to Harvey through the agreed Slack channel and booking link. Candidate and job-seeker replies are passed to GRN Piedmont separately.
