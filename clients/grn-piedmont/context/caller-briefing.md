GRN Piedmont — grnpiedmont.com

An executive search firm that finds and places senior leaders and hard-to-fill specialists for manufacturing and fire & life safety companies across the US.

WHO THEY SELL TO
CEOs, Presidents, Owners, COOs, CFOs and CHROs at mid-sized manufacturers, industrial firms and fire protection companies, roughly **200–1,000 employees**, in the Eastern and Central time zones. Not managers or directors.

WHAT THEY SELL
- Retained executive search: they go out and find leaders and specialists who are already doing the job well somewhere else and are not applying to ads
- **Performance-based hiring**: before searching, they build a profile of what the hire must actually accomplish in year one, with measurable goals, then interview and score every candidate against it
- **Complimentary Hiring Process Effectiveness Review** — a free look at how the company defines a good hire, interviews, and decides, with practical fixes

WHY A PROSPECT TAKES THE CALL
A leadership or specialist seat has been open for months, the resumes coming in are wrong, and every bad hire costs them a year. Or they have no internal recruiter and are growing, opening a plant, or losing people to retirement.

Worth knowing: **over 90% of candidates they present get an interview**, and in 2026 eight roles were filled after presenting a single candidate each. On one fire and life safety client they screened 380 people, presented ten, placed eight. 85% repeat business over 15 years. GSA contract holder. Nearly 15 years in business, based in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina.
