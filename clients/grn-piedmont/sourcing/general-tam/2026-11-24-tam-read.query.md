## 1. Quality check of the Blitz TAM (`qc_tam.py` -> `2026-11-24-companies-qc.csv`, no API calls)

**Biggest problem: half the file has no HQ state and much of that half is not American.**
- 12,510 of 22,631 rows (55%) have no HQ state in Blitz. Blitz tags them `country_code: US` with no state and no city.
  - A 75-record probe confirmed this: Blitz returns US-tagged, state-less records under the same filter.
  - So the HQ-state exclusion could not drop them, and the ET/CT time-zone rule was never checked for them.
- Sampled no-state rows were Chinese, Hungarian, Iranian, Indian, Malaysian, Georgian and Belarusian companies.
- 4,885 rows carry a foreign signal: a country-code domain (.cn, .in, .tr, .br, ...), a foreign legal suffix (Co., Ltd, Pvt, GmbH, Sdn Bhd, Ltda, ...) or a non-Latin name.
- The rest of the no-state rows are thin pages: median 6 employees on LinkedIn, against 85 for rows that have a state and a description.

**Noise by industry label.** Read from a keyword screen of every description plus three eyeballed random samples (40 verified-state rows, 40 core, 25 review):
- **Motor Vehicle Manufacturing (2,740 rows) is mostly car dealerships and repair shops:** Miller Auto Group, Bill Dodge, Andrew Chevrolet, Eric's Car Care.
  - Only 29% of its rows describe manufacturing, the lowest share of the large labels.
  - Rule `dealer/auto service` catches 995 of them.
- **Other noise** (each with its own rule; every row keeps its reason in `qc_reason`):
  - benefit plans and union locals: "... Welfare Benefit Plan", "Paperworkers Union Local 675"
  - student chapters, schools and hospitals filed under food or chemical labels
  - wholesale distributors: M.S. Walker wine, F&S Fresh Foods
  - government agencies: DARPA
- Labels with the cleanest descriptions (60 to 70% with a manufacturing signal):
  - Packaging and Containers
  - Industrial Automation
  - Machinery
  - Fabricated Metal
  - Plastics
- The noisiest labels are Motor Vehicle Manufacturing, Furniture, Beverage, Textile and Agriculture/Construction/Mining Machinery, each with 20% or more rows showing no manufacturing signal.

**Precision by tier (eyeball):**
- core: 34 of 40 fit. The misses were a hospital, a school, a Colombian co-op, a linen service, a meal-delivery firm and Enterprise's holding company.
- review: about 10 of 25 fit. These are mostly real small or sub-unit plants plus leftover dealers.
- **False-drop check:** of the 1,204 companies in the live MPC campaign (known real, they posted jobs), 40 landed in drop.
  - The first rule set dropped 162. Softened rules now send a noise hit with a verified state and 30+ on LinkedIn to review, not drop.

**F&LS precision (264 rows)** (`qc_fls`):

- 88 MEP contractors with a fire division: The Hill Group, Gutridge.
- 28 adjacent (security, low voltage).
- 74 not F&LS: general contractors, waterproofing, volunteer fire departments, AFSA/CIBSE chapters.
- Precision is 28% specialists, or 61% if multi-trade contractors with a fire division count.

**Why they differ:**
- **Blitz raw is inflated** by state-less, foreign and ghost pages (see QC). Its company size is the LinkedIn self-declared band, so a page with 6 members can still say "201-500".
- **AI Ark matches any office location, not HQ.** It cannot apply the ET/CT HQ rule exactly, so 4,630 slightly overstates the ET/CT count. Its industries are its own 919-value taxonomy (23 manufacturing values mapped, match mode STRICT). It agrees with the Blitz core tier within 5%.
- **DiscoLike classifies by website into 53 broad categories.** A manufacturer can sit in AUTOMOTIVE, CONSUMER_PRODUCTS or FOOD_AND_BEVERAGE instead of MANUFACTURING, so its MANUFACTURING count is a floor. The union of adjacent categories (about 7,800, with overlap) is the ceiling.
- **Clay** (MCP `search-companies`, 12 manufacturing labels, LinkedIn size 201-500 / 501-1,000, HQ in a US state outside the 13 excluded) returns no total, so it gives a fit and recall check, not a size. See `2026-11-24-clay-sample-companies.csv`.
  - First page: 20 companies, 13 fit, 4 borderline, 3 not fit (DTC pet food, consumer subscription, a reseller).
  - **18 of the 20 are already in the Blitz TAM** (17 in core). Blitz recall on established manufacturers is high; the problem is precision, not recall.
- **Conclusion:** the real manufacturing TAM is about **6,000 to 6,500 companies**, and the core tier is the defensible SAM to work first.
  - Blitz counts are indicative (CLAUDE.md warning). Every tier number above comes from the saved file, not from `total_results`.
