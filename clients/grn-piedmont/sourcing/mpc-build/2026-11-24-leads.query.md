## Steps (scripts in this folder; raw files in the session scratchpad, not committed)

1. **`pull_segment_leads.py`**
   - Input: every non-F&LS company in `../tam/2026-11-24-mpc-segments-jobs.csv` (1,256 companies).
   - Each company goes to one segment: its family with the most kept postings, with ties going to the family of its newest posting.
   - The open role is the newest kept posting in that family.
   - Blitz API `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword` per company:
     - tiers: tier 1 is the executive over the family (`spec.py` `TIER1`; EHS uses the operations tier), tier 2 is CEO/President/Owner, tier 3 is CHRO/VP HR;
     - `exclude_title`: Manager, Director, Assistant, Coordinator, Recruiter, Former, Sales, Retired, Intern;
     - location US;
     - `max_results` 5 at net-new companies and 10 at companies already in the live campaign (to get past the people already contacted).
   - **2,415 records.**
   - Anyone already in `../recruitment-specific-open-roles/2026-11-16-leads.csv` or `-11-23-leads.csv` is marked `removed: already in 734516/756466`. The match is on LinkedIn URL or first+last+company.
   - A person returned for two companies is kept once.
2. **`fetch_jds.py`**
   - For each company with a launch lead (193), read the chosen posting on the public LinkedIn posting page.
   - If it is closed, try up to 3 other kept postings in the same family, newest first, and use the first open one.
   - Result: 99 open, 94 closed. Closed ones stay in, as Moe instructed on the live campaign (2026-11-16, "run on the closed ones as well"), and `posting_open` shows which.
   - 3 companies switched to another posting.
   - 5 companies have a role first posted 60+ days ago and reposted in the last 30 days; they get the repost `open_line`.
3. **Phrases:** written with Moe's prompt (`../recruitment-specific-open-roles/jd_phrase_prompt.md`) by 4 Claude sub-agents.
   - 192 were written from the full description and 1 from the Blitz summary.
   - Reviewing the rows they flagged removed 4 companies outside the ICP: Big Dan's Car Wash, Arnold Oil Company (fuel distributor), Dobbs Equipment (equipment dealer), and Continental Battery Systems (the role is sales/account management).
4. **`build_segments.py`** writes each `../mpc-<segment>/` folder. Leads then go through `tools/leads.py normalize --source blitz`.
5. **`sequence.py`** holds the HeyReach sequence per segment. It is the live 734516/756466 copy, with the role-family slot in messages 2 and 3 and `{Open_Line}` in message 1.
