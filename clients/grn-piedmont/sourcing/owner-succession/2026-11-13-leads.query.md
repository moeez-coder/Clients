## Steps

### 1. Companies

- **Tool:** `gtm_sheet_find_companies`, Blitz.
- **Filters:**
  - `countries` US
  - `sizes` 201-500 and 501-1000
  - `industries`: Manufacturing, Industrial Machinery Manufacturing, Machinery Manufacturing, Chemical Manufacturing, Plastics Manufacturing
  - No states filter, per the repo owner's instruction.
- **Result:** 8,728 companies, imported into table **1. Companies** (`2079087b-…`). About 8,728 Blitz credits.

### 2. AI yes/no check

- **Column:** "Owner Succession Fit (AI)".
- **Model:** `openai/gpt-6-luna` with web access. The repo owner chose Luna.
- **Output:** a JSON schema whose `qualifies` field can only be `"yes"` or `"no"`. It also returns `ownership`, `leader_name`, `leader_title`, `years_in_role` and `reason`.
- **A company gets "yes" only if:**
  - it is privately held, founder-led or family-owned (not public, PE-backed or a subsidiary);
  - it actually manufactures;
  - an owner, founder or family member has held the top seat for 15+ years, or tenure can't be found but ownership clearly qualifies.
- **Prompt fix after the 10-row test:** leader and years must describe the same qualifying owner. Daniel Defense had first returned the newer CEO's 3 years; it now returns Marty Daniel at 25.
- Rows 1–6 and 8–10 were scored with the first prompt. All 9 were clear "no"s: public, PE-backed or subsidiaries.
- **Result:** 508 "yes", 8,220 "no".
  - All rows: `2026-11-13-all-companies-ai-check.csv`
  - The 508 "yes" companies were sent to table **2. Qualified Companies** (`78d86371-…`) and saved as `2026-11-13-companies.csv`.

### 3. Leads

- **Tool:** `gtm_sheet_find_people`, Blitz, `companies_from_table` = 2. Qualified Companies.
- **Filters:**
  - Person in the US.
  - Titles include Owner, Founder, Co-Founder, President, CEO, Chief Executive Officer, Chairman, Executive Chairman.
  - Titles exclude Vice President, VP, Assistant, Executive Assistant, Assistant to.
  - `per_company` 2 was requested, but Blitz ignores it, so some companies have more than 2.
- **Per-company depth (CLAUDE.md TAM rule):** manufacturing is a large TAM, so 1–2 top-seat contacts per company. The title filter keeps it to top-seat people only.
- **Result:** 310 people from 525 company lookups, in table **3. Leads** (`35453097-…`), about 310 Blitz credits.

### 4. Lead columns

- **Lookup:** each lead is matched back to 2. Qualified Companies by company LinkedIn URL.
- **`ownership`:** taken from the company check.
- **`years_in_role`:** filled only when the lead IS the qualifying leader (last name matches) and tenure is 15 or more years. That's 47 leads. Every other lead gets the HeyReach fallback Message 1, which has no tenure line.
- **`Status`:**
  - **303 "launch set".**
  - **7 removed for "not a top-seat title":** President & GM, Group COO, board or committee members, and one "Owner, Board of Directors" caught by the word-match rule.
- **Push to HeyReach:** wired to `730731` with custom fields `ownership` and `years_in_role`. Auto-run is off, `run_if` is Status = "launch set", and **nothing has been pushed**.

`2026-11-13-leads.csv` is keyed by `linkedin_url` (no duplicates). Empty cells are written as `-`.
