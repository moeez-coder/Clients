## 3. Making them live, and catching GetLeads' loose matches

- GetLeads rows carry a company domain but often not the company's LinkedIn URL. A second lookup, **Qualified Company (domain)**, matches `Company Domain` to 2. Qualified Companies `Domain`.
- Fallback columns:
  - field columns `ownership (domain)`, `Top Leader (domain)`, `Leader Years (domain)`, `Qualified Name (domain)`
  - merge column `ownership_final` (the LinkedIn-URL match first, then the domain match)
- `years_in_role` reads either match.
- **Push to HeyReach** now sends `ownership_final` as the `ownership` custom field. It is still auto-run off, with run_if Status = "launch set".
- **Problem found:** GetLeads tags many unrelated people with a qualified company's domain (hairdressers, farms, consultants, restaurants on e.g. traxxas.com, nordicware.com).
- **Status rule added:** a row that matched by domain only must also match by name. One of these must hold:
  - a word of the lead's company name appears in the qualified company's name;
  - a word of the lead's company name appears in the domain;
  - the domain root appears in the lead's company name.
  - Otherwise: `removed: works at a different company (domain match only)`.
- The title rule now also catches "Vice-President".
