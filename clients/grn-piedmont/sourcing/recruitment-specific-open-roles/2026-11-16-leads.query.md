## Steps and exact queries

1. `pull_jobs.py`: `POST /v2/jobs/search`, cursor-paged at 50, five role families. Exact request bodies: `jobs_request_bodies.json` (manufacturing bodies are sent as two industry halves, 35 + 33 labels). Filters: role-family title keywords with exclusions, US job location, `FULL_TIME`, last 120 days; company `is_agency: false`, sizes `201-500` + `501-1000`, US HQ, HQ state not Mountain/Pacific/AK/HI; fire & life safety adds 5 company keywords.
2. `build_companies.py` + `spec.py`: removes noise and picks **one open role per company** (its most recent kept posting). Every posting stays in `2026-11-16-jobs.csv` with a `status` (kept, or removed with the reason).
3. `pull_people.py`: `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword`, one call per kept company. Tier 1 = the executive over the role family, tier 2 = CEO / President / Owner / Founder (exact-match brackets), tier 3 = CHRO / VP HR. `exclude_title` includes Manager and Director (client instruction). US only. `max_results` 2 for manufacturing, 4 for fire & life safety. LinkedIn URLs only, no email enrichment.
4. `build_leads.py`, then `python tools/leads.py normalize raw/leads-raw.csv 2026-11-16-leads.csv --source blitz`.

`raw/` (API responses) is git-ignored; the three CSVs carry everything in it.
