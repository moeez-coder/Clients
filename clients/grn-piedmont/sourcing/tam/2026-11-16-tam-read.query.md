# TAM/SAM read for the Recruitment Specific (open role / MPC) campaign (2026-11-16)

- **What:** sizing of the open-role pool for GRN Piedmont, plus a small eyeball sample. Done before the campaign was added to the client doc (CLAUDE.md "Campaign doc standard").
- **Client:** GRN Piedmont, tracking_clients `3abc2243-889c-4177-a75a-3ac680766d20`.
- **Why:** Moe asked for a Recruitment Specific campaign on the Trillium Construction "Recruitment Specific Campaign" messaging (tracking_clients session `e75caebb-aebc-4bbe-857b-633e192c1ab6`), then set the signal window to **120 days**.
- **Session:** claude.ai/code session_013MzxQfShB3KmCf8R7naacT.

## Tools and exact queries

1. **Blitz API `POST /v2/search/companies`** and **`POST /v2/jobs/search`**, `max_results: 1`, reading `total_results`. Script `tam_counts.py`; every request body and total is in `tam_counts.json`.
   - Firmographics: 68 manufacturing industry labels (exact jobs-search enum values; sent as two halves because Blitz caps `industry.include` at 50), sizes `201-500` + `501-1000`, US HQ, HQ state not in the 13 Mountain/Pacific/AK/HI states.
   - "Automotive" (legacy label) left out: it is mostly car dealerships.
   - Fire & life safety: 8 contractor/security labels + company keywords `fire protection`, `fire sprinkler`, `fire alarm`, `fire suppression`, `life safety`.
   - Jobs: role-family title keywords, US job location, `FULL_TIME`, `is_agency: false`, posted in the last 30 / 90 / 120 days.
2. **Blitz sample** (`tam_sample.py`): the 50 newest 30-day postings per role family (`2026-11-16-jobs-sample.csv`, 250 rows) and `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword` on the first 3 companies per family (`2026-11-16-sample-leads.csv`, 36 rows, keyed by `linkedin_url`; one row is a `-` placeholder for a company with no match).
3. **Clay MCP `search-companies`**, two DSL queries, first page (20 rows) each, saved with a fit read in `2026-11-16-clay-sample-companies.csv`. Clay returns no total, so it is a fit check, not a size.
   - `select from companies where industry in (<11 manufacturing labels>) and company_size in ("201-500", "501-1,000") and locations.any(country_name = "United States" and is_headquarters = true) and jobs.exists(job_title contains ("Plant Manager", "Operations Manager", "Director of Operations", "Engineering Manager", "Quality Manager", "Supply Chain Manager", "Purchasing Manager") and job_still_open = true and job_posted_date >= today() - interval 30 days)`
   - `select from companies where company_size in ("201-500", "501-1,000") and locations.any(country_name = "United States" and is_headquarters = true) and description contains ("fire protection", "fire sprinkler", "fire alarm", "fire suppression") and jobs.exists(job_still_open = true and job_posted_date >= today() - interval 30 days and job_title contains ("Manager", "Director", "Vice President"))`

**Blitz records used:** about 400 in total (count runs 6 + 34 + 34 + 44, sample 285).

- **Postings per company:** 200 sampled manufacturing postings came from 151 companies (1.32). That gives roughly 500 to 650 hiring companies per 30 days and roughly 2,000 to 2,900 over 120 days before noise is removed. The exact count comes from the full pull.
- **Noise read (sample):** about 1 in 7 manufacturing postings is not a fit (hotel GM, tire retailer, AutoNation, a government contractor, "People/IT/Marketing Operations Manager"). Fire & life safety is much noisier: 9 of 24 sampled companies were true fire protection businesses, the rest general, mechanical or civil contractors. The pull therefore needs a company-name/title gate for F&LS and a title exclusion pass for manufacturing.
- **Clay fit check:** 14 of 20 manufacturing rows fit (noise: venture-backed and Pacific-HQ companies, a meal-delivery marketplace). 3 of 20 F&LS rows fit; a description keyword alone mostly returns MEP engineering design firms.
- **Persona reachable:** of 14 sampled companies, 13 returned 1 to 3 contacts at C-level, VP or Owner level with Manager and Director titles excluded. Tier 2 ("President", "Owner") also matched "Vice President of ..." and "Product Owner"; the full pull uses exact-match brackets for those.
- **Blitz counts are indicative** (CLAUDE.md warning).
