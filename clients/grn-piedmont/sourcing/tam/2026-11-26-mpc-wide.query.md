# GRN Piedmont: MPC company expansion to 51-5,000 employees (2026-11-26)

**Why:** Moe, 2026-11-26: "We need to expand the companies aswell the count of compaines should be around 1000 atleast", then, asked which lever, "add 51 - 5000".
This is **outside GRN's stated ICP** (201-1,000 employees). Moe made the call, the same way as the Director change on 2026-11-25.
It expands the five existing segments (`mpc-executive`, `mpc-operations-plant`, `mpc-engineering-quality`, `mpc-supply-chain-purchasing` and `mpc-ehs-maintenance`).
It does not open new segments.

## Query (`mpc_wide_pull.py`)
- **Endpoint:** Blitz API `POST /v2/jobs/search`, cursor-paged at 50.
- **Same as the 2026-11-24 pull (`mpc_segments_pull.py`):**
  - the 68 manufacturing labels, grouped in 10 verticals
  - the same two leadership-title chunks
  - a 120-day window
  - US HQ, excluding the 13 Mountain/Pacific/AK/HI states
  - `is_agency` false, US job location, FULL_TIME
  - the same title-noise, company-noise and DNC gates
  - one role family per posting
  - reposts collapsed to the oldest date
- **Only change:** `company.size.include` is `["51-200"]` and then `["1001-5000"]`, run as separate passes.
  - 201-1,000 was pulled on 11-24, so the bands are disjoint and no company is pulled twice.
- **Fire & life safety is not pulled.** It is folded into 730732.
- Request bodies, first-page `total_results` and records used are in `2026-11-26-mpc-wide-pull.json`. Blitz records used: 7,910.
