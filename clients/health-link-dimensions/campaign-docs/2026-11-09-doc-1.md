# HealthLink Dimensions Campaign Plan, Doc 1

Ten LinkedIn campaigns built from your ICP form. Campaigns 1 to 5 are open-role campaigns, one per audience. Each opens with a commercial, marketing or media role the company is hiring for right now, because that new hire usually inherits the HCP data on day one. Campaigns 6 to 10 reach the same five audiences through a different buying signal: a launch, a funding round, a device clearance, a new leader or a new agency account.

Across every campaign:

- **Geography:** United States.
- **Offer:** a free review of the prospect's HCP data. We compare a sample of their HCP list against yours and show what is out of date, missing or inconsistent.
- **Goal:** a qualified discovery call.
- **Proof points used:** monthly-verified provider emails covering 98% of U.S. MDs, DOs, NPs and PAs; 3.8M+ privacy-safe provider emails; 95% client retention and an NPS of 79; TrustArc TRUSTe certified, NAI member, SOC 2.
- **Excluded from every list:** your current clients and anything on your do-not-contact list. Please send us both before we build lists. We already know of AstraZeneca, AbbVie, Gilead, Takeda, Incyte, Bausch Health, Octapharma, Publicis Health Media, EVERSANA and Relevate Health.

## Campaign 1: Open Roles, Specialty Pharma and Commercial Biotech

### ICP

- **Market:** Life sciences.
- **Segment:** U.S. specialty pharma and commercial-stage biotech with 201 to 5,000 employees. Their products are prescribed by defined specialist groups, so they license HCP data for field and omnichannel promotion. Examples: Neurocrine, Jazz, Supernus, Axsome, Corcept, Insmed, Madrigal.
- **Signal:** a U.S. job posting in the last 30 days for one of these roles: Commercial Operations, Omnichannel, Marketing Analytics, Commercial Analytics, Marketing Operations, CRM, Customer Engagement, Brand Manager or Brand Director, or HCP Marketing.
- **Size:** about 3,090 companies match the firmographics, and about 80 of them post a matching role in any 90-day window.

### Persona

The leader the open role reports to: Director, Senior Director, VP or Head of Commercial Operations, Omnichannel, Marketing Analytics, Customer Engagement or Marketing, plus Brand Directors.

### Lead list

- **Source:** companies with live job posts that match the roles above.
- **Who we reach:** the hiring manager first, then up to 3 peers in commercial ops, omnichannel or marketing.
- **Volume:** 3 to 4 contacts per company. The pool is small, so we go deeper at each account.
- **Filtered out:** CROs, contract sales organizations and research-tools companies.
