## Offers by segment

Each segment has one guarantee offer and one free offer. The open-role campaigns (1 to 5) lead with the free offer, since a new hire wants a quick win. The trigger campaigns (6 to 10) lead with the guarantee, since a launch, funding round or new leader means a budget decision is coming. All terms below are proposals for your team to confirm.

### Pharma and biotech

- **Guarantee:** Reach more of the right prescribers in 90 days, or your next campaign is free.
- **Free offer:** A snapshot of your HCP audience: who you can reach, and who you are missing.

### Medical device

- **Guarantee:** Up-to-date surgeon and specialist data every month, or that month is free.
- **Free offer:** 100 of your records cleaned and updated.

### Healthcare agencies

- **Guarantee:** Better HCP engagement on your next client campaign than the industry average, or the next send is free.
- **Free offer:** An HCP audience plan you can take straight to your client.

### Future segments

- **Hospitals:** More physician referrals in 6 months, or your money back. Free offer: a referral gap report.
- **Health plans:** An accurate provider directory, guaranteed in writing. Free offer: a directory health check.
