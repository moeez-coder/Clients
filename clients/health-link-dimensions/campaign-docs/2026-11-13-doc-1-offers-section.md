## Potential offers and freebies (for your feedback)

These are potential offers and freebies only. None are final, and none will go into outreach until your team signs off. For each one, please comment: keep, change or drop. Add any other offers you think will pique your prospects' interest.

Our suggestion is that the open-role campaigns (1 to 5) lead with a freebie and the trigger campaigns (6 to 10) lead with a guarantee. We would like your view on that too.

### Pharma and biotech

- **Potential guarantee:** Reach more of the right prescribers in 90 days, or your next campaign is free.
- **Potential freebie:** A snapshot of your HCP audience: who you can reach, and who you are missing.

### Medical device

- **Potential guarantee:** Up-to-date surgeon and specialist data every month, or that month is free.
- **Potential freebie:** 100 of your records cleaned and updated.

### Healthcare agencies

- **Potential guarantee:** Better HCP engagement on your next client campaign than the industry average, or the next send is free.
- **Potential freebie:** An HCP audience plan you can take straight to your client.

### Future segments

- **Hospitals:** Potential guarantee: More physician referrals in 6 months, or your money back. Potential freebie: a referral gap report.
- **Health plans:** Potential guarantee: An accurate provider directory, guaranteed in writing. Potential freebie: a directory health check.
