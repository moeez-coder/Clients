# HealthLink Dimensions Campaign Plan, Doc 2

Twelve LinkedIn campaigns, rebuilt around the people we message rather than the type of company they work at. This plan replaces Doc 1. It is built from our 16 November call, Christopher's agency persona deck, Parris's pharma persona deck and Parris's tailored campaign draft.

How the plan is organised:

- **Each campaign is one persona.** Pharma has five (from Parris's deck), agencies have four (from Christopher's deck), and med device has two.
- **One campaign follows a brand event:** a new indication that moves a brand into a specialty it has not promoted to before. J&J Innovative Medicine and PTC Therapeutics are its first named accounts, from Parris's draft.
- **Seniority is a copy tier, not a separate campaign.** Each campaign reaches Influencers (C-suite, SVP, VP), Decision Makers (VP, MD, Director) and Advocates (Associate Director, Manager), with the copy adjusted for each.
- **Each campaign opens with one of two signals:** a role the company is hiring for in that function, or a recent event (a launch, approval, clearance, new leader or new client win).

Across every campaign:

- **Geography:** United States.
- **Offer:** the Provider Insight Report. We match the prospect's HCP list against our provider data and walk them through it live on a short call: providers who have retired or died and are still on the list, the digital IDs available for programmatic targeting, and the providers they are not reaching yet. The message teases the report and asks a soft yes. The report itself is shown on the call, not sent.
- **Call to action:** a short call. Please confirm whether you want a calendar link in the messages or a reply that our caller follows up on.
- **Goal:** a qualified discovery call, routed to Parris.
- **Proof points used:** monthly-verified provider emails covering 98% of U.S. MDs, DOs, NPs and PAs; 3.8M+ privacy-safe provider emails; 95% client retention and an NPS of 79; work with 11 of the 15 largest pharma companies; TrustArc TRUSTe certified, NAI member, SOC 2.
- **Copy rules from your persona decks:** show the method behind every number, keep the economics open, and never ask the prospect to take on extra work. No "trust us" language and no claims we cannot show on their own data.
- **Excluded from every list:** your current clients, the 199 accounts on your do-not-contact list, and the 12 named individuals on its second tab (their wider agencies stay open).
- **Staggered sends:** where a company appears in more than one campaign, we space the sends so it is never contacted by several campaigns at once.
- **Launch order:** we start with Campaign 1 (Brand Marketing) and Campaign 9 (Agency Leadership), the largest pharma and agency pools, then add the rest once reply rates are in. Sizes below were counted in November 2026 after removing your do-not-contact list and current clients: people are U.S.-based people with matching titles, and leads are what our per-company limits produce.
