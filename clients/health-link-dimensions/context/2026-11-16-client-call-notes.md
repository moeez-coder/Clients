# 2026-11-16 client call: offers and personas

## Decisions
- **Generic freebies are out** ("up-to-date surgeon data every month or that month is free" was rejected). HLD sells consultatively; offers must solve a persona's pain point.
- **Agency messaging is split by persona** (Christopher), not by vertical. His persona deck is in `context/agency-personas-cwm.md`.
- **Lead offer: the "Provider Insight Report"** (Meghan). The prospect's provider (MPI) file is matched to HLD's database, showing:
  - retired or deceased providers still on the list
  - digital IDs available for programmatic targeting
  - incremental providers they are not reaching
  - The turnaround is minutes.
- **Delivery:** the message teases the report and asks a soft yes. A warm caller then books the call, and the report is shown live on the call. It is not emailed, so it can't be misread and doesn't risk getting blocked.
- **Signals stay:** new hires and open roles. Parris asked twice whether open roles have worked for other clients; Moe said yes.
- **Senders:** branded SDR accounts ("Account Executive at HLD"). HLD can watch and reply via the portal. Leads are funnelled to Parris, who decides who takes each call.

## Persona notes from the call
- **Agency planning and activation** want strategy, new tactics and new audiences to impress a client, with less workload.
- **Agency investment / finance** want lower CPMs and cost reduction.
- **Agency measurement** want physician-level data (PLD) and proof of efficacy.
- **Pharma / med device (Parris):** a "complimentary feasibility analysis" against a specific clinical trial's inclusion criteria got a Sanofi meeting ahead of SCOPE. He wants messaging tailored to company and persona, and said doc 1 "seemed too general".
