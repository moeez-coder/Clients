## Slide 1: LEADERSHIP PERSONA

### Influencers / Sponsors / Recommenders (C-Suite / SVP / VP)

- **What they worry about:** Losing strategic relevance, margin erosion, being seen as execution-only, losing competitive positioning to other holding companies
- **What they want:** Enterprise-level differentiation, “strategic capability,” identity they can take into new business meetings
- **Psychological drivers:** Status, leadership presence, “visionary” identity, validation from peers
- **What success looks like:** Agency seen as smarter, modern, more innovative than other HoldCos
- **Internal pressures:** Pressure to modernize without overspending, pressure from clients to show innovation
- **YES triggers:** Messaging that positions HL as capability they own, not vendor they “use”
- **NO triggers:** Messaging that sounds like just another tactical vendor or audience provider

### Boat Rockers / Decision Makers / Assessors (VP / MD / Director)

- **What they worry about:** Taking political risk with a new partner, internal resistance, unclear ROI, methodology questions
- **What they want:** A defensible business case that justifies budget and identity decisions
- **Psychological drivers:** They want to be seen as the ones who made the smart strategic call
- **What success looks like:** The business case holds in front of CFO + analytics leadership
- **Internal pressures:** Pressure to show financial efficiency and defensible methodology
- **YES triggers:** Deterministic identity + measurable lift + transparent attribution
- **NO triggers:** Claims without methodology clarity, unclear data lineage

### Advocates (Associate Director / Manager)

- **What they worry about:** Not being taken seriously upward, being ignored in evaluation cycles, technology that creates more work
- **What they want:** A solution they can personally advocate for that makes them look informed
- **Psychological drivers:** Career advancement, visibility, internal recognition
- **What success looks like:** Leadership listens to them; they are pulled into decisions
- **Internal pressures:** Pressure to learn new tech fast and have a POV
- **YES triggers:** Easy talking points and “make-me-look-smart” explanations
- **NO triggers:** Platforms that are hard to explain or justify to senior leadership

## Slide 2: INVESTMENT PERSONA

### Influencers / Sponsors / Recommenders (C-Suite / SVP / VP)

- **What they worry about:** Budget risk, overspending, CPM inflation, lack of scaled reach
- **What they want:** Higher match rates, lower CPMs, scalable deterministic identity. Usability across their partner ecosystem.
- **Psychological drivers:** Being the deal-maker who improved yields
- **What success looks like:** Increased match rate without raising costs
- **Internal pressures:** Pressure from CFO-style procurement discipline
- **YES triggers:** Transparent economics, margin protection
- **NO triggers:** Anything that looks premium without effort justification

### Boat Rockers / Decision Makers / Assessors (VP / MD / Director)

- **What they worry about:** Platform fragmentation, inefficient supply paths, unclear performance value
- **What they want:** Clear total cost of ownership + identity clarity
- **Psychological drivers:** Being able to stand up to internal economic scrutiny
- **What success looks like:** Portfolio-wide efficiency gains
- **Internal pressures:** Pressure from holding company procurement rules
- **YES triggers:** Proven lift, scale, and transparent economics
- **NO triggers:** “Black box” pricing, platform friction

### Advocates (Associate Director / Manager)

- **What they worry about:** Complicated onboarding, day-to-day supply disruption
- **What they want:** Clean activation, easy-to-launch deals
- **Psychological drivers:** Simplicity and workflow happiness
- **What success looks like:** Fast time-to-launch
- **Internal pressures:** Pressure to operationalize quickly
- **YES triggers:** Tools that decrease workload
- **NO triggers:** Implementation friction

## Slide 3: Planning and Activation Teams

### Influencers / Sponsors / Recommenders (C-Suite / SVP / VP)

- **What they worry about:** Campaign waste, bad identity, unclear segments
- **What they want:** Precision targeting that “just works” everywhere
- **Psychological drivers:** Confidence in execution,  audience truth ease of use
- **What success looks like:** Lower waste, higher precision
- **Internal pressures:** Pressure from clients demanding innovative approaches and strategies

- **YES triggers:** Programmatic compatibility + DSP coverage, new offerings (email)

## Slide 4: Data and Analytics Persona

### Influencers / Sponsors / Recommenders (C-Suite / SVP / VP)

- **What they worry about:** Non-compliant data, unclear lineage, analytic blind spots
- **What they want:** Deterministic identity + strong compliance
- **Psychological drivers:** Technical intellectual legitimacy
- **What success looks like:** Proven incremental value
- **Internal pressures:** Pressure from security/compliance/legal
- **YES triggers:** Transparent methodology
- **NO triggers:** “Trust us” language without data validation

### Boat Rockers / Decision Makers / Assessors (VP / MD / Director)

- **What they worry about:** Attribution failure, noise in models, questionable causality
- **What they want:** Clear incremental lift with defensible methodology
- **Psychological drivers:** Being “the brain of the org”
- **What success looks like:** Attribution they can defend to the CFO or client
- **Internal pressures:** Pressure to validate every claim
- **YES triggers:** Deterministic truth + measurement standards
- **NO triggers:** Probabilistic with no transparency

### Advocates (Associate Director / Manager)

- **What they worry about:** Technical integration complexity
- **What they want:** Simple, documented data workflow
- **Psychological drivers:** Tools that don’t create work
- **What success looks like:** Easy enablement
- **Internal pressures:** Pressure from managers for enablement speed
- **YES triggers:** Straightforward data activation documentation
- **NO triggers:** Anything that makes activation harder
