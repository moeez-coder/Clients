Health Link Dimensions — healthlinkdimensions.com

They sell up-to-date contact details for doctors, nurses and other US healthcare professionals, and they clean up the contact lists companies already have.

WHO THEY SELL TO
Marketing, demand-gen, data and sales-ops leaders — plus recruiting leaders — at pharma, medical device, healthcare tech and healthcare staffing companies, hospital systems, and the marketing agencies that serve them.

WHAT THEY SELL
- **Healthcare provider contact data** — names, specialty, practice address, phone and email for licensed clinicians across the US, licensed for marketing, recruiting or research use
- Data cleaning and appending — they take the list you already have, correct the wrong records and fill in the missing emails and addresses
- Email campaigns sent to that audience on your behalf

WHY A PROSPECT TAKES THE CALL
Clinician contact details **go stale fast** — doctors move practice, change email, retire. That means bounced emails, returned mail and dead phone numbers, so budget gets burned and campaigns or recruiter outreach never reach the person they were built for.
