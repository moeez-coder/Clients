## What they do

HealthLink Dimensions is a healthcare data and marketing company that has supported life sciences, hospitals, payers, agencies, and recruiters with accurate, privacy-safe data and measurable marketing solutions for over 25 years. Its core asset is a verified database of healthcare provider (HCP) and facility information, which it describes as the largest database of its kind in the nation, audited by third-party verifier, BPA Worldwide. The database covers over 6 million healthcare providers, with specialties including prescribers (MDs, DOs, NPs, PAs), pharmacists, optometrists, registered nurses, oncology nurses, dentists, veterinarians, and behavioural health professionals. Headline scale figures include 1.2M+ MDs and DOs, 600K+ NPs and PAs, 3.4M+ clinicians with validated email addresses, 95%+ email coverage for MDs, DOs, NPs and PAs, and 95% client retention.

The offering is organised into four product pillars spanning the data lifecycle: Profile (define your audience with verified HCP data), Enrich (enhance, clean and update your in-house provider records), Engage (activate HCPs across trigger email, programmatic, social and Reveal anonymous website traffic), and Pulse (measure results, script-lift and campaign analytics).

The foundational Profile product, Practitioner & Facility Demographics, is built from over 500 sources, structured with standardised formatting logic, and validated by an in-house research team to drive more accurate targeting, cleaner directories, and stronger performance across marketing, compliance and operations. Records are updated monthly and designed for real-world application in marketing, compliance, recruitment, and network development. The dataset spans provider contact info (full name, practice address, phone, fax and email); professional credentials (NPI, DEA number, taxonomy codes, licence status and expiration, board certifications, medical education history); practice details (specialty, subspecialty, practice type, languages spoken, telemedicine participation); and facility demographics covering hospitals, health systems, IDNs and group practices, including leadership and decision-makers.

What they position as distinct: HealthLink argues public sources fall short because records from NPPES and state licensing boards are often unstructured, out of date, or conflicting, and without consistent maintenance more than 20% of provider data can become inaccurate in a single year. In response, their solution uses standardised data structures, proprietary validation rules, and live research oversight, integrating into CRM, PRM, MAP, or provider directory systems, with records updated monthly using data from over 500 public and private sources, and each contact validated using a four-step process. The company frames its differentiation around three core differentiators — Product Excellence, Superior Service, and Privacy-Safe Practices — with privacy practices validated independently by TrustArc, the Network Advertising Initiative, and SOC 2 Type II attestation.

## Who they target

HealthLink serves organisations that need to reach physicians and other clinicians, spanning life sciences, hospitals, payers, agencies, and recruiters. The site's "Who We Work With" navigation defines five target segments: Life Sciences & Pharma, Hospitals, Health Plans, Marketing Agencies, and CMEs.

- **Life Sciences & Pharma** — aimed at drug manufacturers seeking to influence prescribers, with the claim that 11 of the 15 largest pharmaceutical companies rely on HealthLink Dimensions for accurate, actionable provider data. Named clients shown include AstraZeneca, AbbVie, Gilead, Takeda, Assertio, Incyte, Bausch Health and Octapharma.
- **Hospitals and health systems** — used to engage the right physicians to improve care coordination, recruit qualified providers, or strengthen referral pathways, with leading organisations like Mayo Clinic, NewYork-Presbyterian, and Trinity Health relying on the data across clinical, operational and marketing teams.
- **Health Plans** — explicitly Third-Party Administrators (TPAs), Managed Care Organisations (MCOs), and Payers, served with network, directory and compliance use cases, and trusted by organisations like Anthem Dental and Blue Cross of Idaho.
- **Marketing Agencies** — helping agencies build smarter strategies and deliver measurable results for healthcare brands, whether planning a national campaign or launching in a new region, with clients including Publicis Health Media, Eversana and Relevate Health Group.
- **CMEs** — continuing medical/nursing education organisations, where CME teams can expect over 20% of their enrolment database to become outdated within just 12 months, risking misrouted outreach and compliance issues; named partners include Matrix Medical, Medscape, VuMedi and ContinuED.

In terms of buyer roles, the solutions target clinical, operational, and marketing teams, and the data itself is built to identify senior leaders, department heads, and decision-makers to support strategic outreach. The market is US-focused, with national coverage of more than 6 million healthcare providers and 6,900 hospitals nationwide.
