## How the pull was made

Script: `sourcing/tam/c1_leads_pull.py`.

- **Tool:** Blitz API `POST /v2/search/people`, paged by cursor, 50 per page. 3,618 Blitz records used.
- **Filters:** the Doc 2 sizing filters for Campaign 1.
  - Company filter: `doc2_common.pharma_co(excl_names=True, kw_excl=True)`, the post-DNC pharma and biotech filter.
  - Titles: `BRAND` minus `BRAND_EXCL`.
  - Levels: C-Team/VP (influencers), Director (decision makers), Manager (advocates).
  - People in the US only.
- **Kept:** a person is kept only when a *current* role is at one of the 441 Campaign 1 companies in
  `sourcing/tam/2026-11-18-doc-2-persona-companies.csv` and that role's title still passes the title filter.
  This gave 3,534 people at 435 companies.
- **DNC re-check:** `dnc.match()` on the company name dropped 0, because the company filter already excludes DNC names.

## Outreach depth (TAM/SAM, inverse rule)

The pool is large: about 3,600 matching people at about 440 companies. Following the inverse TAM rule and Doc 2's
Campaign 1 volume line, we go narrow at each company:

- **Most companies:** 2 people, 1 influencer or decision maker (decision maker preferred) and 1 advocate. Distinct titles are preferred.
- **10001+ employee companies:** up to 5 people (2 influencer or decision maker, 3 advocate), roughly one per franchise.
- **Unfilled slots:** if a company lacks one level, the other level fills its cap.

Result: 733 leads. 235 companies have 2 leads, 182 have 1, 13 have 5, and 5 have 3 or 4.

## Cap change: Moe's rule, 5 to 10 per company (2026-11-19, pulls 2 and 3)

- **Why:** the fit check showed the real Campaign 1 universe is 117 companies, not the roughly 440 used for sizing. The
  original cap of 2 per company then gave only 235 leads for 8 senders.
- **Moe's rule:** "Cap per company is 5-10", then "10 if less number of people are found and 5 if more". This is the
  inverse TAM rule at segment level: a small pool gets 10 per company, a large pool gets 5.
- **Campaign 1 read:** a large pool, with 2,582 matching people at 117 companies (about 22 per company), so the cap is
  **5 per company, big pharma included**.
- **Mix within the cap:** 2 influencers or decision makers (decision makers preferred) and 3 advocates, with distinct titles
  preferred. If a company is short on one level, the other level fills the gap.
- **Command:**
  `python3 sourcing/tam/c1_leads_pull.py 2026-11-19-leads-3.csv ../c1-brand-marketing/2026-11-19-fit-company-slugs.txt 5 5`
  (the arguments are output name, fit slug file, cap for most companies, cap for 10001+ companies). Weak titles are
  filtered inside the script when a fit file is given.
- **Result:** 432 leads at 117 companies.
  - Levels: 237 decision makers, 162 advocates, 33 influencers.
  - Companies: 66 have 5 leads, 6 have 4, 11 have 3, 11 have 2 and 23 have 1.
- **Blitz cost:** pulls 2 and 3 each re-ran the full search, using 3,618 records each. Pull 3 wrote the cache, so later cap
  changes are free.
