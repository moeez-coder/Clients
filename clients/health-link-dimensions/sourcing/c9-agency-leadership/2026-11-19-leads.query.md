## How the pull was made

Script: `sourcing/tam/c9_leads_pull.py`, run with argument `10` (the cap).

- **Universe:** the Doc 2 sizing's wide agency universe for the leadership persona, from
  `sourcing/tam/2026-11-18-doc-2-agency-companies.json` key `leadership`.
  - Health-focused agencies only.
  - DNC and client names dropped with the corrected `dnc.match()`, which now catches SOLVE(D).
  - 351 agencies remain (sizing section 14 said 352).
- **Tool:** Blitz API `POST /v2/search/people`, with `company.linkedin_url` in batches of 50. 899 Blitz records used.
- **Titles:** `persona_counts.CELLS["leadership"]`, the influencer and decision-maker includes with the influencer
  excludes. These match the sizing.
- **Filters:** levels C-Team, VP, Director and Manager; US people; the person's *current* role must be at the agency.
- **Result:** 896 people at 350 agencies.

## Cap (Moe, 2026-11-19)

Moe's rule: "10 if less number of people are found and 5 if more". C9 is the small-pool case, at about 2.6 people per
agency, so the cap is **10 per agency**, influencers first.

Result: 821 leads.
- Levels: 482 influencers, 339 decision makers.
- Agencies: 165 have 1 lead, 96 have 2, and 14 large agencies hit the cap of 10.
