# 2026-10-14 snapshot — AI & data governance buying center, AI-qualified companies

**Client:** Teleion (`5072d422-da5b-44db-adab-b5c2f8d67a2f`)
**Segment status:** expanding an existing segment — the AI governance buying center of config "AI Governance & Production — Enterprise - Custom" (`91734597-7a99-4eca-b725-68428e2bc9c1`). The user's own limits (headcount ≤ 1,000, revenue ≥ $20M) are narrower than that config's enterprise scope.
**Why:** the user gave the buying center and its titles (Chief AI Officer, Head of AI, CISO, CIO, Chief Data Officer, Director of Infrastructure), asked to find those people in the lists already supplied, then qualify their companies as AI-first / AI-native or AI-enabled with heavy use of AI, and return the prospects.

## Rules given by the user
- Seniority VP and Director; no CEO and no COO. The named C-level titles in the buying center (CIO, CISO, Chief Data Officer, Chief AI Officer) were kept as the named titles; CEO, COO, Chief Executive, Chief Operating, Associate Director, Assistant and AVP were excluded.
- Maximum 10 people per company (not reached: at most 3 per company found).
- Company headcount at most 1,000.
- Minimum revenue $20M.

## Input
Both people lists the user supplied: the 10,587-person persona list (`12th-Oct-Default-view-export`, companies in the first Clay account export) and the 49,966-person list (`List-1-Default-view-export`, the all-ICP signal accounts). Headcount was taken from the account exports (list 1 used LinkedIn headcount). Not stored here. Contacted accounts (the 152 signalled in the first export) and Teleion's DNC companies were removed.

## Steps
1. **People** (`2026-10-14-people.py`): title match to the buying-center functions (AI, security, data, IT/CIO, infrastructure) at VP/Director/Head/Chief level, company at most 1,000 employees. 682 people at 508 companies.
2. **Company evidence** (`2026-10-14-pull.py`): Blitz `POST /v2/enrichment/company` (page text and specialties), Blitz `POST /v2/jobs/company` (AI/ML titles among the last 90 days of postings, up to 200), EXA `POST /search` (two queries per company, last 12 months). Raw: `2026-10-14-company-evidence-raw.json.gz`.
3. **Classification** (`2026-10-14-class0.py` then `2026-10-14-final.py`):
   - *AI-first / AI-native:* company page mentions AI 3+ times, or a tech company with 15%+ AI/ML roles (and 5+), or the company's own content calls itself AI-native/AI-first.
   - *AI-enabled (heavy use):* 5+ AI/ML roles, or 3+ with at least 5% of postings, or AI product/deployment news plus 2+ AI/ML roles.
   - Everything else is excluded: 508 → 42 companies.
   - Manual overrides after reading the evidence (in `-final.py`): Forge dropped (the evidence was about Atlassian's Forge, a different company); Altruist's BPCE news removed (another company); WorkWave downgraded from AI-first to AI-enabled (AI product launch only).
4. **Revenue floor** (`-final.py`): the only revenue source is the account export's range. Companies whose range's lower bound is $20M or more (25-50M, 50-100M, 100-500M) are "confirmed" and go in `2026-10-14-leads.csv`. Companies with no revenue, or in the 10-25M band that straddles the floor, go in `2026-10-14-leads-revenue-unconfirmed.csv` instead.
5. **People per company:** ranked AI → security → data → CIO → infrastructure, then more senior titles first, at most 10.

## Result
- `2026-10-14-companies.csv`: 42 AI-qualified companies (14 AI-first, 28 AI-enabled), with evidence and revenue status.
- `2026-10-14-leads.csv`: 18 people at 15 companies with confirmed revenue of $20M or more.
- `2026-10-14-leads-revenue-unconfirmed.csv`: 31 people at 27 companies that pass everything else; revenue must be checked before calling.

## Caveats
- The result is small because the headcount cap removes about 99% of the people (53,003 rows were at companies over 1,000 or of unknown size) and the lists are weighted to banks.
- AI intensity is measured from public signals (AI/ML job titles, company page wording, news), not from the companies' internal use. A bank that uses AI heavily but hires few AI roles will be missed.
- "AI-first" means AI is how the company describes its product; verify before calling.
- Some titles are odd matches (for example "Investment Partner and Chief Information Officer" at Tala, "VP of Threat Investigation and Defense" at WSECU).
- Phone numbers are not included; people were not checked as still in role.
