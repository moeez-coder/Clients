# 2026-10-12 leads snapshot — company-level decision makers at the all-ICP signal accounts

**Client:** Teleion (`5072d422-da5b-44db-adab-b5c2f8d67a2f`)
**Segment status:** expanding existing segments; people at the accounts in `2026-10-12-companies.csv` (the eight job-signal configs listed in `2026-10-12-companies.query.md`).
**Source:** user-supplied export `List-1-Default-view-export` (49,966 people at about 227 companies, with the account's signal codes, trigger text and notes already attached; apparently a Clay export, not an API pull by this session). The input is not stored here; `2026-10-12-leads-filter.py` and `2026-10-12-leads-cap.py` reproduce the filter.
**Why:** Teleion's cold caller said four title groups perform well (see `../icp-decision-makers-by-persona/2026-10-12-leads.query.md`); the user asked for "the right people" from this list.

## What "right people" means here
The export is mostly mid-level staff (7,723 "Vice President", 1,691 "Senior Vice President" and so on) and, at big firms, thousands of people at one company. A plain title match kept 2,087 people, but most were not the company's own officers: divisional CEOs and CFOs (for example "CEO of Business Banking" at JPMorgan), regional and market presidents, and recruiters whose title mentions "Office of the CFO". So the filter requires the title to be the company-level role itself:
- Core titles, in the four persona groups: Chief Compliance Officer, Chief Risk Officer, BSA/AML Officer (Compliance, Risk & Fraud); CIO, CISO, Chief Model Risk Officer (AI & Data Governance); Treasurer, CFO, Chief Accounting Officer (Payments & Finance); CEO, President, CHRO/Chief People Officer, Chair (Executive Sponsor).
- A rank prefix or suffix (EVP, SVP, Managing Director, Group, Global) and the company's own name are allowed; anything else in the title (a division, region, product line, "Office of ...") rejects the row. Retired/former/advisory/deputy/interim/assistant and Vice Chair are rejected.
- Removed people at the 152 contacted accounts and Teleion's DNC companies (none were in this list).
- At most 3 people per company per persona group, ranked chief/CEO/chair titles first, shortest title next.
- Result: 49,966 → 387 people at 174 companies (Compliance, Risk & Fraud 25, AI & Data Governance 42, Payments & Finance 132, Executive Sponsor 188). 2,087 → 449 by the strict title rule, then 57 cut by the per-company cap, then rows with neither a company nor a signal removed.

## Columns
Same as the earlier persona list, plus `segment`, `signal_codes`, `signals_triggers` and `signal_notes` carried over from the export (the job postings behind each account's signal, with dates and links). Use `signal_notes` as the call opener.

## Caveats
- This rule trades recall for precision: it will miss real company-level officers with unusual titles, and large groups like JPMorgan keep only the top few people (and JPMorgan's divisional leaders are deliberately gone).
- Plain "President" titles are ambiguous: in firms like HUB International many regional presidents carry exactly this title, so the cap picks 3 without being able to tell which is the company-level one. Review those rows.
- Chinese-language company names (for example Medtronic, Marvell) come from the export; some regional-subsidiary executives may be listed under the global company.
- About half the signalled accounts rest on the weaker E2 (M&A-integration job-title) signal; E1, P3, P5 and M2 are stronger.
- Phone numbers are not included.
