**Supersedes:** `2026-10-14-leads.csv` for calling. That file is kept unchanged (snapshots are append-only), but 35 of its people were already being worked and must not be called from it.
**Why:** the user supplied two lists of people already in play and asked that none of them be included:
- `2026-10-12-leads-Default-view-export` (388 rows; the persona list's signal-account leads, with mobile numbers added)

## Method
Same rules, ranking and caps as `2026-10-14-leads.query.md` (`2026-10-14-select-2.py`), plus an exclusion step: a candidate is skipped if their LinkedIn URL (normalized: lowercase, no protocol/www/country prefix, no trailing slash or query) is in either file, or, as a fallback, if their full name and company domain both match. The 889 excluded LinkedIn URLs are saved in `2026-10-14-leads-2-exclusion-linkedin-urls.txt` (URLs only; the phone numbers in the source files are not stored here).
