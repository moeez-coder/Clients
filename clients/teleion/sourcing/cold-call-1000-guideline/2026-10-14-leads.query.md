# 2026-10-14 leads — 1,000 cold-call prospects per the Teleion Cold Calling Guideline

**Client:** Teleion (`5072d422-da5b-44db-adab-b5c2f8d67a2f`)
**Segment status:** expanding existing segments; all buying centers in the guideline across the FS configs and the enterprise configs (no new config-slug).
**Why:** the user supplied the "Teleion Cold Calling Guideline" (Ali Rehman, 7 Oct 2026) and asked for 1,000 people matching its criteria, each with the Teleion service to sell, excluding CEO and COO and companies over 10,000 employees.

## Criteria from the guideline (and the user's overrides)
- Industries: Finance, Healthcare, Technology, Retail, Manufacturing. Start with Financial Services (community/regional banks, fintech and payments).
- Company size 201 to 10,000 employees (guideline says 201 to 10,000+; the user capped it at 10,000). The earlier 1,000 cap is removed.
- Revenue over $20M.
- Titles from the guideline's buying-center table, at C-level, VP, Director or Head of Department: AI & data governance; marketing operations; compliance, risk & fraud; payments & finance; operations & procurement; executive sponsor. CEO and COO excluded (user), President excluded (not in the table and usually CEO-level), sales managers/directors/reps excluded (guideline), founders/owners excluded (usually CEO), assistant/associate VP, retired, advisory and interim titles excluded.
- US-based people only (guideline: US first); Teleion's DNC companies removed; regulators removed (Federal Reserve, FINRA and similar); the 152 already-contacted accounts removed.

## Source
The two user-supplied people exports (`12th-Oct-Default-view-export`, 10,587 rows; `List-1-Default-view-export`, 49,966 rows) joined to the account files for headcount, revenue and signals: the user's first Clay account export and this repo's `fs-compliance-ops-signals-recheck/2026-10-12-companies.csv` and `all-icps-job-signals-expansion/2026-10-12-companies.csv`. No new API calls. Inputs are not stored here; `2026-10-14-people.py` and `2026-10-14-select.py` reproduce the result.

## Selection
- 4,793 people pass the title, size and exclusion rules.
- Revenue: "confirmed" when the account export's revenue range starts at $20M or more; "inferred" when there is no revenue figure but the company has 1,000+ employees; "unverified" when the range is $10-25M; "unknown" when nothing is available. Unknown-revenue and non-US people were not selected.
- Ranking score: the company's dated signal matches the person's buying center (+40; for example BSA/AML hiring for a compliance title), the company has any signal (+10), revenue confirmed (+15) or inferred (+10), Financial Services (+10, the guideline's first segment), US (+8), West Coast (+3), seniority (Chief > EVP/SVP > VP/Head > Director).
- Spread: at most 4 people per company and 2 per buying center per company (large TAM, so fewer per account, per the repo standard). Result: 1,000 people at 442 companies.

## Columns
`teleion_service_to_sell` maps the buying center and title to one of the guideline's seven service lines. `trigger` gives the company's dated signal with its source when one exists for the person's area, otherwise says to use the general opener. `call_opener`, `proof_point` and `discovery_question` are taken word-for-word from the guideline's trigger-opener table, proof-points table and discovery questions. `priority_rank` is the call order.

## Result
- Buying centers: payments & finance 331, AI & data governance 316, compliance, risk & fraud 178, operations & procurement 85, executive sponsor (CHRO) 62, marketing operations 28.
- Revenue: 726 confirmed, 273 inferred from headcount, 1 unverified.
- 913 at Financial Services companies; 177 have a dated signal matching their own buying center, 313 work at a company with any signal.
