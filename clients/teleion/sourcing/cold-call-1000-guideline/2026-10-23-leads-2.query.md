## Tool and limits
- Blitz `POST /v2/enrichment/phone` was tried first and returned 401 "You do not have access to this route": the Blitz key does not include phone lookups.
- Prospeo `POST https://api.prospeo.io/enrich-person` with `{"enrich_mobile": true, "only_verified_mobile": true, "data": {"linkedin_url": ...}}` (header `X-KEY`). It costs 10 credits per number found; "no match" costs nothing.
- The account had 12,537 credits (renews 2026-11-07), enough for about 1,250 numbers, not 2,000. The user declined the question about how many credits to spend, so the session used the cautious default: enrich in `priority_rank` order and stop while about 1,500 credits remain for other clients. It stopped with 1,617 left (`2026-10-23-phones.py`).

## Result
- Ranks 1-1,175 checked: 1,090 verified US mobiles, 2 Canadian numbers left out, 83 with no match.
- Ranks 1,176-2,000 (825 people): not checked; `phone_status` says so. Re-run `2026-10-23-phones.py` after the renewal, or use another source.

## Caveats
- These are mobile numbers: the cold-calling guideline asks the team to confirm Teleion's rules on mobile numbers and calling hours before the first shift, and every number must be checked against Teleion's Do Not Contact list before dialing.
- "Verified" is Prospeo's own status; numbers can still be out of date.
