## Qualification (same as the 1,000, minus the signal ranking)
- Guideline buying-center titles at C-level, VP, Director or Head level. Excluded: CEO, COO, President, founder/owner, all sales roles (plus "revenue" titles that are sales roles), assistant/associate VP, retired, advisory and interim titles.
- Company 201 to 10,000 employees; revenue over $20M. "Confirmed" when the account export's range starts at $20M or more; "inferred" when there is no figure but the company has 1,000+ employees. Companies whose range straddles $20M (10-25M) or has no figure under 1,000 employees were not used.
- US-based people only. Excluded: Teleion's DNC companies, regulators, trade associations, the 152 already-contacted accounts, everyone in either version of the 1,000 list, and the 889 people in the user's two already-worked lists (`2026-10-14-leads-2-exclusion-linkedin-urls.txt`).

## Steps
1. **Company universe** (`2026-10-23-universe.py`): 805 qualifying companies, 604 from the user's first Clay account export with revenue confirmed and 201 from that export or `../all-icps-job-signals-expansion/2026-10-12-companies.csv` with revenue inferred from headcount.
2. **People pull** (`2026-10-23-pull.py`): Blitz API `POST /v2/search/people` with `company.linkedin_url` = 5 companies per request (161 requests, cursor-paginated), `people.job_level` = C-Team, VP, Director, `people.job_title.include` = buying-center keywords, `people.location.country_code` = US. 20,671 records, 0 errors. Raw: `2026-10-23-blitz-people-raw.json.gz`.
3. **Filter and select** (`2026-10-23-build.py`): the person's current job must be at a qualifying company; same title rules as the 1,000 list; leftover people from the user's people exports added. 14,523 candidates. Ranked by revenue confirmed over inferred, Financial Services first (the guideline's lead segment), West Coast, then seniority; spread at most 4 per company and 2 per buying center per company (raised in steps only if needed; not needed).
