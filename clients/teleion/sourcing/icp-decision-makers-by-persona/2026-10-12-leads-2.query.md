# 2026-10-12 leads-2 — call-personalization signals, 200-person sample

**Client:** Teleion (`5072d422-da5b-44db-adab-b5c2f8d67a2f`)
**Segment status:** expanding existing segments; a quality-check sample, not the full list.
**Why:** the cold caller needs a company-specific line to open with. This sample tests two signal types before running them on all 5,063 people in `2026-10-12-leads.csv`.

## Sample
200 people from `2026-10-12-leads.csv`: 50 per persona group (Compliance/Risk/Fraud, AI & Data Governance, Payments & Finance, Executive Sponsor), one person per company, random seed 7 (`2026-10-12-leads-2-pull.py`).

## Signals pulled (last 90 days for jobs, 120 days for news)
1. **Open role in the persona's own area.** Blitz `POST /v2/enrichment/domain-to-linkedin` then `POST /v2/jobs/company`; the title is matched against a persona-specific pattern (for example BSA/AML/compliance/fraud/risk for Compliance; treasury/payments/accounting for Finance). Interns, co-ops, sales, branch and loan-officer roles are dropped. Days since posting are computed, because the cold caller reported that posts 30-60 days old convert best.
2. **Company news.** EXA `POST https://api.exa.ai/search` (about $0.007 per search, 3 searches per company: M&A, regulatory action, AI announcement). Kept only when the signal word and the company name or domain appear in the headline, the item has a publish date, and it is not a job post, earnings release, rebrand, naming-rights deal, social post or event.

Pipeline: `2026-10-12-leads-2-pull.py` (pull), `-refine1.py` and `-refine2.py` (filters), `-openers.py` (rating and opener text). Raw responses: `2026-10-12-leads-2-raw-exa-blitz.json.gz`. The first unfiltered pass returned news for 181 of 200 companies, almost all noise; the filters cut that to 60, and the file shows signals for 103 of 200 people.

## Confidence
- **high:** a bank/credit-union/financial deal verified by company name or domain in the headline, or an open role posted within 60 days.
- **medium:** an open role 61-90 days old, a regulatory item, or an AI announcement for the AI & Data Governance persona.
- **low:** other AI announcements, or deals that fail the finance check.
- **none:** nothing found, or the source row has no company name.

## Opener text
`call_opener` is a suggested line, not a script. The proof points quoted are the figures in Teleion's own ICP form (BaaS sponsor bank: examiner agreement to close 64% of findings in 12 months; Outreach ISO/IEC 42001 in 4 months with zero findings; a 3,663-ticket backlog cut, resolution time down 92%; 18+ years running enterprise operations). Check them with Teleion before the caller uses them.

## Known problems
- Not yet matched by LinkedIn company ID, so generic names ("Community Bank", "First Bank", "CSI") can pick up a different company's news. Review medium and low rows by hand.
- Some accounts are not real prospects (for example Federal Reserve System, ICBA) and some hits come from vendors rather than banks.
- A role posted at a company is not proof it sits under this person.
- 8 rows have no company name in the source and received no signal.
- Only the open-role and news signals were tried; enforcement orders from regulator sites and person-level signals (new in role, recent posts) were not.
