# 2026-10-12 leads snapshot — persona filter on user-supplied people export

**Client:** Teleion (`5072d422-da5b-44db-adab-b5c2f8d67a2f`)
**Segment status:** expanding existing segments (no new config-slug); people at the ICP accounts that had no signal in the Clay account export. Not tied to one config, so it sits in its own folder.
**Source:** user-supplied export `12th-Oct-Default-view-export` (10,587 people, 1,458 companies; columns Company Name, Name, Job Title, Location, Company Domain, LinkedIn Profile; apparently a Clay export, not an API pull by this session). The input file is not stored here; the filter is reproducible with `2026-10-12-filter_people.py`.
**Why:** Teleion's cold caller reported which titles perform; the user asked for those titles from this list.

## Persona groups (from the cold caller's feedback)
| Group | Titles matched | Caller note |
|---|---|---|
| Compliance, Risk & Fraud | Chief Compliance Officer, Chief Risk Officer, BSA / BSA-AML / Bank Secrecy Act Officer (and combined titles such as Chief Risk & Compliance Officer) | More push back, but higher conversion rate on 30-60 day posts |
| AI & Data Governance | CISO, CIO, Chief Model Risk Officer (the caller's "CSIO" was read as CISO) | Easy to talk with |
| Payments & Finance | Treasurer, CFO, Chief Accounting Officer | CFO and Treasurer easiest and most flexible to talk to; Treasurer especially |
| Executive Sponsor | CEO, President, CHRO (Chief Human Resources Officer / Chief People Officer), Board Chair / Chairman | Fewer pickups |

`persona_group` is the first matching group in the order above; `also_matches` lists any others (for example a CFO who is also CRO).

## Filters applied
- Title keeps: the titles above. Dropped: retired, former, emeritus, advisor/advisory, deputy, interim, assistant, board member/treasurer, vice chair, vice president (so "President" is not matched inside "Vice President"). Bare "CRO"/"CCO"/"CAO" abbreviations were NOT matched, because they appear as Chief Revenue / Commercial / Administrative Officer in this file.
- Removed people at the 152 contacted accounts from the Clay account export (by domain), and at Teleion's DNC companies (Amazon, Starbucks, Lyft, BECU, Gates Foundation, Microsoft).
- Result: 10,587 → 5,063 people at 1,312 companies. 4,650 had no title match and 874 sat at contacted accounts.
- `company_signal_codes` / `company_signal_tier` were added from this session's two 2026-10-12 company snapshots (domain match); `-` means the company has no signal in either file (that does not mean it was checked for P1 or P4).

## Caveats
- Only 13 people have a BSA/AML title and 2 have Chief Model Risk Officer, so those two are thin; Compliance leads with CCO and CRO.
- "Community Bank President" (115 people) matches President; these are often regional heads at larger banks, so review before calling.
- Title matching is on the title text only; location, seniority and whether the person is still at the company are not verified. Titles like "EVP, Chief Risk Officer" were kept.
- Phone numbers are not in the source and were not looked up (the user is handling that).
