Q: Who are you, exactly?

Q: How did you get my details?
A: Through LinkedIn — you replied to a message from Christian there, so this is me picking that thread back up. Nothing bought, nothing scraped.

Q: What does it cost?
A: It depends entirely on scope, and that's exactly what the call is for. Christian will walk you through it once he knows what you're actually trying to solve.

Q: Can you tell me more about what they actually do before I commit to a meeting?

Q: We already do this in-house / we've got someone for that.

Q: Who else do you work with?
A: I'm not going to name clients on a cold follow-up call — Christian can talk through relevant examples directly with you.

Q: How long before we'd see anything?
A: That depends on what you'd be starting from, so I'd only be guessing. Put it to Christian on the call — **it's the first thing he'll want to scope.**

Q: I'm not the decision maker.
A: No problem — who would be? Happy to speak to them, or you're welcome to sit in on the call too so you hear it first-hand.

Q: Just send me something by email instead.
A: I can do that, but honestly an email won't tell you much — this is one of those things that's quicker to explain in fifteen minutes. **Shall I pencil something in and send the invite at the same time?**

Q: What actually happens on the call?
