## What they do

twoHundred runs exclusive, invitation-only communities and events for C-level leaders. twoHundred curates invitation-only local events for C-level leaders from top 200 enterprises — a trusted space for peer insights, meaningful discussions, and lasting connections. Founded in Copenhagen in 2017, the company draws on a specific heritage: it was inspired by a tradition that began in 1911, when the Solvay Conferences brought the world's leading minds together, by invitation only, to exchange ideas that shaped the future. Its stated framework is Enable, Evolve, Elevate, and it creates exclusive forums where C-level executives from the top 200 enterprises in each Nordic country connect to share insights, challenge perspectives, and strengthen leadership.

The offering runs across two complementary event formats plus role-based communities. The Forum is the larger, recurring gathering: the twoHundred Forum consists of exclusive, invitation-only gatherings for senior executives from the largest Nordic enterprises, usually held twice a year, blending keynotes and structured networking in a trusted, sales-free environment. It is a premium, biannual event bringing together senior leaders for open dialogue and peer exchange, with keynotes from distinguished thought leaders and curated networking sessions, delivering both inspiration and practical solutions in a confidential setting. The Roundtable is the intimate format: twoHundred Roundtable is an intimate, invitation-only forum where senior executives engage in focused dialogue on urgent topics, with each Roundtable bringing together a select group to share insights, explore solutions, and build trusted relationships in a confidential setting. On the home page the Roundtable is further described as a private event where C-level leaders and a select partner engage in open, insightful discussions and share knowledge in a trusted setting.

These events are organised into dedicated executive communities. Each twoHundred community brings together C-level executives to share insights, challenges, and strategies within their specific field, spanning CIO, CISO, CFO, CHRO and CSCO/CPO (Supply Chain & CPO) groups. The community is a trusted arena for sharing insights, learning from peers, and building lasting connections that go beyond individual events.

What makes it distinct is the curated, non-commercial model. Membership and attendance are invitation-only and selective, with every event designed for quality over quantity — a trusted space for meaningful dialogue, strategic growth, and lasting relationships. Crucially, commercial partners are positioned as contributors rather than sellers: selected partners join not to sell, but to contribute, enabling knowledge sharing, evolving strategic thinking, and elevating leadership impact across industries. The company operates on the ground across the region — with offices across the Nordics, its team brings together event specialists, community builders, and partnership strategists, with Community Directors in Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Finland.

## Who they target

twoHundred serves two distinct audiences: senior executives (as members) and vendors (as partners).

The members are the most senior leaders of the largest regional enterprises. Its community brings together C-level leaders from the top 200 enterprises in each Nordic country. By buyer role, the communities and events are organised around specific C-suite functions — CIO, CISO, CFO, CHRO and CSCO/CPO — and event audiences on the site also extend to CMO, CCO, CTO, CDO, CPO and sustainability leaders. By way of example, the CFO programme states that the twoHundred CFO Events unite senior finance executives from the top 200 local enterprises, with CFOs exploring strategy, transformation, and leadership through curated discussions and peer-led sessions in a trusted, confidential environment, while the CISO programme brings together senior security leaders from top 200 local enterprises for open, strategic peer discussions in a trusted, sales-free environment.

Geographically the focus is the Nordics, extended to the Netherlands. The partner page is explicit that the reach covers C-level decision makers from the top 200 local enterprises in Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, and the Netherlands — and event listings on the site include Stockholm, Oslo, Copenhagen and Amsterdam venues. In terms of company size, the consistent target is the top 200 (largest) enterprises in each country.

The second audience is commercial partners — vendors and experts wanting access to these executives. twoHundred's strategic partners are leading experts who engage directly with C-level decision makers. The value proposition to them is relationship-led rather than lead-based: partners gain direct access to exclusive C-level communities and, by engaging in curated, invitation-only events, build authentic relationships with decision-makers — not leads from a list. As partners, they join roundtable discussions built for open, honest exchange, take part in seated lunches that foster deeper conversation, and connect with senior leaders who match their ideal customer profile. Named partners referenced on the site include Workday and Ivalua, indicating B2B technology and enterprise-software vendors as typical buyers.
