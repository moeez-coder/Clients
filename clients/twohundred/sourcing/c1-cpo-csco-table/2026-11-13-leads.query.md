## 1. Companies (`2026-11-13-companies.csv`)

`POST /v2/search/companies` was run as two queries, with every page pulled.

**Filters shared by both queries:**
- `keywords.include`: supply chain software, procurement software, source-to-pay, spend management, supply chain planning, transportation management system, warehouse management system, logistics software, supplier management, e-procurement
- `employee_range`: 501-1000, 1001-5000, 5001-10000, 10001+. This applies the ICP form's 501+ employee rule.

**The two queries:**

| Query | Industries | Results |
|---|---|---|
| A | Software Development, IT Services and IT Consulting, Technology, Information and Internet | 835 |
| B | Transportation, Logistics, Supply Chain and Storage; Truck Transportation; Freight and Package Transportation; Warehousing and Storage | 348 |

Together they gave 1,181 unique companies.

**Dream accounts added by hand:** `POST /v2/enrichment/domain-to-linkedin` and then `/v2/enrichment/company` for the client's dream and known S2P/SCM vendors that the keywords missed. Only those with 501+ employees were kept: GEP, Infor, Infios and Uber Freight. Coupa is on the DNC list, so it was dropped.

**Qualification:**
1. **Pre-filter in a script.** It scored each company by supply chain and procurement terms in its specialties. It dropped companies with fewer than 150 LinkedIn employees or a domain on the DNC list. From query B it kept only companies whose description mentions software, SaaS or a platform. 250 candidates were left.
2. **Manual review.** Each candidate was reviewed by hand against the C1 qualifier: the core product must be supply chain planning, procurement, S2P/AP, spend, logistics, TMS, WMS or freight-tech software.
   - **Excluded:** IT services firms and SIs, ERP suites, 3PLs, staffing firms, and legacy brands now inside another vendor (JDA, RedPrairie, BluJay, Kewill, Amber Road, GT Nexus, Lawson).
   - **Result: 57 companies.**
3. **DNC.** The 53-domain client DNC was applied (`../../context/dnc-2026-11-13.csv`, pulled from tracking_clients `dncList` on 2026-11-13). It removed Zycus, Ivalua, Basware, Coupa, SAP, Oracle and Workday Strategic Sourcing, among others.

`leads_found` = 0 is kept in the companies file so the drop-off stays visible (24 companies).

## 2. Leads (`2026-11-13-leads.csv`)

`POST /v2/search/employee-finder` was run per company, in two passes. Both passes used `job_level` C-Team, VP and Director, and `job_function` Advertising & Marketing, Sales & Business Development, and General Business & Management.
1. `country_code` DK, SE, NO, FI, NL. Gave 223 people.
2. `sales_region` EMEA, up to 2 pages, keeping only titles or headlines that mention Nordic, Benelux, Scandinavia, Northern Europe, EMEA, Europe or a target country. Gave 213 people.

**Filters (script `rank.py`, logic summarised):**
- **Kept:** only the current role at the target company. The title must say Nordic, Benelux or EMEA/Europe, or the person must be based in DK, SE, NO, FI or NL.
- **Dropped:**
  - ICs and non-GTM roles: account execs and managers, presales and solutions, CS, partnerships, SDR, ops and the like.
  - Titles scoped to another region: South, Central and Eastern, DACH, Iberia, France, Italy, or APAC-only.
- **Persona tiers, from the doc's C1 persona:**
  1. Marketing (field, regional, EMEA, events, ABM, CMO)
  2. Country Manager, MD, GM or RVP for the region
  3. Sales leadership
- **Ranking:** by tier, then Nordic/NL-based or Nordic-titled first, then connections.

**Depth: up to 5 per company.** C1's TAM/SAM is small (57 companies), so per the outreach-depth rule we go wide inside each one, as the doc says ("Up to 4 or 5 contacts per company, because this segment is small").

**Result:**
- 75 unique leads at 33 companies, keyed on `linkedin_url`. There are no duplicates.
- Persona tiers: tier 1: 16, tier 2: 11, tier 3: 48.
- Countries: NL 22, GB 21, FR 9, SE 5, NO 5, DE 4, DK 3, FI 3, other 3.
