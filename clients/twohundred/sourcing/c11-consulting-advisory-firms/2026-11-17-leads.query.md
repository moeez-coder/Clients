# C11 Consulting and Advisory Firms: 2026-11-17 pull (new segment)

- **Why:** Moe asked for a new campaign for consulting firms (2026-11-17), after sizing showed the "Consulting" part of the client's ICP form had never been sourced.
- **Tool:** Blitz API `POST /v2/search/people`.
  - Company filter: industries Business Consulting and Services + Information Services, 501+ employees.
  - People filter: C-Team/VP/Director in Marketing, Sales & BD, or General Business; located in DK/SE/NO/FI/NL.
  - Returned **6,071 people**.
- **Filters:** the same title, region and 53-company DNC rules as C5 to C7, plus anyone already live in C1/C5/C7/C6 removed. That left 643 people at 144 companies.
- **Not consultancies (excluded by hand):** 488 left at 91 firms. Examples: Elsevier, Experian, Dun & Bradstreet, Xerox, Rystad Energy, staffing (SThree), testing labs (Kiwa, Applus+, Normec), accounting services (Aspia, Accountor), job boards, "Independent Consultant". The full list is in the session's build script.
- **Depth: up to 15 per company.** The TAM is small (~91 firms), so we go deep, but without a cap Accenture alone had 160 contacts and BCG 72. Lifting the cap would add ~210 more, mostly Accenture and BCG managing directors.
- **Result: 278 leads at 91 firms.**
