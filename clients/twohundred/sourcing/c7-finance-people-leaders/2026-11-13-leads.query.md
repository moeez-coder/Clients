# c7-finance-people-leaders: 2026-11-13 pull

- **Run by:** session `claude/twohundred-init`, 2026-11-13, on Moe's "size them and actually source them".
- **Tool:** Blitz API (`BLITZ_API_KEY`), called directly over HTTPS. Flat plan, ~14M records left (resets 2026-12-07); this whole batch cost no extra spend.
- **DNC:** the 53-company client DNC (`../../context/dnc-2026-11-13.csv`) was applied to every segment, by domain and by name.
- **Geography:** every lead is based in DK/SE/NO/FI/NL, or holds a Nordic/Benelux/EMEA title. Other-region titles (South/DACH/Iberia/APAC/UK-only) were dropped.
- **Persona tiers:** 1 = marketing (field/regional/EMEA/events/ABM/CMO), 2 = country manager/MD/RVP, 3 = sales leadership. Non-GTM and IC roles excluded. Ranked tier, then Nordic-based first, then connections.
- **Keyed on `linkedin_url`**, deduped within the segment.

## Method
- `POST /v2/company/tam-by-people` then `/v2/search/people`.
- Company filter: finance software (ERP, FP&A, treasury, payments, AP, close, accounting) and HR software (HCM, payroll, talent, workforce, recruiting, LMS) keywords across software + financial-services + HR-services industries, 501+ employees.
- Depth: 3 contacts per company.
- Result: 500 leads at 259 companies. Workday, SAP and Oracle are on the DNC and were removed.
- NOTE: the `community` variable (CFO vs CHRO) is NOT yet set per lead. It needs a per-company classification step (which software the vendor sells). Until then the HeyReach fallback copy ("CFO and CHRO communities") is used.
