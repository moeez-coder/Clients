## Resulting universe definition
**In:** discrete manufacturers (OEMs, including national arms of foreign manufacturers) of
industrial products with a deep or configurable catalogue, across:
construction & agricultural equipment · mining · cranes & lifting · material handling ·
heavy trucks, truck bodies & trailers · packaging & process equipment · HVAC & hydronics ·
pumps, compressors, valves & flow control · hydraulics & pneumatics · power transmission ·
fasteners & components · industrial tools, tooling & workholding · electrical & automation
components · machine tools · metrology · power equipment · robotics / physical AI ·
aerospace components · marine / RV / powersports components · high-volume consumable parts.

**Out:** fashion & apparel (owner, 2026-09-29) · consumer retail & retailers · gaming ·
building products & architectural specification (HVAC excepted — reopened by campaigns 3
and 10) · patient-care medical devices · rail & transit aftermarket · semiconductor capital
equipment · contract & office furniture · distributors, dealers, integrators, job shops ·
software, services, education, media.

**Changed from the earlier rules:** HVAC & hydronics now IN; aerospace now IN (campaign 3
names it); vehicle/truck OEMs IN for campaign 10 only; high-volume consumables IN.

**Size:** campaigns 3/5/7 set $100M+, campaign 9 $250M+, campaigns 1/6 no floor. Universe
keeps all qualifying sizes and tags them; revenue data is sparse (Blitz 1.6% fill), so
headcount band is carried as the proxy (≥500 employees ≈ client "Yes" tier, ~1% error).
