# Query — 2026-09-28 companies snapshot (CAD-to-render agencies, new segment)

**Segment:** new segment ("VNTANA 2.0"). The repo owner asked for *"marketing companies that turn CAD into renders"*: CGI, 3D-rendering, product-visualisation and technical-animation agencies that take manufacturers' CAD and turn it into marketing imagery. VNTANA automates exactly that pipeline (CAD → optimised web 3D/renders), and "Global Agencies" is one of VNTANA's own site verticals. So these agencies are either (a) prospects/partners for VNTANA themselves, or (b) a route to their **clients**: brands already paying to turn CAD into content, which is VNTANA's pitch. Part (b) is in `2026-09-28-agency-clients.csv` (see its own `.query.md`).

**tracking_clients config:** none yet. No sourcing config exists for this segment (as of `list_sourcing_configs`, 2026-09-28), so there is no config UUID to cite. Create one before building campaigns on it.

**Tools (the repo owner named AI Ark and EXA mid-session; Blitz ran first):**

1. **Blitz API `POST /v2/search/companies`**. `industry.include` = Advertising Services, Marketing Services, Design Services, Graphic Design, Animation and Post-production, Media Production, Photography, Architecture and Planning. `keywords.include` = CGI, 3D rendering, 3D render, renders, product rendering, product visualization, 3D visualization, 3D animation, CAD, photorealistic, 3D product. `employee_range` = 1-10 … 1001-5000. 40,470 total matches; 400 pages × 25 = 10,000 pulled (partial; cursor not exhausted). Very noisy: Blitz keywords are OR'd single-word full-text, so it returned mostly architecture/BIM drafting shops.
2. **AI Ark `POST /v1/companies`, keyword query.** `account.keyword.any.include`, sources DESCRIPTION + SEO + KEYWORD in **`WORD`** mode (phrase match; `SMART` mode returned Roblox/Warner Bros/universities, so don't use it here). Phrases: CAD to render, CAD rendering, CGI product, product CGI, product rendering, 3D product rendering, product visualization/visualisation, photorealistic rendering, 3D product animation, industrial CGI, technical animation, industrial animation, product animation, 3D product visualization, CGI studio, CGI agency. `exclude` (same sources): architectural visualization, architectural rendering, archviz, real estate rendering, interior rendering, BIM. **3,316 total, all pulled** (34 pages × 100).
3. **AI Ark `POST /v1/companies`, lookalike.** `lookalikeDomains` seeded with LinkedIn company URLs (per the domain-seed warning in CLAUDE.md), constrained by `account.industries` (WORD: advertising services, marketing services, design services, graphic design, animation and post-production, media production, photography).
   - Seed set A (product-CGI shops): kinetic-vision, ikarus3d, 3dexcite, medialab-3d-solutions, renderthat → 30 pages × 100 = 3,000 pulled (10,000 cap). On-topic rate held at 60–87% per page through page 10.
   - Seed set B (creative/motion studios): moonshot-cgi, run-for-the-hills, whipsaw, lavalabs-moving-images, brickland-sweden → 10 pages = 1,000 pulled. Weaker (25–40% on-topic per page), so not pulled deeper.
4. **EXA `POST /search`, discovery.** 8 natural-language queries (e.g. "3D product rendering agency that turns manufacturer CAD files into photorealistic marketing renders", "agency that converts SolidWorks and STEP files into photoreal product images…"), each run with `category: company` and without, `numResults: 100`. Directory/marketplace/social/tool-vendor domains dropped. 336 domains were new to the pool; 290 resolved to a LinkedIn company via **Blitz `/v2/enrichment/domain-to-linkedin`** and were enriched via **Blitz `/v2/enrichment/company`**.

**Merge + scoring:** deduped on normalised company LinkedIn URL (15,995 unique across all sources). Regex relevance scoring over each company's description/SEO/keywords/specialties (`fit_score`, `fit_tier`, `cad_mentioned`, `render_terms_found` columns):
- Must mention a render term (render/CGI/photoreal/3D or product visualisation/3D or product or technical animation/3D product/3D modelling/configurator) **and** a product/industrial term.
- Must be agency-shaped (agency-type industry, or a studio/CGI/3D/render/visual/creative/design word in the name).
- Dropped: LinkedIn `/school/` pages; education/staffing/games/government/real estate/construction/architecture industries; archviz-dominant descriptions (architecture terms ≥ 2 and ≥ product terms).
- **Tier A** = mentions CAD/SolidWorks/Creo/CATIA/NX/Inventor/STEP/engineering data **and** ≥2 render terms, or ≥3 render terms and ≥3 product terms. **B** = ≥2 render terms or ≥3 product terms. **C** = the rest that passed the gates.
- Removed 2 rows whose domain is on the client's `tracking_clients` `dncList`.

**Result:** 6,187 agencies: 928 A, 3,027 B, 2,232 C. Top HQ countries: US 1,190, IN 1,067, GB 650, PK 213, DE 199. Many rows are 1–10-person freelancers (kept, but filter on `employees_on_linkedin`). **Not manually vetted row by row.** The regex tiers are a first-pass sort, so eyeball rows before outreach.

**TAM/SAM read (per CLAUDE.md's outreach-depth rule):** large on paper (thousands), but the reachable agency set with 11+ staff and A/B fit is ~800. As an agency-partner play, that's mid-sized, so go **2–3 contacts per agency** (founder/MD, head of new business/partnerships, head of CGI/3D) rather than 1. No people have been pulled yet.
