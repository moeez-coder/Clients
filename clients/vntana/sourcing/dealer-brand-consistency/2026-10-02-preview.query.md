## Limits
- Only 274 of 559 US companies and 722 of 2,847 UK/EU candidates have any persona found by Blitz in these functions: the rest need a
  Prospeo person search or a title-specific Blitz query (Prospeo can filter by exact title; not run here).
- Tier 1 regex has false positives (e.g. "Channel Development Manager" at an energy retailer, "Network Facilities … Manager" at a car
  importer); tier 3 is a fallback and is 60% of rows. Core T1+T2 is the safer number.
- Not run: EXA dealer-locator search, AI Ark dealer-manufacturer lookalikes, and the Haiku dealer-site qualification for the full UK/EU
  set — those are the actual sourcing run.
