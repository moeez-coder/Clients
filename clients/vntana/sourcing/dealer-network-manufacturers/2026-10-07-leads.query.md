# Query — 2026-10-07 leads snapshot (dealer-network manufacturers)

**Config:** `Dealer-network manufacturers reviewed for inconsistent product imagery across 10 dealer sites`, UUID `4d4f021e-e85b-4ce9-8ec6-96bdf98870a3`; persona `d584cc62-e518-458a-abe9-6b229be69f84`; campaign "The Dealer Who Rebuilt Your Brand Badly" (`2a5ec07e-6fac-46e5-a6e4-8a0c6a3458d8`, HeyReach `637979`).
**Input accounts:** the **1,502 companies** in `2026-10-07-companies-qualified.csv` with tier Q1/Q2/Q3 and a company LinkedIn URL (Q4, 156 lowest-confidence, were *not* contacted-sourced).
**Output:** `2026-10-07-leads.csv`, **1,044 people at 727 companies**, one row per person, **keyed by the person's LinkedIn profile URL (first column)** per the repo standard. Deduped on that URL.
**No emails, phones or enrichment were pulled.** This is prospect identification only.

## Who we contact (from the persona)

Director of Channel Marketing · Director of Dealer Marketing · Dealer Development Manager · Director of Marketing Communications · Co-op Marketing Manager · (flow control / hydraulics) the distributor-marketing or rep-program owner. The client brief's *wrong contacts*: VMO, analytics, compliance, procurement.

## Tool and passes (all Blitz API `POST /v2/search/people`, `company.linkedin_url` ≤10 per call, `max_results: 50`, cursor-paged)

1. **Persona-title pass.** `people.job_title.include` (keyword/FTS): Channel Marketing, Dealer Marketing, Dealer Development, Co-op/Coop/Cooperative Marketing, Marketing Communications, Marcom, Distributor Marketing, Dealer Programs, Dealer Network, Dealer Relations, Dealer Operations, Partner Marketing, Channel Manager, Channel Programs, Rep Program, Dealer Experience, Dealer Support; `exclude`: Intern, Student, Assistant, Receptionist, Recruiter, Consultant, Freelance, Retired. `job_level` = C-Team/VP/Director/Manager. `location.country_code` = US, CA, GB, IE, AU, NZ. → 470 rows.
2. **Marketing-function pass (fallback).** `job_function = Advertising & Marketing`, same levels and countries, no title filter, 5 companies per call, ≤8 pages. Added because only 190 of 1,502 companies had anyone with an exact persona title in those countries. → 2,304 rows.
3. **Worldwide pass, only for the 984 companies still without a lead.** Same as pass 2 with no country filter. → 721 rows. These people carry `geo_scope = outside_target_countries` so they can be filtered out.

## Ranking and selection (deterministic, `build_leads.py`)

The person must be *currently* at the target company (matched on the current-job company LinkedIn URL). Title score: persona titles (channel/dealer marketing, dealer development, co-op, distributor marketing…) 100 → `A_persona_title`; marketing-communications/brand 80 → `B_marcom_brand`; other marketing leaders 60 → `C_marketing_lead`; +10 for director/head/VP/CMO, +5 for manager/lead; −10 product/field marketing; −15 demand-gen/performance/SEO/social/CRM/events; sales-only "channel" titles scored down to 45 (excluded); the brief's wrong-contact titles (analytics, compliance, procurement, purchasing, VMO, finance, HR, legal, IT, engineering, supply chain, logistics, quality, designers/photographers…) are dropped. **Minimum score 60; top 2 per company.**

**Outreach-depth rule (CLAUDE.md):** this segment is large (1,502 accounts in Q1–Q3, ~1,290 in Q1+Q2), a large TAM/SAM, so **fewer contacts per company: ≤2**, rather than the 4–6 used on the ~25-account Named Accounts config.

## Result

- 1,044 leads / 727 companies (48% of the 1,502): `A_persona_title` 117 · `B_marcom_brand` 171 · `C_marketing_lead` 756. Target countries 744, outside 300. By company tier: Q1 154 · Q2 779 · Q3 116.
- **775 companies have no lead** (no Manager+ marketing person on LinkedIn/Blitz at that company). Gaps to fill with Prospeo person search (credits remain) or a title-wider pass if wanted.
- Most contacts are tier-`C` marketing leads, not exact channel/dealer-marketing owners; only ~11% of leads hit a persona title. Smaller OEMs rarely have a named channel-marketing role, so the marketing head is the realistic contact. If the client wants *only* persona titles, filter `persona_fit = A_persona_title`.

## Caveats

- Titles/levels come from LinkedIn via Blitz and can be stale; a person's "current" job is as of Blitz's last refresh.
- `A_persona_title` rows include some Dealer *Development* titles that sit in sales organizations (still the persona's "Dealer Development Manager").
- DNC check is company-level only (done at the company stage); no person-level suppression list has been applied. Nothing has been pushed to HeyReach/Clay.
