### 2. Contacts: Blitz API `POST /v2/search/people`, `company.linkedin_url` ≤5 per call, `max_results: 50`, cursor-paged
- **Pass A (in-country):** `people.job_title.include` (FTS) = Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, Chief Executive Officer, President, Owner, Chairman, Managing Director, General Manager, Division President, Group President, Business Unit President, Division Head, Head of Division, Executive Director; `exclude` = Assistant, Intern, Student, Associate, Retired, Advisor, Board Member, Non-Executive; `location.country_code` = US, CA, GB, IE, AU, NZ, DE. 10,524 raw rows (FTS "President" also matches every VP, hence the scoring below).
- **Pass B (worldwide):** same, no country filter, only for the 463 accounts without a ≥75-scoring executive in pass A. 1,656 raw rows. Rows from this pass or located outside those 7 countries carry `geo_scope = outside_target_countries`.

### 3. Ranking and selection (deterministic: `cc_rank.py`, `cc_build.py`)
Person must be *currently* at the target company (current-job company LinkedIn URL match). Title score: **100** founder / co-founder / CEO / owner / chairman · **90** president (incl. "President & GM") · **85** managing director · **80** division / group / business-unit head · **75** chair of the board only · **70** general manager (incl. VP & GM). Rejected: VP-level titles (unless GM), assistants/PAs ("PA to CEO"), committees, product/process/project owners, dealers/distributors, functional presidents/GMs (Sales, Marketing, Finance, Service…). Regional presidents/GMs ("President, North America") are kept. Names must be present and plausible. Local executives get +10 when scores tie.
**One lead per account.** **Exception:** accounts with ≥5,000 employees get a second lead if it's a distinct division-level head (score ≥70), because a single group CEO is not the realistic relationship at a Caterpillar-sized firm. (Large TAM ⇒ fewer contacts per account, per CLAUDE.md; hand-picked/relationship campaign ⇒ top of the org only.)
`shortlist_500 = yes` marks the **500 accounts** with the highest `handpick = account_points×20 + employees/1000 (capped) + title_score/10`; these are the "small, hand-picked" starting list. The rest is reserve for continuous low-volume running.
