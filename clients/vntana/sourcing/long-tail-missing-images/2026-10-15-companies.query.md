Trigger per brief: products on the company's own site showing "image coming soon" or sharing a photo with another SKU; deep catalogs (1,000+ SKUs or 50+ variants/family); contacts = product marketing, eCommerce content, PIM.

## Steps
1. Candidates: `icp-universe-na-weu` IN companies tagged pumps/valves, hydraulics/pneumatics/power transmission, components/fasteners/consumables, electrical/automation or machine tools/tooling, with a domain -> 7,985.
2. Pass 1 crawl (own Python crawler, browser UA, 70 threads): homepage + catalog/product links, up to 14 pages/site; flags placeholder images (src/alt such as coming-soon, no-image, image-not-available, placeholder) and "image coming soon / not available" text, and images shared by 3+ pages. 6,179 reachable; 261 with explicit placeholders; 994 with 3+ shared images -> 1,152 deep candidates.
3. Pass 2 (deep): sitemap(s) -> product URLs; 40 random product pages per site; per page checks placeholder or an `og:image`/first content image shared with 1+ other sampled pages (site-wide default images excluded when shared by more than 25% of the sample). 746 sized sites.
4. Qualifying: tier A = >=100 product URLs in sitemap and (>=1 placeholder product or >=15% of sampled pages flagged) -> 174 companies. Tier B = explicit placeholder seen in pass 1 but no sitemap to size the catalog -> 144 companies.
5. `number` for message copy: tier A = flagged share of sample x product URLs in sitemap (an ESTIMATE, rounded); tier B = placeholders actually observed on crawled pages (a lower bound). `number_basis` states which. Itemized sample: `2026-10-15-itemized-missing-images.csv` (2,460 flagged product pages with family, issue, image URL) — this is only the sampled pages, not the full list the message promises.
6. People: Blitz `POST /v2/search/employee-finder` (VP/Director/Manager; marketing, engineering, sales, operations, general mgmt; US/CA/GB/CH/NO/EU27), title-classified: T1 eCommerce / PIM / product data+content / catalog / digital content, T2 product marketing / marketing communications; up to 3 per company; Prospeo `POST /search-person` gap-fill by domain (domain-matched only).

## Limits
