# Query — 2026-09-15 leads snapshot #5 (Open Check push, "at least 5,000" request)

**Tool:** Blitz API `POST /v2/search/companies` + `POST /v2/search/people`
**Purpose:** Repo owner asked to add "atleast 5000 prospects" to the HeyReach Open Check
campaign (`601752`, "US & Europe | Open Check | General"). This snapshot documents the
methodology, the actual outcome, and why the 5,000 target was not reachable within
VNTANA's defined buyer persona.

## Step 1 — exhaust the remaining company pool

Before widening the people-search, the three VNTANA sourcing configs with unexhausted
Blitz cursors were paginated to completion (see the `2026-09-15-companies-2.csv` /
`-companies-3.csv` files and their `.query.md`s in `aftermarket-oems/`,
`casual-connections/`, and `ivt-expo-off-highway/`). Key finding: Blitz's `total_results`
estimate is **not reliable** — actual retrievable company counts were far below what
`total_results` reported in every case (confirmed only by cursor reaching `null`).

Result: a combined pool of **10,313 unique companies** spanning every VNTANA sourcing
config (built from every native `company_linkedin_url` already in the repo, the three
newly-exhausted configs, and DiscoLike tag-reconstructed URLs for the B2B eCommerce OEMs
and original Aftermarket OEMs configs).

## Step 2 — widen the people-search job_level

All prior rounds this session only searched `job_level: ["C-Team","Director","VP"]`.
This round added **"Manager"** for the first time across the entire 10,313-company pool —
the primary lever available without diluting the persona further. `job_level: "Staff"` or
`"Other"` was deliberately **not** added: VNTANA's own client-defined buying-committee ICP
(from `tracking_clients.get_client`) specifies VP/Director/CDO-level buyers, and dropping
to individual-contributor level would put mostly non-decision-makers into the campaign.

**Request body (per batch of 50 companies):**
```json
{
  "company_linkedin_urls": ["<batch of up to 50 URLs>"],
  "job_level": ["C-Team", "Director", "Manager", "VP"],
  "job_function": ["Advertising & Marketing", "General Business & Management",
                   "Operations", "Customer/Client Service"]
}
```
207 batches run (10,313 companies ÷ 50/batch) → **10,350 raw people** returned.

## Step 3 — quality filter

Applied the consolidated `filter_mega.py` pipeline (same categories used all session):
`BAD_SIGNALS` (retired/former/job-seeker), `EXCLUDE_TITLE_WORDS` (board members, C-suite
finance/compliance roles, retail/store management — including French board-member titles
after a prior round's near-miss), `OFFTOPIC_SIGNALS` (pharma/biotech/medical), a
`RELEVANT_KEYWORDS` allowlist (marketing/digital/ecommerce/aftermarket/product/customer
experience titles), `DNC_DOMAINS` (VNTANA's `tracking_clients` DNC list), a `BAD_COMPANIES`
denylist (~150 known-bad companies accumulated across every round this session, now
normalized consistently via `re.sub(r'[^a-z0-9 &]','',...)` on both sides to fix a prior
period-stripping asymmetry bug), and a new `DEALER_KEYWORDS` list matched directly against
company names (dealer/dealership/rental/distributor/wholesale/tire/forklift/etc.).
Deduped against `linkedin_url` and against all 972 already-contacted URLs from this
client's prior 4 leads snapshots.

Result: **1,202 qualified leads**.

## Step 4 — two-stage industry-tag contamination scrub

1. Cross-referenced each lead's `company_linkedin_url` against a repo-wide
   `industry → company` map (10,185 companies). Excluded all leads whose company is
   tagged `Motor Vehicle Manufacturing` / `Motor Vehicle Parts Manufacturing` /
   `Automotive` / `Transportation Equipment Manufacturing` **unless** on the verified
   16-company heavy-truck allowlist (Daimler Truck, MAN Truck & Bus, TRATON GROUP, Volvo
   Trucks, Iveco, Scania México/Australia, Battle Motors, Wrightbus, NFI Parts, Midwest
   Transit Equipment, New Way Trucks, Model 1 Commercial Vehicles, Humbaur, Detroit
   Chassis, Chereau). → removed 464 leads (738 remain).
2. Cross-referenced against the `Wholesale Building Materials` tag (mostly legitimate
   manufacturers, but hides distributors) and manually excluded 10 confirmed distributors
   (CEDEO, hagebau, Howarth Timber, Dick's Lumber, POINT.P, Quéguiner, Union-Matériaux,
   US LBM, Redi Carpet). → 728 remain.
3. Manually excluded "Brandt Group of Companies" / "Brandt Positioning Technology" (4
   entries) — a Canadian John Deere/heavy-equipment dealer network that matched no
   automated keyword. → **724 leads** ready for batch-level review.

## Step 5 — manual batch-by-batch review before each push (per CLAUDE.md: document as you go)

All 724 leads were split into 8 batches of ≤100 and each batch was read and manually
reviewed immediately before its push, catching contamination the automated filters missed.
New categories caught this round (on top of the categories above): consumer/luxury
automotive brands (Lamborghini, Kia Europe, Stellantis México, Volvo Cars, Mazda
Philippines), Caterpillar/JCB/Bobcat dealer networks not matching generic dealer keywords
(MacAllister Machinery, Riggs Cat, Warren Cat, Toromont Cat, Puckett Machinery, TC Harrison
Group, Vegusa Maquinaria), industrial distributors hiding under manufacturer-sounding names
(SunSource, Spartan Surfaces, MAD Elevator, RB Global/Ritchie Bros equipment auctions),
service bureaus (Quickparts — on-demand 3D-printing manufacturing service, not a product
manufacturer), consulting/systems-integration firms (Grantek, revaine), marketing/events
agencies (DCC Marketing, Duality Marketing Solutions, TWIY Marketing, Haflaty, ideazmeet),
off-ICP institutions and companies (IATA trade association, McLaren Racing, Shanghai
Baozun e-commerce agency, CIMCON Software, Chime, CrossCountry Mortgage, Stronghold
Digital Mining, Agos consumer finance, M Markovitz Ltd clothing wholesaler, Lovell
Partnerships homebuilder, LoneStar Group equipment rental), and one automotive-adjacent EV
battery JV (ACC - Automotive Cells Company). One data-quality fix: a lead's name fields
contained CJK characters mixed with a Latin name (Owens Corning's "徐可捷 Ella XU") —
corrected to "Ella" / "Xu" so HeyReach/Clay personalization renders in English.

Total removed across all 8 batch reviews: 64 leads (724 → 660 pushed net).

## Result

- **661 leads attempted** across 8 `add_leads_to_campaign` calls to campaign `601752`.
- **660 accepted** (1 was already present in the campaign as a duplicate and was
  rejected by HeyReach itself — not a data-quality miss on this end).
- Campaign `totalUsers` went **930 → 1,590**, confirmed via `get_campaign(601752)`.
- This CSV (`2026-09-15-leads-5.csv`) contains all 661 attempted leads, in the same
  format as prior snapshots (`linkedin_url` first, `-` for blanks, `relevance_score`
  always `-` since it isn't computed by this pipeline).

## Why "at least 5,000" was not reached

The addressable company universe for VNTANA's relevant industries via Blitz is now
**exhausted** — pagination on every remaining config hit `cursor: null` this round,
capping the pool at 10,313 unique companies. Even after widening `job_level` to include
"Manager" (the broadest expansion possible without dropping below VNTANA's own
client-defined VP/Director/CDO buying-committee persona) and applying the full contamination
scrub, the realistic qualified-and-deduped yield topped out at 724 leads, of which 660
were net-new and clean enough to push. This is a hard ceiling of the current data sources
and persona definition, not a stopping-short — closing the gap to 5,000 would require
either a second sourcing tool (e.g. AI Ark API, confirmed live but unused this round),
broader industries/geography, or accepting a persona dilution (Staff-level titles) that
was deliberately avoided to protect campaign quality.
