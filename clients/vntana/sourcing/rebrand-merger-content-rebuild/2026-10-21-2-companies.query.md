## Limits
- **Precision:** events were extracted from headlines and Exa summaries by a small model without opening the articles. Spot checks show clear misses (an executive-appointment story tagged as a rebrand; a subsidiary news item credited to its parent). Parent/subsidiary and name-collision calls are judgement. Treat A/C rows as leads to verify, not confirmed facts.
- **Recall:** the second sweep's articles were filtered by headline/industrial keywords first; roughly 24,000 of its 32,755 new articles were not read. Exa news skews to press-release wires; company-site announcements are underrepresented.
- **"Acquisition bringing multiple product brands together"** is not separately verified; any closed manufacturer-to-manufacturer deal counts (188 of the net-new). A stricter brand-portfolio read would cut that figure.
- Prospeo revenue is banded and its name matching missed or mis-matched some companies (645 net-new not found). Tier C rows have employee counts only.
- Tools used: Exa `/search`, Prospeo `/search-company`, Haiku extraction agents. Blitz/AI Ark/DiscoLike/ColdIQ not used.
