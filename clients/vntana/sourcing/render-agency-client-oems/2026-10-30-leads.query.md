# Query — 2026-10-30 leads snapshot (Render campaign contacts)

**Config:** `Industrial OEMs paying an outside render agency, with recent launch / show / PIM project`, UUID **`7166f187-d30d-4d52-86be-79ba8f4f3dbf`**. **Feeds** HeyReach campaigns `693232` / `693233` (lists `1082187` / `1082188`). **Output:** `2026-10-30-leads.csv`, **297 people at 99 of the 158 accounts**, keyed by person `linkedin_url` (first column), deduped on it. Contacts only; no emails or phones pulled; nothing pushed to HeyReach or Clay.

## Tool and pass
Blitz API `POST /v2/search/people`, `company.linkedin_url` = one verified company page per call (up to 3 pages per account), `people.job_title.include` in three groups (A: product marketing, marketing communications, eCommerce, digital experience/transformation, 3D visualization; B: aftermarket and service; C: director/head of marketing, CIO/CTO, business architecture, business development), `exclude` = Security, Cyber, CFO, Chief Financial, Finance, HR, Legal, Compliance, Procurement, Purchasing, Sales Representative, Account Executive, Account Manager, Intern, Student, Assistant, Retired, Advisor, Board Member; `location.country_code` = US, CA, GB, IE, DE, FR, NL, SE, IT, ES, CH, DK, NO, FI, BE, AT (Ashley: North America and Europe). The title list is the approved include/exclude set from the VNTANA Slack channel (Ashley, Jul 10 and Aug 28), plus family keywords. A first batched pull (5 companies per call) hit Blitz's 400-row cap on 7 batches and crowded out smaller companies, so the final pull is one company page per call.

## Ranking (deterministic, `build.py`)
The person must be *currently* at the target company (current-job company LinkedIn URL or domain match). Title score: 100 product marketing / marketing leader / eCommerce / digital experience and transformation; 80 aftermarket, digital product, CIO/CTO/technology; 65 creative, growth marketing, business development, product/operations manager; +10 for VP/Head/Director/Chief/Global titles, +5 for manager. Rejected: security, cyber, finance, HR/people/talent, legal, compliance, procurement, sales-rep titles, analysts, engineers, developers, consultants, interns, assistants, retired, advisors, and any aerospace/defense/military title. **Minimum score 75, top 4 per account** (CLAUDE.md: small TAM, so more contacts per account; capped because many accounts are very large groups).

## Result
297 leads at 99 accounts (4 per account at 55, 1-3 at the rest); 134 accounts had a verified page and 35 of those had no qualifying contact (small, non-NA/EU, or no matching titles). 144 in the US; the rest Europe. 284 leads belong to accounts with `render_copy_ready = yes`. The `product`, `category` and `demo_link` columns are the three custom variables the approved Render copy needs.

## Caveats
- Titles and "current job" come from LinkedIn via Blitz and can be stale.
- Where two account names share one company page (Stanley Black & Decker, ABB and ABB Robotics), contacts attach to one account only.
- No person-level DNC or earlier-campaign suppression was applied (company-level DNC only). Check overlap with earlier VNTANA leads files before loading.
