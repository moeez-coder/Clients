## Processing

- **Deduped on `linkedin_member_id`** (HeyReach's `linkedin_id`), which maps 1:1 to the profile URL: 10,600 → **10,261 unique prospects**. 280 people were in more than one list: 230 in two, 43 in three, 5 in four, 2 in five. Their lists are joined in `source_lists`, and `source_list_count` gives the number. Overlap happens because a person's company can fall into more than one Sales Nav size or public/private filter.
- `linkedin_url` is HeyReach's `profileUrl`, normalized to end in `/`. 1,493 rows have a Sales Nav-style member-ID URL (`/in/ACoAA…`) rather than a readable vanity URL. The URL still works and is unique, but it won't string-match vanity URLs held in other files.
- `in_prior_vntana_leads`: **103 prospects** already appear, by normalized URL, in an earlier VNTANA `*leads*.csv` in this repo. The column names the segment folder(s) they appear in. They are flagged here, not removed.
- Line breaks inside headlines were flattened to spaces, giving one line per lead. Empty fields are written as `-`.
- Dropped from the output: `imageUrl`, `about` (mostly a copy of the headline), and the always-empty email, tag and custom fields.
