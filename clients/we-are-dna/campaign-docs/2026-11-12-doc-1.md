# We Are DNA Campaign Plan, Doc 1

This plan covers ten LinkedIn campaigns, built from your ICP form.

Campaigns 1 to 5 are sector campaigns, one per sector. Each one starts from a board-level event that is already public, such as an acquisition programme, a results statement that names people costs, or a gender pay gap report. Events like these usually point to an attraction and retention problem, and that is exactly what your EVP and Employer Brand work fixes. We speak to the CEO and the most senior people leaders, we never lead with job adverts, and we never offer candidates.

Campaigns 6 to 10 reach the same five sectors through a different buying signal from your ICP form:

- new private equity investment
- a new CEO or people leader
- employer reputation pain
- growth, M&A or a rebrand
- a careers site that needs work

Across every campaign:

- **Geography:** United Kingdom, companies headquartered in the UK.
- **Company size:** 201+ employees as the core, with 51 to 200 included where the sector fits.
- **Offer:** the messages below use a free AI visibility audit as the starting offer. We show the prospect what ChatGPT, Google's AI answers and similar tools tell candidates about them as an employer, and where that differs from the story they want to tell. Six other offers are set out at the end of this doc for your thoughts, and we can swap any of them in.
- **Goal:** a qualified discovery call with your team.
- **Proof points used:**
  - MAN Truck & Bus UK, £1m a year saved
  - DHU Healthcare, over £2m added to the bottom line in 20 months
  - Glendola, unwanted staff turnover cut by over 50%
  - boohoo and Pets at Home, over £600k a year saved each
  - Superdry, over £500k a year saved
  - 100+ clients since 2010, NPS of 88
- **Excluded from every list:** your current clients and anything on your do-not-contact list. Your ICP form mentions a do-not-contact file, but it has not reached our system, so please send it again. We also need your current client list (see "Before launch" at the end).
