## Campaign 4: Casual Connections, Bristol Business Leaders

### ICP

- **Market:** all five of your sectors: health and care, retail and hospitality, manufacturing and engineering, law and accounting, and financial services.
- **Segment:** private sector employers with 51 to 5,000 staff, headquartered in Bristol.
- **Why this one is different:** this is not a sales campaign. John and Eoin connect, as themselves, with the people running businesses in their own city. There is no pitch. The aim is a wider network of local leaders who know who DNA is, so that when they think about their people or their brand, they think of you.
- **Size:** 312 companies in the first wave. After that we move on to the rest of the South West (about 1,000 more), then nationally.

### Persona

- **Who we connect with:** the CEO, Managing Director, Founder or Managing Partner first.
- **At firms with 200+ staff:** also one other member of the C-suite, either the Chief People Officer, COO, CFO or CMO.
- **Who sends:** John and Eoin each send from their own LinkedIn profile.

### Signal and data

- **Signal:** a shared city. There is no trigger event. The reason to connect is that they lead a business in Bristol, where DNA is based. We take this from each company's registered headquarters in a business database.
- **Data:** of the companies that fit your profile, 312 are headquartered in Bristol. In a test on the 20 largest, we found about one confirmed, UK-based C-suite leader per company. That suggests a first wave of about 300 people.
- **Pace:** LinkedIn limits how many invitations a person can send. At around 100 a week each, John and Eoin together cover the first wave in about two weeks. That leaves time to see how it lands before we go wider.

### Lead list

- **Source:** a UK company and people database, cross-checked against a second source. Only people whose current title is in the C-suite and who are based in the UK are kept.
- **Volume:** one leader per company, or two at firms with 200+ staff. When the same person hears from both John and Eoin it stops feeling casual, so we keep it light.
- **Filtered out:**
  - your do-not-contact list
  - anyone already in another campaign
  - charities and public bodies
  - people whose title only looks senior, such as "Owner" used by fee earners, or the global CEO of an overseas parent company

### Messaging

No offer leads this campaign, by design. It is a personal connection, not an approach. If someone replies and wants to talk, John or Eoin takes it from there in their own words.

**Connection request, from John**

Hi {first_name}, I founded DNA here in Bristol back in 2010 and I like to know the people running good businesses locally. Would be good to connect. John

**Connection request, from Eoin**

Hi {first_name}, I joined DNA in Bristol as MD this summer and I'm keen to get to know more people leading businesses in the area. Good to connect. Eoin

**Message 1, the day after they accept**

Thanks for connecting, {first_name}. Always good to know more people at the helm of businesses round here. Hope things are going well at {company} heading into the new year.

No further messages are sent. Any conversation after this is one to one.
