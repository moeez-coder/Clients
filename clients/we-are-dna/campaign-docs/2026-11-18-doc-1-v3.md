# We Are DNA Campaign Plan, Doc 1

This plan covers ten LinkedIn campaigns, built from your ICP form.

Campaigns 1 to 3 lead the plan. They share one list: about 1,400 UK employers in your five sectors whose own staff rate them below average on public review sites. Each campaign speaks to a different person at the same company, the CEO, the people leader and the talent lead, with a different promise, so we learn which one books the most calls.

Campaign 4 is different: John and Eoin connect, as themselves, with the leaders of businesses in Bristol. There is no pitch.

Campaigns 5 to 8 are sector campaigns, one per sector (law and accounting firms are reached through Campaigns 1 to 3). Each one starts from a board-level event that is already public, such as an acquisition programme, a results statement that names people costs, or a gender pay gap report. Events like these usually point to an attraction and retention problem, and that is exactly what your EVP and Employer Brand work fixes. We speak to the CEO and the most senior people leaders, we never lead with job adverts, and we never offer candidates.

Campaigns 9 and 10 reach the same five sectors through a different buying signal from your ICP form:

- new private equity investment
- a new CEO or people leader

Across every campaign:

- **Geography:** United Kingdom, companies headquartered in the UK.
- **Company size:** 201+ employees as the core, with 51 to 200 included where the sector fits.
- **Offer:** Campaigns 1 to 3 start with a free reputation snapshot (Offer 8), which includes what AI tools tell candidates about the company. Campaigns 5 to 10 use a free AI visibility audit as the starting offer, and Campaign 4 has no offer by design. We show the prospect what ChatGPT, Google's AI answers and similar tools tell candidates about them as an employer, and where that differs from the story they want to tell. Eight other offers are set out at the end of this doc for your thoughts, and we can swap any of them in.
- **Goal:** a qualified discovery call with your team.
- **Proof points used:**
  - MAN Truck & Bus UK, £1m a year saved, with attrition cut from 20% to under 10% and agency spend from over £600k to under £30k within a year
  - DHU Healthcare, over £2m added to the bottom line in 20 months
  - Glendola, unwanted staff turnover cut by over 50%
  - boohoo and Pets at Home, over £600k a year saved each
  - Superdry, over £500k a year saved
  - 100+ clients since 2010, NPS of 88
- **Excluded from every list:** your current clients and anything on your do-not-contact list. Your ICP form mentions a do-not-contact file, but it has not reached our system, so please send it again. We also need your current client list (see "Before launch" at the end).

## Campaign 1: Employer Reputation, the Cost of Turnover, CEOs and Managing Directors

### ICP

- **Market:** All five sectors: health, care and veterinary; retail and hospitality; manufacturing and engineering; law and accounting; financial services.
- **Segment:** private-sector UK employers with 51 to 5,000 staff.
- **Signal:** staff rate the company noticeably below average on public employer review sites, on at least 30 reviews. That means an overall rating under 3.5, or culture and values or senior leadership under 3.3. Campaigns 1 to 3 share this list, and each speaks to a different person at the same company.
- **Size:** about 1,400 companies.

### Persona

CEO or Managing Director, and the Chief Operating Officer or Chief Financial Officer at larger groups. You told us you sell to the C-suite, so this campaign talks about money rather than people metrics.

### Signal and data

- **Signal:** public employer review ratings from the last 12 months, at least 30 reviews per company, checked in November 2026.
- **Data:** we checked 29,489 UK employers in your five sectors. 1,796 fall below the bar, and we are confident of the match for 1,384 of them. The rest are held back until we have checked each one by hand.
  - **By sector:** retail and hospitality 560, financial services 329, manufacturing and engineering 191, health, care and veterinary 173, law and accounting 131.
  - **By size:** 51 to 200 staff 389, 201 to 500 staff 375, 501 to 1,000 staff 250, 1,001 to 5,000 staff 370.
- **What the list looks like:** a typical company on it is rated 3.2 overall and 2.8 for senior leadership, and fewer than half of its staff would recommend it to a friend. Senior leadership is the weakest area at almost half of them.
- **Freshness:** ratings move slowly, so the list stays useful for months. We re-check it before each wave.

### Lead list

## Campaign 4: Casual Connections, Bristol Business Leaders

### ICP

- **Market:** all five of your sectors: health and care, retail and hospitality, manufacturing and engineering, law and accounting, and financial services.
- **Segment:** private sector employers with 51 to 5,000 staff, headquartered in Bristol.
- **Why this one is different:** this is not a sales campaign. John and Eoin connect, as themselves, with the people running businesses in their own city. There is no pitch. The aim is a wider network of local leaders who know who DNA is, so that when they think about their people or their brand, they think of you.
- **Size:** 312 companies in the first wave. After that we move on to the rest of the South West (about 1,000 more), then nationally.

### Persona

- **Who we connect with:** the CEO, Managing Director, Founder or Managing Partner first.
- **At firms with 200+ staff:** also one other member of the C-suite, either the Chief People Officer, COO, CFO or CMO.
- **Who sends:** John and Eoin each send from their own LinkedIn profile.

### Signal and data

- **Signal:** a shared city. There is no trigger event. The reason to connect is that they lead a business in Bristol, where DNA is based. We take this from each company's registered headquarters in a business database.
- **Data:** of the companies that fit your profile, 312 are headquartered in Bristol. In a test on the 20 largest, we found about one confirmed, UK-based C-suite leader per company. That suggests a first wave of about 300 people.
- **Pace:** LinkedIn limits how many invitations a person can send. At around 100 a week each, John and Eoin together cover the first wave in about two weeks. That leaves time to see how it lands before we go wider.

### Lead list

- **Source:** a UK company and people database, cross-checked against a second source. Only people whose current title is in the C-suite and who are based in the UK are kept.
- **Volume:** one leader per company, or two at firms with 200+ staff. When the same person hears from both John and Eoin it stops feeling casual, so we keep it light.
- **Filtered out:**
  - your do-not-contact list
  - anyone already in another campaign
  - charities and public bodies
  - people whose title only looks senior, such as "Owner" used by fee earners, or the global CEO of an overseas parent company

### Messaging

No offer leads this campaign, by design. It is a personal connection, not an approach. If someone replies and wants to talk, John or Eoin takes it from there in their own words.

**Connection request, from John**

Hi {first_name}, I founded DNA here in Bristol back in 2010 and I like to know the people running good businesses locally. Would be good to connect. John

**Connection request, from Eoin**

Hi {first_name}, I joined DNA in Bristol as MD this summer and I'm keen to get to know more people leading businesses in the area. Good to connect. Eoin

**Message 1, the day after they accept**

Thanks for connecting, {first_name}. Always good to know more people at the helm of businesses round here. Hope things are going well at {company} heading into the new year.

No further messages are sent. Any conversation after this is one to one.
