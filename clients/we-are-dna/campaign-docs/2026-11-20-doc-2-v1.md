# We Are DNA Campaign Plan, Doc 2

This plan covers five LinkedIn campaigns, built from your ICP form. It sits alongside Doc 1 (Nov 12, 2026), which holds the employer reputation campaigns, Casual Connections and the health and care buy-and-build campaign. In Doc 1 these five were Campaigns 6 to 10; nothing in them has changed except their numbers.

Campaigns 1 to 3 are sector campaigns, one per sector: retail and hospitality, manufacturing and engineering, and financial services (health and care is Doc 1's Campaign 5, and law and accounting firms are reached through Doc 1's Campaigns 1 to 3). Each one starts from a board-level event that is already public, such as a results statement that names people costs, or a gender pay gap report. Events like these usually point to an attraction and retention problem, and that is exactly what your EVP and Employer Brand work fixes. We speak to the CEO and the most senior people leaders, we never lead with job adverts, and we never offer candidates.

Campaigns 4 and 5 reach the same five sectors through a different buying signal from your ICP form:

- new private equity investment
- a new CEO or people leader

Across every campaign:

- **Geography:** United Kingdom, companies headquartered in the UK.
- **Company size:** 201+ employees as the core, with 51 to 200 included where the sector fits.

Eight other offers are set out at the end of this doc for your thoughts, and we can swap any of them in.

  - DHU Healthcare, over £2m added to the bottom line in 20 months

  - boohoo and Pets at Home, over £600k a year saved each

  - 100+ clients since 2010, NPS of 88

## Campaign 1: People Costs on the Board Agenda, Retail and Hospitality

### ICP

- **Market:** Retail, leisure and hospitality.
- **Segment:** UK retailers, restaurant and pub groups, hotel groups and contract caterers with large frontline workforces. Examples of the type: Greene King, Mountain Warehouse, Village Hotels, BaxterStorey.
- **Signal:** in the last 12 months the company has named labour costs, staff turnover or retention as a pressure or principal risk in its annual report, results or a trading update, or has relaunched its customer brand without changing how it presents itself as an employer. With wage floors and employer National Insurance rising, staff turnover is now a margin question for every board in the sector.
- **Size:** about 3,280 UK companies in these sectors have 201+ employees. We expect several hundred to meet the signal. Listed and private equity backed groups publish the most, so we start with them.

### Persona

CEO or Managing Director, Chief Operating Officer, and Chief People Officer or People Director. Where the trigger is a customer rebrand, the Chief Marketing Officer too.

### Lead list

- **Source:** annual reports, results statements, trading updates and rebrand announcements, matched to company LinkedIn pages.
- **Volume:** 2 contacts per company: the CEO or COO, and the most senior people leader. The pool is large, so we stay focused at each account.
- **Filtered out:** franchise units with no central team, recruitment and staffing agencies, and your existing clients, including Superdry, AllSaints, boohoo group, Pandora, Joules, Pets at Home and Glendola.
