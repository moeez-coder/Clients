**Subject:** Re: Campaign doc, revised approach

Hi John,

Thanks for the quick and honest feedback. You're right on both points, and we've reworked the plan.

**No open roles anywhere.** We've taken the job-post angle out completely. Nothing in the outreach now mentions roles, vacancies or candidates, so there's no chance of being mistaken for a recruitment agency.

**Aimed at the C-suite and just below.** Every campaign now goes to CEOs and MDs, Chief People Officers, HR and People Directors, COOs and, for law and accounting, Managing Partners. Each one opens on something that is already on the board's agenda:

1. **Health, care, vet and dental groups growing by acquisition.** One employer story across every acquired site, with DHU's £2m result as the proof.
2. **Retail and hospitality groups that have named labour costs or staff turnover in their results.** Retention as a margin lever, with Glendola, Superdry and boohoo as the proof.
3. **Manufacturing and engineering businesses with a large gender pay gap.** Every employer with 250+ staff publishes one. We use it as an attraction problem rather than a pay problem, with MAN's £1m as the proof.
4. **Top 100 law and accounting firms after a merger, private equity deal or change of managing partner.** What it means to work there now.
5. **Financial services firms with a large gender pay gap or Women in Finance Charter targets.** Senior attraction and retention.

Campaigns 6 to 10 stay as trigger campaigns: private equity investment, a new CEO or people leader, employer reputation, growth or rebrand, and the careers site. They have also been moved up to the same senior audience.
