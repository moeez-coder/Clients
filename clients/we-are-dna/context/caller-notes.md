# Caller briefing

We Are DNA — wearedna.co.uk

A Bristol creative agency that helps companies attract, keep and engage staff, and win customers, through branding, careers sites and internal communications.

WHO THEY SELL TO
Heads and Directors of Talent Acquisition, Employer Brand, HR and People, Internal Comms, plus Marketing and Brand Directors. Medium-to-large UK employers with recognisable names and sizeable workforces — retail and fashion, financial services, automotive, utilities, tech.

WHAT THEY SELL
- **Employer branding**: they research what it is actually like to work at your company, write the employee promise (the EVP), then launch it to staff and candidates. Run on their **BrandShift** method, which covers the whole employee journey, not a one-off campaign.
- Careers sites, recruitment advertising and social ads, advice on your recruitment software, and a newer piece checking how your employer brand shows up in AI search answers.
- Employee engagement and marketing: onboarding, internal comms, engagement surveys, culture and values work, plus brand identity and customer website builds.

WHY A PROSPECT TAKES THE CALL
Roles sit open too long, new hires leave inside a year, and the careers page says nothing about what the place is really like. In most companies the talent side and the marketing side also run separately, so candidates and customers get two different stories.

WORTH KNOWING
Trading since 2010, **100+ clients, NPS of 88**. Public case studies include Superdry, AllSaints, boohoo group, Pandora, Pets at Home and MAN Truck & Bus UK.

# Caller FAQs

Q: What is this about?
A: I'm calling on behalf of We Are DNA, a Bristol agency that does employer branding and employee engagement. In plain terms, they help companies work out what makes them worth joining, then put that in front of candidates and staff. I wanted to see if it's worth a short intro call.

Q: Who are you, are you We Are DNA?
A: No, I work with them on the outreach side. I'm the one who messaged you on LinkedIn. **I'm not the person who does the work** — my job is just to see if it's relevant and get you in front of John and the team.

Q: How did you get my details?
A: From your LinkedIn profile. You came up because of your role in talent and people, which is exactly who they usually work with. If it's not relevant, just say and I'll leave you alone.

Q: What does it cost?
A: It depends completely on scope — a careers site is a different thing to a full employer brand programme. **That's exactly what the first call is for**, they'll tell you straight once they understand what you're trying to fix.

Q: We already do this in-house, why would we need you?
A: A lot of their clients have internal teams too — they tend to come in for the research and the heavy lift rather than replacing anyone. Worth putting that to them directly on the call so they can be honest about whether there's a gap.

Q: We already have an agency for this.
A: Fair enough, most people at your level do. The thing that usually makes them worth a look is **they handle both the employer brand and the customer brand under one roof**, so the two aren't telling different stories. Thirty minutes to compare is all I'm asking for.

Q: Who else do you work with?
A: Publicly named work includes Superdry, AllSaints, boohoo group, Pandora, Pets at Home and MAN Truck & Bus UK. They've helped **over 100 clients since 2010 and have an NPS of 88**. They'll walk you through the relevant case studies on the call.

Q: What if it doesn't work?
A: They start with a diagnostic and research rather than guessing, so you see the findings before anyone builds anything. The specifics of how they scope and measure it is a question for John — I'd rather he answered that properly than me guess.

Q: How long before we'd see anything?
A: Depends on whether it's a careers site or a full EVP programme. Ask them on the call and they'll give you a realistic timeline for your situation — they're pretty blunt about that sort of thing.

Q: I'm not the decision maker.
A: No problem — who owns employer brand or internal comms your end? Happy to speak to them, or **you're welcome to sit in on the call too** if it's a shared decision.

Q: Just send me something instead.
A: I can do that, but what I send is generic and usually sits unread. Fifteen or twenty minutes with John and you'll know in the first five whether it's relevant. Shall I look at Tuesday or Thursday?

Q: Is this recruitment, are you selling me candidates?
A: No, **they don't place people and there's no placement fee**. They're a creative agency — they build the brand and the materials that make candidates want to apply to you in the first place.
