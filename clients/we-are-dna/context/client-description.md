## What they do

We Are DNA is a Bristol-based creative agency (telephone 0117 300 3000) that, by its own description, helps clients find, keep and engage the staff or customers they need through Employer Branding, Employee Engagement or Marketing Solutions. The agency frames its purpose as creating stronger brands and better businesses by finding, keeping and engaging the staff or the customers our clients need to be successful, and it has been operating in this space since 2010.

The work spans three service pillars:

- **Employer Branding** — The agency researches, develops and launches employer brands. As they put it, a good Employer Brand can save you money and make you money, and your Employer Brand needs to be compelling to those who will succeed and stay with you. Their stated method is to work with organisations in a number of ways: review your current Employer Brand via a diagnostic process, conduct research, develop your EVP and Employer Brand, and launch it internally and externally. This is underpinned by their trademarked **BrandShift™** methodology, which they describe as an approach that will help you unlock the potential inherent in your people and strive for a consistent and cohesive employment experience right across the employee lifecycle. Sub-services include EVP development, an Employer Brand Diagnostic, Candidate Attraction & Engagement, Careers Sites, ATS Consultancy, Social Recruiting, Employer Reputation, Employee Advocacy, Media Planning & Buying, and a newer **Employer Brand AI Visibility** offering.
- **Employee Engagement** — Covering onboarding and induction, reward and recognition, engagement surveys and strategy, vision/mission/values, health and wellbeing, learning and development, culture, internal comms and offboarding. The rationale given is that an employee engagement strategy can save you money and make you money.
- **Marketing & Strategy** — Strategy and consultancy, branding and identity, websites, customer attraction and engagement, and customer surveys. On websites, they state they design and build a website tailored to your business needs, beginning with what you want to achieve and planning the online customer journey accordingly, with no one-size-fits-all approach.

What makes them distinct is positioning as a single agency bridging both the *employer* audience (staff/candidates) and the *customer* audience (marketing), underpinned by the proprietary BrandShift™ lifecycle methodology. They emphasise an objectives-first, jargon-free approach — clients appreciate their straightforward approach, planning and lack of jargon, which they believe is the best way to work. Their values are stated plainly: firstly, do the right thing; secondly, never compromise on quality; and thirdly, always bring your best self, under a vision to create a buzz and make good stuff happen. They cite credentials including 100 clients helped over the years and a Net Promoter Score of 88 (surveyed May 2020).

## Who they target

We Are DNA targets medium-to-large employers and consumer brands that need to attract and retain talent or reach customers — primarily organisations with recognisable brands and sizeable workforces. Their published case studies name well-known clients including Superdry (EVP and employer brand development), MAN Truck & Bus UK (ROI and employer branding), AllSaints (careers site), boohoo group (EVP and employer brand), Pets at Home, Glendola, Pandora (social ad creator for a global talent team), Joules (online internal comms), Aldermore, Infinis, Opus Talent Solutions, ABD Solutions and Bath Building Society.

Across industries, the client base spans retail and fashion, financial services, automotive/industrial, recruitment/talent, utilities and SaaS/technology — for the websites discipline specifically, they have worked across the private, charity and education sectors and for companies of all sizes, including corporate sites, e-commerce, campaign microsites and student recruitment sites.

In terms of buyer roles, the services map clearly to **HR, talent acquisition and internal communications leaders** (employer branding, EVP, engagement, careers sites, ATS consultancy) and to **marketing and brand leaders** (branding and identity, websites, customer attraction and engagement). The enquiry form reinforces this split, routing contacts by subject into Employer branding, Employee engagement, Marketing, Vision Mission & Values, or Other.
