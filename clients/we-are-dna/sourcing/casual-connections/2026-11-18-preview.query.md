## Queries

1. **Blitz** `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword` per company (`preview_leads.py`), `max_results` 2, UK only, no emails:
   - Tier 1: CEO, Chief Executive Officer, Managing Director, Group Managing Director, Managing Partner, Senior Partner, Founder, Owner.
   - Tier 2: Chief People Officer, Chief Operating Officer, COO, Chief Financial Officer, CFO, Chief Marketing Officer, CMO, Chief HR Officer.
   - `exclude_title`: Assistant, Intern, PA to, Recruiter, Talent Acquisition, Sales, Non-Executive, Advisor, Board Member, Deputy, Regional, Area, Store, Branch, Site, Practice Manager, Account, Business Partner, People Partner, Product Owner, EA to, Recruitment, Finance Partner, HR Partner.
   - **30 Blitz records.** The first run, without the expanded excludes, used 24 records and was discarded: Blitz matched "Senior Partner" to "Senior People Partner" and "Owner" to "Product Owner".
   - Raw: `2026-11-18-preview-waterfall-raw.jsonl` and `2026-11-18-preview-blitz-raw.csv`.
2. **Clay MCP** `search-contacts`, 10 of the companies by domain:
   - Query: `select from people where experiences.any(is_current = true and job_title is_similar_to ("Chief Executive Officer", "Managing Partner", "Chief People Officer", "Chief Operating Officer", "Chief Financial Officer"))`.
   - Page 0, 20 contacts. Raw: `2026-11-18-preview-clay-raw.json`.
   - Two earlier calls failed with Clay's "Unexpected error running search DSL page". They used LinkedIn company URLs, a `location_country` filter and `limit 3 by clay_company_id`.
3. **Combine** (`build_preview.py`):
   - Dedupe on LinkedIn URL.
   - UK-located only.
   - Strict C-suite regex, plus a bad-title regex.
   - Max 2 per company, tier 1 first.
   - DNC check against `context/dnc-list.csv`.
   - Normalised with `tools/leads.py normalize --source blitz+clay`. The per-row provider is in the `source` column. `all_sources` reads blitz+clay on every row, because normalize takes one `--source`.

## TAM/SAM read and contacts per company (the inverse rule)

- **TAM:** 29,489 companies, which is **large**, so the inverse rule gives **fewer contacts per company: 1 to 2**. Tier 1 (CEO, MD or Managing Partner) goes first. A tier 2 (CPO, COO, CFO or CMO) is added only at 201+ staff, where the C-suite is real.
- **Why so few:** a founder-to-founder note works best one to one per company. Two people at the same company both getting "the founder wants to connect" reads as a campaign.
- **Capacity sets the wave, not the TAM** (see `campaign-docs/2026-11-18-casual-connections-plan.md`). At about 100 to 200 invitations a week per founder account, the whole TAM is a multi-year job. So the pull is done in waves, sized to sends, and nothing is bulk-pulled up front.
