## Method

1. **Companies:** `2026-11-23-companies.csv` (1,384; `build_companies.py`). 2 have no LinkedIn page (Scottish Autism, Gordon Murray) and were skipped, so 1,382 for C1 and C2 and 993 for C3.
2. **Blitz** `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword` (`full_pull_blitz.py`): one call per company per campaign, cascades in `personas.py`, `location.country_code = GB`, `include_headline_search = false`, `max_results 10`. 3,757 calls, 0 errors, **3,404 Blitz records**. Raw `2026-11-23-blitz-raw.jsonl`. Blitz-only stage outputs kept as `2026-11-23-blitzonly-*-stage.csv`.
3. **Clay MCP gap-fill** (`search-contacts`, workspace 770250) on companies Blitz left without a launch-set contact, 10 companies a call (`2026-11-23-clay-gaps.json`: C1/C2 union 971 companies in 98 batches; C3 899 in 90). **Stopped by Moe after 35 of 188 batches** (C1/C2 batches 0-12, C3 batches 0-21): each call returns ~20k tokens, so the full sweep was projected at ~3.5M more agent tokens. 285 contacts, in `2026-11-23-clay-raw.json`. Results that came back inline were transcribed by subagents through `clay_append.py --stdin`; a slug-vs-name check found 2 slugs without the name (`vptalent`, `kvgshannon`), both plausible vanity URLs. Clay added C1 +38, C2 +16 (+46 also found by Blitz), C3 +15 launch-set leads.
4. **Combine** (`full_build.py`): same person = same LinkedIn URL or same first + last name at the company; UK only; strict persona title regex; C1 drops divisional MDs ("Managing Director, Retail", 154) and plain "Managing Director" where it is a rank title (several at one firm, 118); a person already on Campaign 6's list (`../pe-investment-triggers/2026-11-16-3-leads.csv`) is `hold: also in Campaign 6` (1 in C1, 1 in C2). Then `tools/leads.py normalize` (4 / 3 / 1 alt-key duplicates collapsed).
5. **Variables:** `clean_first_name`, `clean_company` (`variables.py`), `sector_peers`, `theme` (weakest Glassdoor sub-rating, from the companies file). Every launch-set row has all four filled.
