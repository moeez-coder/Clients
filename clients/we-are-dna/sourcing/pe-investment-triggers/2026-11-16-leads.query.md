## Steps, tools and exact queries

1. **Blitz count check:** `pe_counts.py` calls `POST /v2/search/companies` with `last_funding_type: Private equity`, UK, 51+ employees, the five sectors, for 2025+ and 2026+.
   - Result: 0–17 companies per sector, so Blitz is not a usable source for UK PE deals.
   - Output: `2026-11-16-pe-counts.json`. Cost: 10 records.
2. **Exa Websets:** `exa_websets.py`, direct API, one webset per sector. Criteria: UK HQ, in the sector, PE deal announced since 2026-08-18, 50+ employees. Enrichments: investor, date, source, LinkedIn.
   - Result: only 12 items in total (the Exa index has few fresh UK deals).
   - Ids: `2026-11-16-exa-websets.json`. Raw items: `2026-11-16-exa-websets-raw.json`.
3. **Exa news search (main source):** `exa_news.py` calls `POST https://api.exa.ai/search`, category news, `startPublishedDate` 2026-08-18, 23 queries (4–5 per sector), 50 results each. Each result carries a structured summary: company, investor, deal type, UK HQ, sector.
   - Result: 1,119 articles, $2.17.
   - Raw: `2026-11-16-exa-news-raw.json`.
4. **First-pass filter and dedupe:** `build_pe_csvs.py`.
   - 489 articles kept. Drop reasons: 357 not UK, 136 not a PE deal, 53 no single company, 32 investor or REIT named, 26 investor not named, 26 debt.
   - That gives 273 distinct companies.
   - Outputs: `2026-11-16-pe-deal-news.csv` (all 1,119, with status) and `2026-11-16-companies.csv`.
5. **Qualification:** `qualify_exa.py`.
   - **Why Exa:** the GTM Sheets AI column with web access was tried first. Its search and scrape tools returned "insufficient credits / rate limit" for every row, so it was stopped after 10 rows.
   - **Method:** Exa `POST /contents` with a structured-summary check on each source article (deal target, PE equity deal, UK HQ, employees, deal date on or after 2026-05-18). Then Exa `POST /search` on linkedin.com for the company page. Cost: $2.18.
   - **Result:** 130 qualified, plus 7 strong webset items added and 2 duplicates dropped, for 132.
6. **Manual review:** `2026-11-16-manual-review.csv`.
   - Dropped 20: investors, out-of-sector companies, non-UK companies, duplicates and companies with no completed PE deal.
   - Re-sectored 8.
   - **112 qualified companies.** Output: `2026-11-16-companies-qualified.csv` (all 280 candidates, with qualified, reason, leads found and leads launch set).
7. **Leads:** `pull_leads.py` calls Blitz `POST /v2/search/waterfall-icp-keyword` per qualified company.
   - Tier 1: CEO, Chief Executive Officer, Managing Director, Managing Partner, Senior Partner, Founder, Group Managing Director.
   - Tier 2: Chief People Officer, People Director, HR Director, Group HR Director, Director of People, Human Resources Director, Chief Operating Officer, Chief HR Officer.
   - Excluded titles: assistants, interns, recruiters, Talent Acquisition, Sales, non-execs, advisors. Location GB, `max_results` 3.
   - **Blitz records used: 149.** The GTM Sheets `blitz_waterfall_icp` column was tried first and failed: it sends `countries` as a string, and Blitz requires an array.
   - Result: 149 leads at 67 companies. Raw: `2026-11-16-waterfall-raw.jsonl`. CSV: `2026-11-16-leads.csv`, `linkedin_url` first, deduped.
8. **Lead review:** 117 are `launch set`. 32 are marked `removed: <reason>`, kept in the CSV rather than deleted:
   - 27 HR, talent, finance or IT "Business Partners" picked up because Blitz matches "Senior Partner" as whole words
   - 2 non-executives
   - 2 off-persona
   - 1 at another company

   **Next pull:** add "Business Partner", "Talent Partner" and "People Partner" to the title excludes.
