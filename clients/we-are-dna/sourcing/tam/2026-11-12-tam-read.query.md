1. **Blitz API `POST /v2/search/companies`**, with `max_results: 1`, reading `total_results`. This gives the base company TAM per segment for two size bands: 51+ (the ICP form's floor) and 201+.
   - Filters: exact Blitz industry enum labels (`docs/blitz-api/company-search-industries.txt` on `origin/claude/stoic-hypatia-yxvmmu`), `employee_range`, and `hq.country_code ["GB"]`.
   - Script: `tam_counts.py`. Request bodies: `tam_counts.json` (`companies_51plus_body`, `companies_201plus_body`).
2. **Blitz API `POST /v2/jobs/search`**, with `max_results: 1`, reading `total_results`. This counts hook-role postings in the UK over the last 30 and 90 days, at UK-HQ companies with 51+ employees in the same industries, with `is_agency: false`.
   - Excluded titles: Intern, Internship, Work Experience, Volunteer, Recruitment Consultant, Recruiter.
   - Request bodies: `jobs_30d_body` and `jobs_90d_body`.
3. **Blitz API `POST /v2/jobs/search`, `max_results: 25`**, using the 30-day body. This is a sample of the 25 most recent postings per segment, used to judge concentration and noise.
   - Script: `tam_samples.py`. Output: `tam_samples.json`.
4. **Clay MCP `search-companies`**, using the `jobs.exists(...)` DSL for segment 4 (UK HQ, 201+, law/accounting, a matching posting in the last 30 days).
   - Clay returns 20 rows per page with no total, so this is a fit check, not a size.
   - Output: `2026-11-12-clay-sample-seg4.json` (taskId `mcp-task_0toe9fwySJxQNVcVXNC`).

## Findings that shape the doc

- **The open-role signal is close to universal in these sectors.** For a recruiter, a posting means one placeable role. For an employer-brand buyer, the signal is a *sustained hiring load*: many postings, the same role reposted, or volume frontline roles.
  - At sourcing, rank companies by matched postings over 90 days (`tam-by-jobs` `matched_jobs`) and work from the top down.
  - The `{open_role}` should be the company's most-posted hook role, not simply its latest.
- **The pools are large, so the inverse rule applies:**
  - Segments 1, 2, 3 and 5 get **2 contacts per company**: tier 1 is the TA, Resourcing or Employer Brand lead; tier 2 is the People Director, HRD or CPO.
  - Segment 4 is a small, defined pool of about 200 firms, so it gets **3 per company**: Head of Graduate/Early Talent Recruitment or Head of Resourcing, HR Director, and CPO.
- **Possible existing clients appear in the pool.** Medivet (seg 1) and Dunbia (seg 3) are named in the client's own ICP form under "client matching", alongside Vets for Pets, DHU/Medvivo, Portman Dentex, Sussex Police, Crowe, PKF Francis Clark, McCarthy & Stone, Ian Williams, Vistry, MAN Truck & Bus, Aggreko, Bodyset, Charterhouse and Clinical Partners.
  - It's unclear whether these are clients or look-alike targets.
  - **Treat them as exclusions until the client confirms.** The DNC file uploaded with the ICP form is not loaded in tracking_clients (`dncList` = 0).
- **Public bodies are noise for MPC:** NHS trusts and health boards (seg 1), CPS (seg 4) and ICAEW (seg 4).
  - The ICP form does list Sussex Police and DHU/111, so the public sector isn't ruled out.
  - Default: exclude NHS trusts and government bodies from the MPC lists, because they buy through procurement frameworks. Raise this with the client in the doc.
- **Blitz counts are indicative** (CLAUDE.md accuracy warning). Check the companies by eye at pull time.
